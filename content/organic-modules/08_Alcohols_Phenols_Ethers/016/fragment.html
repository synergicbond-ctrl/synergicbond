29.6 Mixed foundation questions

Practice 58 L1 MIX

Which of these is soluble in aqueous NaOH: ethanol, phenol, anisole, cyclohexanol?

Show answer
  1. Phenol only (it forms sodium phenoxide).
Practice 59 L1 MIX

Give a simple chemical test to distinguish phenol from ethanol.

Show answer
  1. Neutral FeCl3: violet with phenol, no colour with ethanol (also: bromine water gives a white precipitate with phenol).
Practice 60 L1 MIX

Give a simple test to distinguish diethyl ether from ethanol.

Show answer
  1. Sodium metal: ethanol evolves H2; ether does not. (Also PCl5: HCl fumes with ethanol.)
Practice 61 L1 MIX

Which reagent distinguishes propan-1-ol from propan-2-ol?

Show answer
  1. Iodoform test (I2/NaOH): positive for propan-2-ol only. (Also Lucas test: 2° turbid in minutes, 1° not.)
Practice 62 L1 MIX

Name the compounds A and B: ethanol —(Cu, 573 K)→ A —(Tollens)→ B.

Show answer
  1. A = ethanal; B = ethanoate (acetate) ion/ethanoic acid with a silver mirror.
Practice 63 L1 MIX

What is the difference between the products of ethanol heated with conc. H2SO4 at 413 K and at 443 K?

Show answer
  1. At 413 K (excess alcohol): diethyl ether; at 443 K (excess acid): ethene.
Practice 64 L1 MIX

Convert phenol to anisole in one step.

Show answer
  1. NaOH, then CH3I (or (CH3)2SO4/NaOH): Williamson synthesis.
Practice 65 L1 MIX

Convert anisole back to phenol in one step.

Show answer
  1. Heat with HI (or HBr, or use BBr3): gives phenol + CH3I.
Practice 66 L1 MIX

Convert propene to propan-2-ol.

Show answer
  1. H3O+ (dilute H2SO4, water): Markovnikov hydration.
Practice 67 L1 MIX

Convert benzene to phenol (two methods).

Show answer
  1. (i) Cumene process; (ii) benzene → benzenesulphonic acid → fusion with NaOH → phenol; or benzene → chlorobenzene → NaOH, 623 K, 300 atm (Dow).
