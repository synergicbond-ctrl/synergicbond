4.6 Amines

  • Primary amine: replace the e of the alkane by -amine: methanamine, propan-2-amine. Locant for the NH₂ is lowest.
  • Secondary and tertiary amines: the longest (or most senior) chain is the parent amine; the other groups are N-substituents (N-methyl, N,N-diethyl). One N-locant per group.
  • Aromatic amine: aniline (retained), with ring locants from the NH₂ carbon. N-substituents on aniline: N-methylaniline, N,N-dimethylaniline.
  • Functional-class names (alkyl + amine) are converted directly: methylamine = methanamine, dimethylamine = N-methylmethanamine, trimethylamine = N,N-dimethylmethanamine.
