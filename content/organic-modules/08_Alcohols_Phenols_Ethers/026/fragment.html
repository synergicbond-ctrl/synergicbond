31.3 Matrix-match questions

Each entry of Column I may match one or more entries of Column II.

Practice 258 L3 MAT ALC

Match the reaction of butan-2-ol (Column I) with the result (Column II). Column I: (A) CrO3/H2SO4; (B) conc. H2SO4, heat; (C) I2/NaOH, warm; (D) sodium metal. Column II: (P) but-2-ene; (Q) butan-2-one; (R) yellow precipitate; (S) hydrogen gas.

Show answer
  1. (A) oxidises the secondary alcohol to the ketone: Q. (B) dehydration (Zaitsev): P. (C) iodoform: R. (D) 2C4H9OH + 2Na → 2C4H9ONa + H2: S.
  2. A–Q; B–P; C–R; D–S.
Practice 259 L3 MAT PHE

Match the reaction (Column I) with the name (Column II). Column I: (A) phenol + CHCl3/NaOH; (B) sodium phenoxide + CO2 (400 K, 5 atm); (C) phenyl ethanoate + AlCl3; (D) allyl phenyl ether, 473 K. Column II: (P) Fries rearrangement; (Q) Claisen rearrangement; (R) Kolbe–Schmitt reaction; (S) Reimer–Tiemann reaction.

Show answer
  1. A–S; B–R; C–P; D–Q.
Practice 260 L3 MAT EPX

Match the reagent used with propylene oxide (Column I) with the product (Column II). Column I: (A) CH3ONa/CH3OH; (B) CH3OH/H+; (C) LiAlH4; (D) CH3MgBr, then H3O+. Column II: (P) propan-2-ol; (Q) butan-2-ol; (R) 1-methoxypropan-2-ol; (S) 2-methoxypropan-1-ol.

Show answer
  1. (A) attack at CH2: R. (B) attack at CH: S. (C) hydride at CH2: P. (D) methyl at CH2: CH3CH(OH)CH2CH3: Q.
  2. A–R; B–S; C–P; D–Q.
Practice 261 L3 MAT PHE

Match the compound (Column I) with its approximate pKa (Column II). Column I: (A) picric acid; (B) p-nitrophenol; (C) phenol; (D) ethanol. Column II: (P) below 1; (Q) between 6 and 8; (R) about 10; (S) about 16.

Show answer
  1. A–P; B–Q; C–R; D–S.
Practice 262 L3 MAT ETH

Match the ether with the products of HI cleavage (Column II). Column I: (A) CH3OC(CH3)3 (cold); (B) C6H5OCH3; (C) CH3OCH(CH3)2 (1 mol HI, heat); (D) C6H5CH2OC2H5. Column II: (P) tert-butyl iodide + methanol; (Q) phenol + methyl iodide; (R) methyl iodide + propan-2-ol; (S) benzyl iodide + ethanol.

Show answer
  1. A–P; B–Q; C–R; D–S.
Practice 263 L3 MAT MIX

Match the compound with a characteristic test or observation. Column I: (A) glycerol; (B) phenol; (C) ethanol; (D) propanone. Column II: (P) acrolein smell on heating with KHSO4; (Q) violet colour with neutral FeCl3; (R) positive iodoform test; (S) oxidised by PCC to an aldehyde.

Show answer
  1. (A) P. (B) Q. (C) R and S (ethanol is iodoform positive and PCC gives ethanal). (D) R.
  2. A–P; B–Q; C–R, S; D–R.
Practice 264 L3 MAT ALC

Match the diol (Column I) with the number of moles of HIO4 consumed per mole (Column II). Column I: (A) ethane-1,2-diol; (B) glycerol; (C) erythritol; (D) propane-1,3-diol. Column II: (P) 0; (Q) 1; (R) 2; (S) 3.
