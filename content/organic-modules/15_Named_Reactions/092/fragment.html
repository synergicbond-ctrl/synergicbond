Part I. Practice problems (330, three levels, full solutions)

How to use: attempt each problem on paper, then open the solution. Level 1 (Foundation, problems 1–110) checks recognition of reactions, reagents and products. Level 2 (Main, 111–230) needs mechanisms, regiochemistry, stereochemistry, multi-step routes and short numericals. Level 3 (Advanced, 231–330) has multiple-correct, integer, matrix-match, paragraph and assertion–reason problems as in JEE Advanced. Every structure used in the solutions was machine-checked. Tag in square brackets shows the topic.
