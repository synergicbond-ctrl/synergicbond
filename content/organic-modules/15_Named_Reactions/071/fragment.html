65. Benzidine rearrangement (and the Bamberger, Orton, Hofmann–Martius family)

Benzidine: hydrazobenzene (N,N′-diphenylhydrazine) + dilute mineral acid → benzidine (4,4′-diaminobiphenyl) as the major product (≈ 70 %) with diphenyline (2,4′-diaminobiphenyl) (≈ 30 %). The diprotonated hydrazobenzene undergoes an intramolecular [5,5]-sigmatropic shift (no crossover with mixed substrates, which proves the intramolecular path); N–N breaks as the C-4–C-4′ bond forms. Kinetics are second order in H+.

Trap If the para position of one ring is blocked (4-substituted hydrazobenzene), the 4,4′-bond cannot form and semidines (N-aryl-o-phenylenediamines or N-aryl-p-phenylenediamines) result instead. Benzidine and diphenyline are both carcinogenic; benzidine is the precursor of the Congo-red type azo dyes.
