4. Acidity and basicity of alcohols

Alcohols are amphoteric: the O–H bond can ionise (very weak acid), and the oxygen lone pair can accept a proton (very weak base). Both properties are essential: acidity explains reaction with metals and bases, basicity explains why acid catalyses substitution, dehydration and esterification (it converts the poor leaving group HO− into the good leaving group H2O).
