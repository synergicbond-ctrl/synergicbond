Block C. Carbon count and chain length

Practice 61 Carbons

Which of these reactions shorten the carbon chain by one carbon: Hofmann, Hunsdiecker, Arndt–Eistert, Curtius, Kiliani–Fischer, Schmidt (on RCOOH)?

Show answer
  1. Hofmann, Hunsdiecker, Curtius, Schmidt shorten by one carbon. Arndt–Eistert and Kiliani–Fischer add one.
Practice 62 Carbons

CH3CH2Br → (KCN) A → (H3O+, Δ) B. How many carbons in B?

Show answer
  1. A = CH3CH2CN, B = CH3CH2COOH: 3 carbons (CN− adds one carbon).
Practice 63 Carbons

Silver butanoate is treated with Br2 in CCl4. Name the product and its carbon count.

Show answer
  1. Hunsdiecker: 1-bromopropane, 3 carbons (CO2 is lost).
Practice 64 Carbons

Benzoic acid is converted to phenylethanoic acid by the Arndt–Eistert sequence. Count the carbons in the starting acid and product.

Show answer
  1. PhCOOH has 7 C; PhCH2COOH has 8 C: one carbon is inserted by CH2N2/Wolff rearrangement.
Practice 65 Carbons

Sodium butanoate is electrolysed. How many carbons does the hydrocarbon product have?

Show answer
  1. Kolbe gives R–R with R = propyl: hexane, 6 carbons.
Practice 66 Carbons

1-Bromopropane + Na/dry ether: carbons in product?

Show answer
  1. Wurtz: hexane, 6 C (3 + 3).
Practice 67 Carbons

(CH3CH2)2CuLi + CH3CH2CH2CH2Br: how many carbons in the product?

Show answer
  1. Only one ethyl is transferred: C2 + C4 = hexane, 6 C.
Practice 68 Carbons

The aldol addition product of propanal: how many carbons?

Show answer
  1. 3-Hydroxy-2-methylpentanal: 6 C (2 × 3).
Practice 69 Carbons

Perkin reaction of benzaldehyde with ethanoic anhydride: how many carbons in the acid formed?

Show answer
  1. Cinnamic acid, PhCH=CHCOOH: 9 C (7 + 2).
Practice 70 Carbons

Diethyl malonate is alkylated twice with CH3I, hydrolysed and decarboxylated. Name the acid and count its carbons.

Show answer
  1. (CH3)2CHCOOH, 2-methylpropanoic acid, 4 C.
Practice 71 Carbons

Ethyl acetoacetate + n-C3H7Br, then ketonic hydrolysis: product and carbons?

Show answer
  1. CH3COCH2CH2CH2CH3, hexan-2-one, 6 C.
Practice 72 Carbons

CH3MgI + CO2: carbon count of the product relative to the Grignard's R?

Show answer
  1. +1 carbon: CH3COOH (2 C from CH3).
Practice 73 Carbons

Benzaldehyde → (ClCH2COOEt, NaOEt) → hydrolysis/decarboxylation. Product and carbons?

Show answer
  1. Darzens: PhCH2CHO, 8 C (+1).
Practice 74 Carbons

Compare Stephen (CH3CH2CN) and Rosenmund (CH3CH2COCl): carbons of the aldehyde in each.

Show answer
  1. Both give CH3CH2CHO (propanal, 3 C).
Practice 75 Carbons

Cyclohexanone + methyl vinyl ketone, base (Robinson annulation). Molecular formula of the bicyclic enone?

Show answer
  1. C6H10O + C4H6O − H2O = C10H14O (10 carbons).
