51. Problem bank, Level 3 (integer type and hard counting)

Each answer below is a single integer unless stated. Counts of isomers in this section were checked by enumeration with RDKit.

Practice 262 L3 Integer

Isomer count

How many alkenes (structural isomers, ignoring stereoisomerism) have the formula C6H12? How many including stereoisomers (E/Z and enantiomers)?

Show answer

13 structural alkenes. Stereoisomers add 5: E/Z for hex-2-ene, hex-3-ene, 3-methylpent-2-ene and 4-methylpent-2-ene (+4) and the enantiomer of chiral 3-methylpent-1-ene (+1): 18 in all (confirmed by enumeration).

Show answer

13 alkenes + 12 cycloalkanes = 25 structural. Including stereoisomers: 18 + 20 = 38.

Practice 275 L3 Integer

Wurtz mixture

A mixture of CH3Cl, C2H5Cl, n-C3H7Cl and isopropyl chloride is treated with Na/dry ether. How many different alkanes (ignoring any elimination or disproportionation) can form by coupling?

Show answer

Pairs (10 combinations of 4 halides including self-coupling): CH3–CH3 (ethane); CH3–Et (propane); CH3–nPr (butane); CH3–iPr (isobutane); Et–Et (butane); Et–nPr (pentane); Et–iPr (isopentane); nPr–nPr (hexane); nPr–iPr (2-methylhexane); iPr–iPr (2,3-dimethylbutane). Practice 279 L3 Integer

Stereoisomers of a symmetrical tetrabromide

How many stereoisomers are possible for CH3CHBr–CHBr–CHBr–CHBrCH3 (the tetrabromide from hexa-2,4-diene)?

Show answer

Four stereocentres in a chain with identical ends (A–B–B–A pattern). For n = 4 centres: 2n−1 + 2(n/2)−1 = 8 + 2 = 10 stereoisomers (some meso).

Practice 281 L3 Integer

Products of E2

How many alkenes (structural, then with stereoisomers) can be formed by E2 from 3-bromo-2-methylhexane? Consider every β-hydrogen.

Show answer

Br is on C-3, so the β carbons are C-2 and C-4. Loss of H from C-2 gives 2-methylhex-2-ene (one alkene). Loss of H from C-4 gives 2-methylhex-3-ene (E and Z). That is 2 structural alkenes and 3 including stereoisomers.

Practice 284 L3 Integer

(CH3)3N+–CH(CH3)CH2CH2CH3 OH− is heated. How many different alkenes are formed and which is major?

Practice 290 L3 Integer

Sigma and pi

How many σ and π bonds does but-1-en-3-yne (CH2=CH–C≡CH) have?

Show answer

σ: 3 C–C + 4 C–H = 7 (C=C contributes 1 σ, ≡ contributes 1 σ). π: 1 + 2 = 3. So 7 σ and 3 π.
