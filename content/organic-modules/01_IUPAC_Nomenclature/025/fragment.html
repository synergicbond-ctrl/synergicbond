Exam Trap Methylene is the divalent –CH₂– group joining two other atoms (as in dichloromethane, methylene chloride); methylidene is =CH₂ attached by a double bond to a single atom. In 3-methylidenehexane the =CH₂ group is a substituent, so the parent chain is saturated.
