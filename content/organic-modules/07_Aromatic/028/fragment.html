Heating allyl phenyl ether at 470 K gives product A; A treated with Br2 (excess, CCl4) gives B. Identify A and B.

Show answer
  1. A = 2-allylphenol (Claisen). B: Br2 adds to the side-chain C=C quickly in CCl4 (no catalyst), giving 2-(2,3-dibromopropyl)phenol; with aqueous Br2 the phenol ring would also be brominated.

Practice 306 L2 · extra

Crotyl phenyl ether is heated. Give the product and state how it differs from the ether's allyl group connectivity.

Show answer
  1. The product is 2-(1-methylprop-2-enyl)phenol (2-(but-3-en-2-yl)phenol): the ring is attached to the carbon that was the CH(CH3) end of the crotyl group (C-3), and the new double bond is terminal. The allyl unit is inverted.

How many dichloronitrobenzene isomers (C6H3Cl2NO2) exist?

Practice 308 L2 · extra

Aspirin (pKa 3.5) passes from the stomach (pH ≈ 2) to the intestine (pH ≈ 8). What is the dominant form in each and why?

Show answer
  1. Stomach: the neutral carboxylic acid (pH below pKa, mostly undissociated). Intestine: the carboxylate anion (pH ≫ pKa: 99.99 % ionised, since the ratio [A−]/[HA] = 108−3.5 ≈ 3 × 104).
Practice 309 L2 · extra

Give a four-step synthesis of p-chlorophenol from p-chloronitrobenzene.

Show answer
  1. (1) Fe/HCl (or Sn/HCl): p-chloroaniline. (2) NaOH to liberate the free amine. (3) NaNO2, H2SO4, 273 K: the diazonium hydrogen sulphate. (4) H2O, warm (heat): p-chlorophenol (the strongly acidic medium prevents coupling).

Practice 310 L2 · extra

Why is the para-Claisen rearrangement observed only when both ortho positions are blocked?

Show answer
  1. The first [3,3] shift always delivers the allyl group to the ortho carbon. If that carbon has an H, the dienone enolises and the sequence stops. If it carries a substituent (no H), enolisation is impossible and a second [3,3] (Cope) shift moves the allyl group to the para carbon, where the dienone can enolise.
Practice 311 L2 · extra

Predict the product of nitrosation of N,N-dimethylaniline (NaNO2, HCl, 273 K) and of phenol under the same conditions. What type of electrophile is involved?

Show answer
  1. N,N-Dimethylaniline gives p-nitroso-N,N-dimethylaniline (green crystals); phenol gives p-nitrosophenol (with some ortho). The electrophile is the nitrosonium ion NO+, a weak electrophile that reacts only with strongly activated rings.
Practice 312 L2 · extra

3-Bromophenoxide (with excess NaNH2 in liquid NH3) reacts through benzyne. Predict the aminophenol formed and explain the regiochemistry.

Show answer
  1. The product is 3-aminophenol (m-aminophenol). Amide ion adds to the benzyne carbon that places the new carbanion as far as possible from the negative charge of O− (i.e. meta to O−); addition next to O− would put two negative charges close together.
