Key idea The golden rule of protecting groups: if a reagent would attack two sites and you want only one, mask the other site with a group that is stable to that reagent but removable afterwards. Ketone → acetal before LiAlH4 or Grignard on an ester; amine → acetamide before nitration; carboxylic acid → ester before Grignard on a ketone; alcohol → THP ether before a Grignard formation from a halo-alcohol.
