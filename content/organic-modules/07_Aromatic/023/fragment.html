Part E. Practice problems (with full solutions)

The problems are graded in three levels. Level 1 (Main) checks each rule once, in single-correct or short-answer form. Level 2 (Advanced) has multi-correct questions, multi-step products, mechanisms and comparison problems. Level 3 (Advanced integer, matrix match, paragraph) mixes several ideas, in the style of the hardest paper questions. Every problem has a worked solution; open the solution only after an honest attempt. A suggested habit: before looking at the solution, write down which of the five questions in section 19.3 applies.
