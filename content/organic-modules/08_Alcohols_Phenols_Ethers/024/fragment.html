Practice 230 L3 MCQ ALC

A secondary alcohol C5H12O gives positive iodoform and rearranges on dehydration. It may be: (A) pentan-2-ol (B) 3-methylbutan-2-ol (C) pentan-3-ol (D) 2-methylbutan-2-ol

Which of the following explain why o-nitrophenol is more volatile than p-nitrophenol? (A) intramolecular H-bond in o (B) intermolecular H-bonding in p (C) higher molar mass of p (D) o-nitrophenol is more polar

Show answer
  1. (A) and (B). Both isomers have the same molar mass; the o isomer is less polar overall because the dipoles partly cancel and its OH is internally bonded.
