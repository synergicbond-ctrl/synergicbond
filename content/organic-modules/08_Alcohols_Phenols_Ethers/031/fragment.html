4.2 Basicity (the oxygen lone pair)

ROH + H+ ⇌ ROH2+. The pKa of protonated alcohols is about −2 to −3, so strong acids (HCl, HBr, HI, H2SO4) protonate alcohols substantially. Protonation turns the bad leaving group HO− (conjugate acid water, pKa 15.7) into the good leaving group H2O (conjugate acid H3O+, pKa −1.7), which departs as a neutral molecule.

Key idea A leaving group is only as good as it is stable as an anion or neutral molecule. Rule of thumb: you can substitute an alcohol if you can turn –OH into –OH2+, –OTs, –OCOR′ (with acid), –OPCl2, –OSOCl, or –OSO3H. Every substitution and elimination of alcohols in this chapter is a way of making OH into a good leaving group.
