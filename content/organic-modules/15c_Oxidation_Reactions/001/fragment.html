Practice 29 Numerical

How many moles of HIO₄ are consumed by 1 mol of glycerol, HOCH₂–CH(OH)–CH₂OH?

Show answer

Two C–C bonds, each flanked by OH groups, are cleaved: 2 mol.

Answer: 2

Practice 30 Numerical

How many moles of O₃ are needed to cleave completely 1 mol of buta-1,3-diene?

Show answer

Each C=C reacts with one O₃: 2 mol.

Answer: 2

Practice 31 Numerical

How many different organic products form (counting each once) when pent-2-ene is ozonised with reductive work-up?

Practice 32 Numerical
