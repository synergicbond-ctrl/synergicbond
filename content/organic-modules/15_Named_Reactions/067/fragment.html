61. Benzilic acid rearrangement

Reagents: a 1,2-diketone with no α-hydrogen (benzil, PhCOCOPh; phenanthrenequinone) + KOH (or NaOH) in water/dioxane, heat, then H3O+. Product: an α-hydroxy acid with two identical or different R groups on the carbinol carbon (benzilic acid, Ph2C(OH)COOH). Mechanism: (1) OH− adds to one C=O giving a tetrahedral alkoxide; (2) one aryl group migrates from the carbinol carbon to the neighbouring C=O carbon (a 1,2-shift; the C=O is the electron-deficient acceptor), (3) the carboxylic acid so formed is deprotonated (irreversible), (4) acid work-up gives the α-hydroxy acid.

1. KOH, H₂O, Δ 2. H₃O⁺⟶
Benzil → benzilic acid (2-hydroxy-2,2-diphenylethanoic acid)
1. KOH 2. H₃O⁺⟶
Phenanthrenequinone → 9-hydroxyfluorene-9-carboxylic acid (ring contraction 6 → 5)
Trap Compare with the other 1,2-shift under base: Cannizzaro (hydride transfer between two molecules), benzilic acid (aryl shift within one molecule), Favorskii (α-halo ketone, ring contraction). An aliphatic 1,2-diketone with α-H (butane-2,3-dione) undergoes aldol instead of benzilic acid rearrangement.
