41. Lucas test and Victor Meyer test (classifying alcohols)

Lucas: anhydrous ZnCl2 dissolved in conc. HCl converts an alcohol into the alkyl chloride, which is insoluble in the aqueous reagent (turbidity or an oily layer). The rate follows SN1 carbocation stability: tertiary and benzylic/allylic alcohols turbid immediately; secondary in about 5 min (at 25 °C); primary no turbidity at room temperature (needs heating). Only works for alcohols of up to ~C6 that dissolve in the reagent. Victor Meyer: RCH2OH → RCH2I (red P/I2) → RCH2NO2 (AgNO2) → HNO2/then NaOH: primary gives nitrolic acid (blood-red colour with NaOH); secondary gives pseudonitrole (blue with NaOH); tertiary gives no reaction with HNO2 (colourless) because the nitro compound has no α-H.
