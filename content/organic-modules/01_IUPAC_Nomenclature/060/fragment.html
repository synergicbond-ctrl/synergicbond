Exam Trap The prefixes ortho, meta and para apply only to two substituents; for three or more, use numbers. Do not mix parents within one name: p-nitrotoluene, 4-nitrotoluene and 1-methyl-4-nitrobenzene are the same compound, but "1-methyl-4-nitrotoluene" is meaningless.
