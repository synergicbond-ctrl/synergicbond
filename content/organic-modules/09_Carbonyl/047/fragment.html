40. Industrially and biologically important carbonyl compounds

Methanal (formaldehyde). Gas, 37–40 % solution formalin. Reacts with phenol in the presence of acid or base to give the thermosetting resin Bakelite (phenol–formaldehyde), and with urea or melamine to give urea–formaldehyde and melamine resins. Forms hexamine with NH3. Gives Cannizzaro, Tollens', Fehling's, Schiff's; not iodoform; is the most reactive carbonyl towards nucleophiles. Ethanal (acetaldehyde). Trimerises to paraldehyde (H+), tetramerises to metaldehyde; chlorination gives chloral (trichloroethanal), whose hydrate is a hypnotic ("knock-out drops"). Chloral with chlorobenzene (conc. H2SO4) gives DDT.

Cl₂⟶ 2 PhCl, conc. H₂SO₄⟶
Ethanal → chloral → DDT (1,1,1-trichloro-2,2-bis(4-chlorophenyl)ethane)

Propanone (acetone). Industrial solvent from the cumene process. With phenol (H+) it gives bisphenol A, the monomer of polycarbonates and epoxy resins. With CHCl3/KOH it gives chloretone (1,1,1-trichloro-2-methylpropan-2-ol), a hypnotic and preservative.

+ + H⁺, −H₂O⟶
Acetone + 2 phenol → bisphenol A (4,4′-isopropylidenediphenol)

Benzaldehyde. Bitter almond smell; autoxidises to benzoic acid in air; Cannizzaro, benzoin, Perkin, Knoevenagel, Claisen–Schmidt; with ammonia it gives hydrobenzamide (PhCH=N)2CHPh (3 PhCHO + 2 NH3 → hydrobenzamide + 3 H2O) not an aldimine; does not reduce Fehling's. Acetophenone, benzophenone. Acetophenone: haloform positive, forms oximes, Beckmann gives acetanilide; Clemmensen gives ethylbenzene; benzophenone with sodium metal gives the deep-blue ketyl radical anion, used as an indicator of dry, oxygen-free ethers and THF.
