8.4 R/S for stereocentres

Definition. A stereocentre is an atom with four different groups whose spatial arrangement matters; its configuration is described as R or S.

  • Assign priorities 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 to the four groups at the stereocentre.
  • Orient the molecule with the lowest priority (4) pointing away from the viewer.
  • If 1 → 2 → 3 runs clockwise, the centre is R (Latin rectus); if anticlockwise, S (sinister).
  • Swapping any two groups inverts the descriptor. If the lowest group points toward you, read the sense and reverse it.
  • In a Fischer projection, horizontal bonds point toward the viewer. If the lowest priority is on a horizontal bond, the apparent direction is the opposite of the true one: reverse it. If it is on a vertical bond, read directly.

Compare & Distinguish L and D describe the arrangement in a Fischer projection relative to glyceraldehyde; R and S describe CIP priorities. L-Lactic acid and L-alanine are (S), D-glyceraldehyde is (R).
Exception & Its Reason L-Cysteine is (R), not (S), although it has the same spatial arrangement as L-alanine. The side chain –CH₂SH outranks –COOH (S outranks O at the first sphere), which changes the order of priorities and therefore the letter.
