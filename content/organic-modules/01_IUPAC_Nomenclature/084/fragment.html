HYF When a question gives both an older-style and a 2013-style name among the options and the stem says "IUPAC name" without "preferred", pick the one that applies the chain-contains-the-multiple-bond convention (older). A well-set JEE question avoids conflicts, so if both options look right the stereodescriptor or the substituent order will be the real difference.
