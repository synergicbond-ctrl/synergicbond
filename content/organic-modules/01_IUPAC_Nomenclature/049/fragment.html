6.1 Monocyclic rings with a principal group

Definition. A monocyclic parent is named cyclo + alkane. The carbon bearing the principal group is C-1 (the locant is not written for a single group: cyclohexanol, cyclohexanone).

  • If the ring also has a double bond, number so that, in order: (a) the principal group has locant 1; (b) the double bond gets the lowest possible locant (2 when it is next to C-1, otherwise 3 or more): cyclohex-2-en-1-ol; (c) then the prefixes.
  • A double bond not next to C-1 takes the number of its first carbon in the chosen direction: cyclohex-3-en-1-ol.
Validity Check Test any ring name by redrawing it: in cyclohex-2-en-1-ol the OH carbon is adjacent to the alkene carbon C-2; in cyclohex-3-en-1-ol it is separated from the alkene by one CH₂.
