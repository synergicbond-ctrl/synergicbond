32. The Robinson annulation

A ring-forming sequence: Michael addition of a ketone enolate to methyl vinyl ketone (or another enone), then an intramolecular aldol condensation of the resulting 1,5-diketone, giving a new six-membered ring with a conjugated enone (cyclohexenone).

+ NaOEt or KOH⟶ intramolecular aldol, −H₂O⟶
Cyclohexanone + MVK → 2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexanone → 4,4a,5,6,7,8-hexahydronaphthalen-2(3H)-one (an octalone)
  • Michael step: the cyclohexanone enolate adds through C-2 to the β-carbon of MVK.
  • Aldol step: the methyl ketone's CH3 (as an enolate) attacks the ring C=O, forming a six-membered ring: C-2 of ring, the two CH2, the C=O, the CH3-derived carbon, and the ring C=O carbon.
  • Dehydration gives the α,β-unsaturated ketone (enone).
+ base; then proline or base⟶
2-Methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione + MVK → Wieland–Miescher ketone (steroid and terpene synthesis)
Key idea Robinson annulation = Michael + intramolecular aldol condensation. Test for the ring: count six atoms formed: the ketone α-carbon, the ketone C=O carbon, the MVK three carbons (β, α, C=O), and the MVK methyl: the ring closes by attack of the MVK methyl carbon on the original ketone C=O.
Example 35 Adv

Robinson annulation: product

Predict the product of base-catalysed reaction of (a) cyclohexanone with MVK; (b) 2-methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione with MVK. Name the reactions in each sequence.

Show solution
  1. (a) Michael addition (C-2 of the enolate to the β-carbon of MVK) gives 2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexanone. Intramolecular aldol condensation (CH3 of the side-chain ketone attacks the ring C=O, then −H2O) gives the octalone, 4,4a,5,6,7,8-hexahydronaphthalen-2(3H)-one.
  2. (b) The same sequence with the doubly activated C-2 of the dione (pKa about 10) gives, after Michael and aldol steps, the Wieland–Miescher ketone, with a quaternary methyl at the ring junction.
