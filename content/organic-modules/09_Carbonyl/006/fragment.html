4. Summary sheet for Part A

  • sp2 planar C=O; δ+ on C; attack at about 107°; C turns sp3.
  • Reactivity rests on sterics + electronics; unsymmetrical achiral carbonyl + achiral Nu → racemate.
  • Suffix: -al (C-1 always), -one (lowest locant); ring CHO = -carbaldehyde; lower-priority C=O = oxo/formyl.
  • Acid > ester > acid chloride > amide > nitrile > aldehyde > ketone > alcohol.
  • b.p.: alkane < ether < carbonyl < alcohol < acid. Lower carbonyls dissolve in water by accepting H-bonds.
  • C=O IR 1700–1740 cm-1; CHO 1H δ 9–10; conjugation lowers ν(C=O).
