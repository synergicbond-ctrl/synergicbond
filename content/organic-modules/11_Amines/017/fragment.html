17. Oxidation of amines

  • 1° aromatic amines. Oxidation depends on the oxidant: acidic K2Cr2O7 (or MnO2/H2SO4) → p-benzoquinone; Caro's acid (H2SO5) → nitrosobenzene; peroxytrifluoroacetic acid or H2O2/CF3COOH → nitrobenzene; neutral/weakly acidic oxidants (e.g. K2Cr2O7/H+ mildly, FeCl3) → aniline black (a polymer); bleaching powder gives a violet colour with aniline (a classical test). Aniline turns brown in air for the same reason.
  • 1° aliphatic amines with peracids → oximes/nitro compounds.
  • 3° amines + H2O2 or peracid → amine N-oxides R3N+–O−. These are polar, water soluble, and undergo Cope elimination.
