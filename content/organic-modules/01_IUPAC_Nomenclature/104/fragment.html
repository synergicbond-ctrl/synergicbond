Part 12 · Graded exercises

Three levels. Level 1 (Main) — one rule at a time, 70 problems. Level 2 (Advanced) — polyfunctional compounds, chain-selection traps, rings, stereodescriptors, single-correct and multiple-correct, 71 problems. Level 3 (Integer, matrix, paragraph, multi-correct) — numerical and encoded answers, matching, comprehension passages, 68 problems. Every name in every solution has been parsed back into a structure with RDKit and its numbering checked by program (see the verification notes at the end of the chapter).
