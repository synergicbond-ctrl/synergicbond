AC-039 [Application] · Akshay topic Q39 · Status: Verified (Ref. [2], p. 812: cyclopentadienyl anion aromatic)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, master-sheet page 19] Cyclopentadiene pKₐ ≈ 15 vs cyclopentane pKₐ > 50 because: (a) cyclopentadiene is particularly unstable (b) cyclopentane has no lone pairs (c) cyclopentadiene is a 4π anti-aromatic compound (d) cyclopentadiene is a 4π non-aromatic compound and after deprotonation it is aromatic

Full solution
  1. Cyclopentadiene is a 4π non-aromatic diene (its CH₂ is sp³).
  2. Removing H⁺ from CH₂ gives the cyclopentadienyl anion: 6π, planar, aromatic. That drives the low pKₐ.
  3. Option (d).

Answer: (d)

AC-040 [Application] · Akshay topic Q40 · Status: Verified (Ref. [2], Table 22-1 text)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 19] α-H of esters pKₐ ≈ 25 vs ketones ≈ 20 because: (a) no resonance stabilisation of ester enolates (b) inductive effect of the ester oxygen destabilises the enolate (c) the electron-donating alkoxy group in esters destabilises the enolate (d) the electron-donating alkoxy group stabilises the enolate

Full solution
  1. In an ester, the OR lone pair donates into the C=O, making the C=O a weaker electron acceptor.
  2. The enolate is therefore less stabilised than a ketone enolate.
  3. Option (c). (Ref. [2], Table 22-1 hint: ketone carbonyl stabilises the enolate better than ester carbonyl because of the donating alkoxy group.)

Answer: (c)

AC-042 [Application] · Akshay topic Q42 · Status: Verified (standard substituent effects on benzyl cations)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 19] Order of carbocation stability for (i) p-CH₃O-benzyl (ii) benzyl (iii) p-CH₃-benzyl (iv) ethyl. Options: (a) i<ii<iii<iv (b) iv<iii<ii<i (c) iv<ii<iii<i (d) ii<iv<iii<i

Full solution
  1. Ethyl cation (iv) has only hyperconjugation: least stable.
  2. Benzyl (ii) is resonance-stabilised.
  3. p-Methyl benzyl (iii) adds a donor (+I, hyperconjugation): more stable than benzyl.
  4. p-Methoxy benzyl (i) adds a +M donor: most stable.
  5. Increasing stability: iv < ii < iii < i = option (c).

Answer: (c)

AC-044 [Application] · Akshay topic Q44 · Status: Verified (direct α-H count; structure reading from image)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 19] Most stable carbonium ion among (a) (CH₃)₃C⁺ (b) (C₂H₅)₃C⁺ (c) CH₃–CH⁺–CH₃ type (secondary) (d) C₂H₅–CH⁺ type (secondary)

Full solution
  1. (a) and (b) are tertiary; (c) and (d) are secondary.
  2. α-H: (a) 9 H; (b) 6 H (three ethyl groups, each CH₂ contributes 2).
  3. Tertiary with more α-H: (a) is most stable.

Answer: (a)
