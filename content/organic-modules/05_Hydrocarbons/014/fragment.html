13. Decalin and fused rings

Decalin (bicyclo[4.4.0]decane) has two fused six-membered rings, with two bridgehead carbons. Cis-decalin (bridgehead H's on the same side) is flexible and can ring-flip; trans-decalin (bridgehead H's opposite) is rigid and cannot flip.

Decalin: trans fusion (bridgehead H's anti) and cis fusion
  • trans-Decalin is more stable than cis by about 11 kJ mol-1 (cis has three extra gauche butane interactions).
  • Decalin has 2 diastereomers (cis and trans), neither being optically active; steroids use the same trans/cis fused cyclohexane concept (A/B ring fusion).
Trap Both decalins are achiral (trans has a centre of inversion; cis has a mirror plane in its time-averaged conformation), although each bridgehead is formally a stereocentre. Do not count a pair of enantiomers for decalin: there are only two stereoisomers, cis and trans.
