Dienes

The last is isoprene, 2-methylbuta-1,3-diene, the monomer of natural rubber (the trivial name is needed in Polymers). The letters –diene follow the root with an a: buta, penta, hexa; and the second e is retained because "diene" begins with a consonant.

Exam Trap Errors to avoid: but-1,3-diene (the a is missing), 1-butyne-3-ene (en must precede yn), and 2-butene or 2-methyl-1,3-butadiene, which are older locant styles; the current systematic forms are but-2-ene and 2-methylbuta-1,3-diene.
