8.8 Writing a full name: a checklist

  • Name the constitution first (Parts 2 to 7).
  • List every stereo-element: each stereocentre (R/S), each double bond (E/Z), with its locant from the final numbering.
  • Put the descriptors in parentheses at the front in ascending locant order: (2E,4S)-4-chlorohex-2-enoic acid.
  • Racemate: (±)- or rac-(R)-; relative configuration: rel-(1R,2S)- (old: (1R*,2S*)).
  • cis and trans may replace descriptors for ring substituents (trans-1,2-dibromocyclohexane).
