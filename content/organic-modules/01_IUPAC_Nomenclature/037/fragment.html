4.8 Sulphur compounds

  • Thiol: –SH, suffix -thiol with the e retained (ethanethiol; propane-2-thiol); prefix sulfanyl (older: mercapto).
  • Thioether (sulfide) R–S–R′: alkylsulfanyl prefix: (methylsulfanyl)ethane = ethyl methyl sulfide.
  • Sulfoxide R–S(=O)–R′: -sulfinyl prefix, e.g. (methanesulfinyl)methane = dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO). Sulfone R–SO₂–R′: -sulfonyl.
  • Sulfonic acid –SO₃H: -sulfonic acid; benzenesulfonic acid; prefix sulfo.
