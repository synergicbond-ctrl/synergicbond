◆Concept

This part derives the theoretical density formula and combines it with a BCC surface-gap calculation.

1. Density Derivation

◆Concept

Mass of one formula unit or atom of molar mass M is M/N_A. If a cell contains Z formula units/atoms, its mass is ZM/N_A.

∑Formula

m_cell = ZM/N_A.

◆Concept

For a cubic cell V=a³.

∑Formula

ρ = ZM/(N_A a³).

2. BCC Interfacial Separation Along an Edge

◆Concept

In BCC, corner atoms do not touch along the edge. The surface-to-surface gap between adjacent corner atoms is a−2r.

∑Formula

For BCC, r = (√3/4)a.

∑Formula

Edge gap = a(1−√3/2) ≈ 0.134a.

3. Unit Discipline

  • 1 pm = 10⁻¹⁰ cm.
  • 1 Å = 10⁻⁸ cm.
  • Use molar mass in g mol⁻¹ to obtain density in g cm⁻³ when a is converted to cm.

⚠JEE Advanced traps & edge cases

  • Use Z for the same chemical unit whose molar mass M is used.
  • Never insert a in pm directly into a formula expecting cm³.
  • In BCC, edge gap is not zero; contact is along the body diagonal.

▶Solved examples

Worked example 1

Example 1 — numerical

A BCC metal has adjacent-corner surface gap 60.3 pm and atomic mass 48. Find density.

  1. 0.134a≈60.3 pm ⇒ a≈450 pm.
  2. Convert a=4.50×10⁻⁸ cm.
  3. Use Z=2 and ρ=ZM/(N_Aa³).

ρ ≈ 1.75 g cm⁻³.

Worked example 2

Example 2

An FCC metal has M=108 g mol⁻¹ and a=408 pm. Estimate density.

  1. Z=4.
  2. a=4.08×10⁻⁸ cm.
  3. Substitute in ρ=4M/(N_Aa³).

ρ ≈ 10.6 g cm⁻³.
