Chapter map and prerequisites

This module teaches how to turn any organic structure into its IUPAC name and any name back into a structure. Scope assumption: the school syllabus on nomenclature, extended to the full range met in JEE Advanced problems (polyfunctional compounds, bridged, spiro and fused rings, heterocycles, stereodescriptors, retained names and the names of common reagents). Points where the 2013 recommendations differ from the long-standing school convention are marked Compare & Distinguish and are enrichment unless stated otherwise.

Prerequisites (each is used explicitly):

  • Hybridisation and the shapes of carbon (sp³, sp², sp): needed to read skeletal and condensed formulas (§1.6).
  • Recognising functional groups: –OH, –CHO, >C=O, –COOH, –NH₂ and so on (§4.1).
  • Types of structural isomerism (chain, position, functional): needed for isomer counting (§2.5, §10.3).
  • Cahn–Ingold–Prelog priority: taught from first principles in §8.2, but a first acquaintance with R/S and E/Z helps.
