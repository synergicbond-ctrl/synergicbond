20.3 Physical properties

  • Boiling points are close to those of alkanes of similar mass and far below those of isomeric alcohols: CH3OCH3 −24 °C vs C2H5OH 78 °C; C2H5OC2H5 35 °C vs butan-1-ol 118 °C; pentane 36 °C. THF 66 °C; 1,4-dioxane 101 °C; anisole 154 °C.
  • Solubility: diethyl ether 7 g per 100 mL water, because O accepts H-bonds from water; solubility is comparable to that of butan-1-ol, the isomer. THF, dioxane and DME are completely miscible (the ring or the extra oxygens make O more exposed).
  • Chemical inertness and solvent power: ethers do not react with bases, dilute acids, active metals (Na is used to dry them), reducing agents (LiAlH4 is used in ether) or oxidants; they are excellent solvents for organometallic reagents, because the lone pairs of oxygen solvate the metal (Grignard reagents are in fact R–Mg–X·2OEt2).
  • Lewis basicity: BF3·OEt2 (boron trifluoride etherate), the protonated ether R2OH+ (pKa about −3.6) in concentrated H2SO4: ethers (and alcohols) dissolve in cold conc. H2SO4, alkanes and alkyl halides do not.
  • Peroxide formation: ethers with α-hydrogens react slowly with O2 (radical chain at C–H α to oxygen), giving hydroperoxides and polymeric peroxides, which are explosive when concentrated by distillation. Diethyl ether, diisopropyl ether, THF, dioxane are the worst. Test: shake with aqueous KI/acetic acid (yellow–brown I2) or acidified FeSO4 + KSCN (red Fe(SCN)3); removal: shake with FeSO4 or Na2SO3 solution, or pass through activated alumina; prevention: store in amber bottles over NaOH/KOH, add BHT as inhibitor, never distil to dryness.
Example 55 L1 Foundation

Ether isomers

Write the IUPAC names of all ether isomers of C4H10O and state which is chiral.

Show solution
  1. Three ethers: methoxypropane (methyl n-propyl ether), 2-methoxypropane (methyl isopropyl ether), ethoxyethane (diethyl ether).
  2. None has a stereocentre; none is chiral. (The isomeric alcohol butan-2-ol is chiral.)
Example 56 L1 Foundation

Boiling points

Arrange in increasing boiling point: pentane, diethyl ether, butan-1-ol, ethyl methyl ether.

Show solution
  1. Ethyl methyl ether (M = 60, bp 7 °C) < diethyl ether (35 °C) ≈ pentane (36 °C) < butan-1-ol (118 °C).
  2. Order: ethyl methyl ether < diethyl ether < pentane < butan-1-ol (the middle two are within 1.5 K).
