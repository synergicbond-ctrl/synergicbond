Learning objectives

By the end of the module you should be able to:

  • define monomer, repeat unit, DP, functionality, tacticity, homopolymer and copolymer, and draw the repeat unit of any named polymer from its monomer(s) and back;
  • classify any polymer by source, structure, mode, intermolecular forces and composition, with the reason for each;
  • write initiation, propagation and termination for a radical polymerisation, and say which monomers suit radical, cationic, anionic and coordination routes and why;
  • explain the orbital reasoning behind regiochemistry, alternating copolymerisation, amide formation and the planarity of the amide group;
  • apply the Carothers equation and the stoichiometric-imbalance equation;
  • explain rubber elasticity, cis/trans rubbers and vulcanisation, and name the synthetic rubbers with their monomers and uses;
  • name the condensation polymers, their monomers, conditions, small molecule lost and uses, and distinguish resol from novolac;
  • calculate M<sub>n</sub>, M<sub>w</sub>, PDI, DP, osmotic-pressure and end-group molar masses and monomer mass balances.
