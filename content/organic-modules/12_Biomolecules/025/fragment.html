5.1 Vitamins

Vitamins are organic compounds needed in small amounts that the body cannot make (or makes too little of); their absence causes a deficiency disease. They are classed by solubility: fat-soluble (A, D, E, K), stored in the liver and fat, excess can be toxic (hypervitaminosis), and water-soluble (B-complex and C), not stored, excreted in urine, need regular intake. Most B vitamins are precursors of coenzymes.

Trap Vitamin C is a lactone, not an acid in the usual sense: ascorbic acid (pKa1 4.2) is acidic because of the enolic OH at C-3 of the 2,3-enediol; it has no –COOH. L-Ascorbic acid is (5R) by CIP and is both a reducing agent (oxidises to dehydroascorbic acid) and a cofactor. Vitamin D is not a vitamin in the strict sense for a person in sunlight.
Trap Vitamin B12 is the only vitamin with a metal ion (cobalt, a Co–C bond in the coenzyme form). Humans cannot synthesise it; bacteria do. It is not found in plant foods, so strict vegans need supplements.

Match vitamin and disease

Match: (a) vitamin C, (b) vitamin B12, (c) vitamin D, (d) vitamin K, (e) vitamin B1 with (i) rickets, (ii) scurvy, (iii) pernicious anaemia, (iv) beri-beri, (v) haemorrhage.

Show solution
  1. (a)–(ii), (b)–(iii), (c)–(i), (d)–(v), (e)–(iv).
Example 73 Adv

Fat- or water-soluble?

Classify as fat-soluble or water-soluble and explain from the structure: retinol, ascorbic acid, vitamin E, riboflavin.

Show solution
  1. Retinol: C20 polyene with a single OH: fat-soluble. α-Tocopherol: C29, one phenolic OH, long isoprenoid chain: fat-soluble. Ascorbic acid: many OH, a lactone, small (C6): water-soluble. Riboflavin: polyhydroxy ribitol chain and polar isoalloxazine: water-soluble.
Example 74 Adv

Vitamin C acidity

Ascorbic acid contains no –COOH, yet it is acidic (pKa1 = 4.2). Which proton is lost and why is the conjugate base stable?

Show solution
  1. The C-3 enolic OH is lost. The conjugate base is a vinylogous carboxylate: the negative charge on O-3 is delocalised through C-3=C-2 onto the lactone carbonyl O (O=C-1), exactly as in a carboxylate.
Example 75 Adv

Vitamin D synthesis

How does sunlight produce vitamin D3 and why does this make "rickets" a disease of low sunlight?

Show solution
  1. UV-B (≈ 290–315 nm) opens the B-ring of 7-dehydrocholesterol (a photochemical electrocyclic ring opening) to give pre-vitamin D3, which thermally isomerises to vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol, C27H44O). Without sunlight or dietary D, calcium and phosphate absorption fails and bones soften: rickets.
Example 76 Main

The B-vitamin containing cobalt

Name the vitamin that contains a metal and give the metal; state the ring system.

Show solution
  1. Vitamin B12 (cobalamin): cobalt (Co3+) bound in a corrin ring (a macrocycle related to porphyrin but lacking one methine bridge).
