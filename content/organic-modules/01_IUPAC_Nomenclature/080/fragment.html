HYF An acid chloride R–COCl is an acyl chloride (acetyl chloride, benzoyl chloride); an anhydride (RCO)₂O uses the acid name (acetic anhydride, phthalic anhydride). Acetyl, acetate and acetic belong to one family: the acyl group, the anion or ester, and the acid.
Compare & Distinguish Acetyl is CH₃CO–, acetyl chloride is CH₃COCl, acetate is CH₃COO⁻ (or its esters), and acetic acid is CH₃COOH. Acetyl and acetoxy (CH₃COO–) differ by one oxygen: acetophenone is a ketone, phenyl acetate is an ester.
