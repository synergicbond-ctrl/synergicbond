RM-059 [Application] · Master sheet Q59 · Status: Verified (matching enumeration, RDKit graph)

Bond order of C1–C2 in naphthalene (2 decimals), using: π-bond order = 1 + fraction of Kekulé structures in which the bond is double; naphthalene has three equivalent Kekulé structures.

Full solution
  1. C1–C2 is an α–β bond. Of the three Kekulé structures, two place a double bond on C1–C2.
  2. Bond order = 1 + 2/3 = 1.67.
  3. Check by enumeration: perfect matchings of the naphthalene π-graph = 3; the α–β bond is double in 2 of them.

Answer: 1.67

RM-060 [Application] · Master sheet Q60 · Status: Verified (matching enumeration)

Bond order of C2–C3 in naphthalene (2 decimals), same rule as Q59.

Full solution
  1. C2–C3 is a β–β bond. It is double in only 1 of the 3 Kekulé structures.
  2. Bond order = 1 + 1/3 = 1.33.

Answer: 1.33

RM-061 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q61 · Status: Verified (matching enumeration; the 5-structure count checked)

Bond order of C9–C10 in phenanthrene (2 decimals). Phenanthrene has five Kekulé structures.

Full solution
  1. C9–C10 is the central CH=CH bond of the middle ring.
  2. Enumerating the five perfect matchings gives a double bond on C9–C10 in four of them.
  3. Bond order = 1 + 4/5 = 1.80.

Answer: 1.80
