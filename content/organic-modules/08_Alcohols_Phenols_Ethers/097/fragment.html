28. How the problem set is organised

There are three levels, with full solutions to every problem (open the solution only after a real attempt).

  • Level 1 (Foundation, L1): single-concept questions: names, classification, one reagent, one rule. About 140 problems.
  • Level 2 (Main, L2): two- or three-step reasoning: mechanisms, comparison and ordering, multi-step synthesis, numerical work. About 140 problems.
  • Level 3 (Advanced, L3): JEE Advanced style: multiple-correct, integer type, matrix match, paragraph-based sets and structure-elucidation puzzles. About 130 problems.

Numbering continues through the three levels (Practice 1, 2, 3, …). Topic tags: ALC (alcohols), PHE (phenols), ETH (ethers), EPX (epoxides), THI (thiols/crown ethers), MIX (mixed).
