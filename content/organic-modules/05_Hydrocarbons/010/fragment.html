9. Summary sheet: alkanes

  • Radical chemistry only: halogenation (F > Cl > Br ≫ I reactivity; I > Br > Cl > F selectivity), nitration, SO2Cl2, combustion, cracking. No reaction with acids, bases, oxidants or reductants at room temperature (except tertiary C–H + KMnO4).
  • Chain mechanism: initiation (hν) – propagation – termination. Rate-determining step is H abstraction. Selectivity is set by radical stability: 3° > 2° > 1°.
  • Preparation: Wurtz (symmetrical R–R, dry ether), Corey–House (R–R′), Kolbe (electrolysis, R–R), soda lime (R–H, one C less), hydrogenation, hydrolysis of Grignard, reduction of RX, Clemmensen/Wolff–Kishner.
  • Bromine abstracts H selectively (late TS): 2-methylpropane + Br2 gives >99 % tert-butyl bromide.
  • Conformations: anti < gauche (3.8) < eclipsed (16) < fully eclipsed (19 kJ mol-1); ethane barrier 12 kJ.
