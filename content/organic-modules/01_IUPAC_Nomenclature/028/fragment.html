3.7 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Name HC≡C–CH₂–CH=CH₂.

Show answer

Pent-1-en-4-yne. The sets {1,4} and {1,4} tie, so the double bond receives the lower locant.

Concept Check

Why is CH₂=CH–CH(CH₃)₂ named 3-methylbut-1-ene and not 2-methylbut-3-ene?

Show answer

The double bond ranks above prefixes in numbering, so it must receive the lowest locant (1), which fixes the methyl at 3.

Concept Check

How many (E)/(Z) stereoisomers does hexa-2,4-diene have, and why not four?

Show answer

Three (EE, ZZ, EZ). The molecule is symmetrical, so (E,Z) and (Z,E) are the same compound.
