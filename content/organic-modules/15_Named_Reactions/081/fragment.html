Selectivity quick map. NaBH4: only aldehydes/ketones (and acid chlorides). LiAlH4: everything polar. DIBAL-H: ester → aldehyde. BH3: acids first. Pd/H2: C=C, C≡C, NO2 first. Na/NH3: alkyne → trans; arene → diene.

Trap A frequent question gives a molecule with two reducible groups and asks which reagent reduces only one: ester + ketone with NaBH4 reduces the ketone only; ester + ketone with LiAlH4 reduces both (protect the ketone as an acetal first to reduce only the ester); COOH + ester: BH3·THF reduces the acid only; C=C + C=O with NaBH4 reduces C=O only, with H2/Pd reduces C=C only; NO2 + C=O: Sn/HCl or H2/Pd reduces NO2 (NaBH4 spares NO2).
