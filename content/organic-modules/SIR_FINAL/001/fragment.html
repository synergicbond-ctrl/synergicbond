2. Basicity of anilines

Aryl amines are much weaker bases than alkyl amines because the nitrogen lone pair delocalises into the ring, and that delocalisation is lost on protonation (Ref. [1], Section 19-6).

Example 1 · Basicity · electronic effects only

Increasing basicity: p-nitroaniline (1.0) < aniline (4.60) < N,N-dimethylaniline (5.06)

The NO₂ group withdraws the lone pair by resonance (−M), so p-nitroaniline is the weakest base. The two N-methyl groups donate by induction (+I), which raises the basicity of N,N-dimethylaniline above aniline. No ortho group is present, so SIR does not apply here.

Source: Ref. [1], Table 19-3 and Section 19-6.
