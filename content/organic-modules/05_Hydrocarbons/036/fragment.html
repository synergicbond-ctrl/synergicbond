35. Isomerisation, oligomerisation and polymerisation

  • Base-catalysed isomerisation: 1-butyne ⇌ 2-butyne (alcoholic KOH, 175 °C) through the allene CH3CH=C=CH2; internal alkynes are more stable (≈ 12 kJ). NaNH2 drives the equilibrium to the terminal acetylide (alkyne zipper).
  • Dimerisation: 2 HC≡CH → CH2=CH–C≡CH (vinylacetylene; Cu2Cl2/NH4Cl).
  • Trimerisation: 3 HC≡CH → benzene (red-hot Fe tube, 873 K); 3 CH3C≡CH → 1,3,5-trimethylbenzene (mesitylene); 3 CH3COCH3 + conc. H2SO4 → mesitylene (by condensation).
  • Tetramerisation: 4 HC≡CH → cyclooctatetraene (Ni(CN)2, Reppe).
  • Polymerisation: ethyne → polyacetylene (conducting polymer, doped).
