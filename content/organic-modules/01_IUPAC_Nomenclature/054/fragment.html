6.7 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Give the von Baeyer name of the hydrocarbon skeleton whose bridges contain 3, 3 and 1 carbons.

Show answer

Bicyclo[3.3.1]nonane (3 + 3 + 1 + 2 = 9 atoms).

Concept Check

Which is numbered first in a bicyclo compound: the longest or the shortest bridge?

Show answer

The longest bridge is numbered first; the shortest bridge is numbered last, starting from the atom nearer C-1.

Concept Check

Name the position of the OH group in the compound HO–C₁₀H₇ (naphthol) in which OH is on a carbon adjacent to a fusion carbon.

Show answer

Position 1 (α): naphthalen-1-ol.
