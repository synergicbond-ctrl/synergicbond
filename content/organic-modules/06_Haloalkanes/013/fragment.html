12. Elimination reactions: dehydrohalogenation

In β-elimination a base removes a proton from the β-carbon while the leaving group departs from the α-carbon, so a C=C double bond forms: H–C–C–X + B− → C=C + BH + X−. Alkyl halides give alkenes this way when heated with a strong base, usually alcoholic KOH or sodium alkoxide. Three limiting mechanisms are recognised: E2 (concerted), E1 (carbocation first), E1cB (carbanion first). They are the elimination counterparts of SN2 and SN1.
