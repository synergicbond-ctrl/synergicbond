Practice 373 L3 Paragraph set 1

Passage on alkene stability and E2

Alkyl halide X (C6H13Br) gives, with alcoholic KOH, a mixture of alkenes. With t-BuOK in t-BuOH the same X gives mainly a single terminal alkene. Hydrogenation of either alkene gives 2-methylpentane. X is a tertiary bromide. (a) Identify X. (b) Give the Saytzeff product. (c) Give the Hofmann product.

Show answer

(a) A tertiary bromide giving 2-methylpentane: 2-bromo-2-methylpentane, (CH3)2CBrCH2CH2CH3. (b) Saytzeff: 2-methylpent-2-ene. (c) Hofmann: 2-methylpent-1-ene (the terminal alkene).

Using the data of the previous problem, which alkene of the Saytzeff pair would give an acetone on ozonolysis, and what is the second product?

Show answer

2-Methylpent-2-ene → propanone + propanal.

Practice 375 L3 Paragraph set 2

Passage on an unknown diene

Compound Y (C7H10) decolourises Br2, adds 2 mol H2 (Pt) to give C7H14, gives an adduct with maleic anhydride, and does not react with Tollens' reagent. Deduce the unsaturation pattern and give one possible structure with its Diels–Alder adduct.

Show answer

DoU of C7H10 = 3. Two H2 are absorbed, so there are two C=C, and the third unit is a ring (C7H14 = a cycloalkane). The maleic anhydride adduct shows a conjugated diene; no terminal alkyne (negative Tollens). One structure: 1-methylcyclohexa-1,3-diene; its adduct with maleic anhydride is a bicyclo[2.2.2]octene anhydride with a methyl group on a bridgehead carbon (the 1-substituted diene gives the "ortho"-type product).

Practice 376 L3 Paragraph set 3

Passage on radical halogenation

2,3-Dimethylbutane reacts with Br2/hν to give mainly Z. Z with alcoholic KOH gives W. W reacts with HBr in the presence of peroxide to give V. Name Z, W and V and state how many stereocentres V has.

Show answer

Br• abstracts the tertiary H (selectivity 1640 : 1): Z = 2-bromo-2,3-dimethylbutane. Alcoholic KOH (Saytzeff): W = 2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene, a symmetrical alkene. Br• adds to either carbon, so V = 2-bromo-2,3-dimethylbutane again (the same as Z), which has no stereocentre (C-3 carries two identical methyl groups).

Practice 380 L3 Reasoning

Why Lindlar stops

Explain why Lindlar's catalyst reduces alkynes but stops at the alkene stage.

Show answer

Pd is poisoned (Pb(OAc)2, quinoline) so that it binds the stronger-binding alkyne but releases the alkene before further hydrogenation; unpoisoned Pd/C binds both and gives the alkane.

Practice 381 L3 Reasoning

Selectivity of bromination

Explain why the bromination of propane gives 97 % of 2-bromopropane although there are six primary and only two secondary hydrogens.

Show answer

The Br• abstraction is endothermic with a late TS, so it senses nearly the full difference in radical stability (secondary vs primary C–H BDE 98 vs 101 kcal mol-1), giving relative rates of 82 : 1 per hydrogen; 2 × 82 = 164 against 6 × 1 = 6.

Practice 382 L3 Reasoning

Gauche effect

Why do 1,2-difluoroethane and ethylene glycol prefer the gauche conformer?

Show answer

Hyperconjugation (σC–H → σ*C–F) stabilises gauche F/F (the gauche effect) in 1,2-difluoroethane; in ethylene glycol an O–H···O hydrogen bond also favours gauche.

Practice 383 L3 Reasoning

Boiling points

Arrange by boiling point: n-hexane, 2-methylpentane, 2,2-dimethylbutane, cyclohexane.

Show answer

Cyclohexane (81 °C) > n-hexane (69) > 2-methylpentane (60) > 2,2-dimethylbutane (50). (Ring compounds have higher bp than the open-chain alkane of equal carbons because of better packing and surface contact.)

Practice 384 L3 Reasoning

Reactivity of alkenes

Which alkene reacts faster with HBr, and why: CH2=CHCH3 or CH2=CHCF3?

Show answer

Propene: its cation CH3CH+CH3 is stabilised, whereas the CF3 group destabilises the cation and deactivates the alkene.

Practice 385 L3 Reasoning

Syn versus anti elimination

Which of the following compounds undergoes syn elimination on heating? (A) alkyl bromide + alc. KOH (B) amine oxide (C) quaternary ammonium hydroxide (D) vicinal dibromide + Zn

Show answer

(B). (A) and (D) are anti E2; (C) is Hofmann anti E2 in most cases.

Practice 386 L3 Reasoning

Hydride shift versus methyl shift

Dehydration of 2,2-dimethylcyclopentanol gives mainly which alkene?

Show answer

Methyl shift gives the 1,2-dimethylcyclopentyl cation (tertiary); loss of H+: 1,2-dimethylcyclopentene (tetrasubstituted, major).

Practice 387 L3 Reasoning

Which carbocation is formed

Rank the stability of the cations from protonating 3-methylbut-1-ene at C-1 and the cations after rearrangement.

Show answer

CH3–CH+–CH(CH3)2 (2°) < (CH3)2C+–CH2CH3 (3°). The 3° cation is more stable by about 60 kJ/mol in the gas phase; rearranged product dominates.

Practice 388 L3 Reasoning

Conjugation and acidity

Why is cyclopentadiene (pKa 16) much more acidic than cyclohexa-1,3-diene (pKa > 35)?

Show answer

Deprotonation gives the cyclopentadienyl anion, which is aromatic (6 π electrons, planar cyclic conjugation); no such stabilisation exists for the cyclohexadienyl anion.

Practice 389 L3 Reasoning

Symmetry and diastereotopic H

How many stereoisomeric 2,3-dichlorobutanes can form by chlorination of (R)-2-chlorobutane at C-3?

Show answer

The two hydrogens on C-3 are diastereotopic (the molecule is chiral). Replacing either of them, and attack on either face of the planar radical, gives two diastereomers: (2R,3R)-2,3-dichlorobutane (chiral) and (2R,3S)-2,3-dichlorobutane (meso).

Practice 390 L3 Reasoning

Why NaOH fails

Why does ethyne not react to a significant extent with NaOH(aq) even though it is acidic?

Show answer

pKa(H2O) = 15.7 < pKa(ethyne) = 25: the equilibrium C2H2 + OH− ⇌ HC≡C− + H2O lies far to the left (K ≈ 10−9).

Practice 391 L3 Calc

Equilibrium constant

Estimate K for HC≡CH + NH2− ⇌ HC≡C− + NH3 using pKa 25 and 38.

Show answer

K = 10(38 − 25) = 1013: effectively complete.

Practice 398 L3 Calc

Heat of reaction

The enthalpy of combustion of ethene is −1411 kJ mol-1 and of ethane −1560; of H2 −286. Calculate ΔH of hydrogenation of ethene.

Show answer

C2H4 + H2 → C2H6: ΔH = (−1411 − 286) − (−1560) = −137 kJ mol-1.

Practice 399 L3 Calc

Heat of formation of ethyne

Given ΔHc(C2H2) = −1300, ΔHf(CO2) = −394, ΔHf(H2O,l) = −286 kJ mol-1. Find ΔHf(C2H2).

Show answer

ΔHf = 2(−394) + (−286) − (−1300) = +226 kJ mol-1 (endothermic: ethyne is unstable relative to its elements).

Practice 400 L3 Reasoning

Why oxyacetylene is hot

Why does an oxyacetylene flame reach about 3000 °C?

Show answer

Ethyne has a large positive heat of formation (+226 kJ mol-1), so its combustion releases more energy per carbon, and O2 (not air) avoids heating N2.
