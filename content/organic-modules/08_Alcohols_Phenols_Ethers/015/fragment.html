29.5 Crown ethers and thiols

Practice 48 L1 THI

What does the name 18-crown-6 mean?

Show answer
  1. A cyclic polyether with 18 atoms in the ring, of which 6 are oxygens: (CH2CH2O)6.
Practice 49 L1 THI

Which cation does 18-crown-6 bind best? Why?

Show answer
  1. K+: its diameter (about 2.7 Å) matches the cavity (2.6–3.2 Å); the oxygens bind it by ion–dipole forces.
Practice 50 L1 THI

Why does KMnO4 dissolve in benzene in the presence of 18-crown-6?

Show answer
  1. The crown ether complexes K+ inside its polar cavity and its non-polar exterior is soluble in benzene; MnO4− accompanies it as the counter-ion (“purple benzene”).
Practice 51 L1 THI

Which crown ether is selective for Li+?

Show answer
  1. 12-Crown-4 (cavity about 1.2–1.5 Å).
Practice 52 L1 THI

Write the formula of ethanethiol and of dimethyl sulphide.

Show answer
  1. C2H5SH and CH3SCH3.
Practice 53 L1 THI

Which is a stronger acid: C2H5OH or C2H5SH?

Show answer
  1. C2H5SH (pKa about 10.5, versus 16).
Practice 54 L1 THI

Why is the boiling point of ethanethiol (35 °C) much lower than that of ethanol (78 °C)?

Show answer
  1. S–H···S hydrogen bonds are very weak (S is large and of low electronegativity), while ethanol forms strong O–H···O bonds.
Practice 55 L1 THI

What are the products of mild oxidation of a thiol (e.g. with I2)?

Show answer
  1. A disulphide, RS–SR.
Practice 56 L1 THI

What is the product of dimethyl sulphide with one equivalent of H2O2?

Show answer
  1. Dimethyl sulphoxide (DMSO).
Practice 57 L1 THI

Name the vesicant (blistering agent) that is a sulphide of formula S(CH2CH2Cl)2.

Show answer
  1. Mustard gas (bis(2-chloroethyl) sulphide).
