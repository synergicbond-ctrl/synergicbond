Example 7 Adv

Make benzene

Give three different ways to obtain benzene from compounds with six carbon atoms or fewer, and say which of them would make toluene from the analogous precursor.

Show solution
  1. (1) Cyclohexane → benzene: Pt/Pd, 573 K (loses 3 H2); the methylcyclohexane analogue gives toluene.
  2. (2) Benzoic acid → benzene: soda lime, Δ (loses CO2); p-toluic acid gives toluene.
  3. (3) Ethyne → benzene: 873 K, red-hot iron tube (cyclotrimerisation); propyne gives mesitylene and 1,2,4-trimethylbenzene, not toluene, since three propyne units are involved.
  4. (4) Phenol + Zn dust → benzene; p-cresol + Zn → toluene.
