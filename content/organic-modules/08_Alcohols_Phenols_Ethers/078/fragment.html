20.2 Isomerism

Ethers CnH2n+2O are functional isomers of alcohols; ethers also show metamerism (isomerism by the distribution of carbons on the two sides of O).

Trap Count methyl sec-butyl ether, CH3OCH(CH3)CH2CH3, as chiral (one stereocentre) when asked for the number of stereoisomers of C5H12O. The ethers of C5H12O are methyl n-butyl, methyl isobutyl, methyl sec-butyl, methyl tert-butyl, ethyl n-propyl and ethyl isopropyl ether.
