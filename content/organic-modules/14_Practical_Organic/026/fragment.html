7.6 Test for phosphorus

Fuse the compound with an oxidising agent, sodium peroxide (Na2O2); phosphorus is oxidised to phosphate. Boil the aqueous extract with conc. HNO3 (convert to H3PO4), then add ammonium molybdate. A canary yellow precipitate of ammonium phosphomolybdate, (NH4)3PO4·12MoO3, shows phosphorus.

Formula Na3PO4 + 3HNO3 → H3PO4 + 3NaNO3; H3PO4 + 12(NH4)2MoO4 + 21HNO3 → (NH4)3PO4·12MoO3↓ + 21NH4NO3 + 12H2O

Sulphur in the same Na2O2 fusion is oxidised to sulphate (BaCl2 → white BaSO4), so the same fusion can test P and S.
