16.5 Lederer–Manasse reaction: phenol and formaldehyde

Phenol + HCHO (in alkaline or acidic solution) → o- and p-hydroxybenzyl alcohols (salicyl alcohol and 4-hydroxybenzyl alcohol). Further reaction with more phenol gives methylene bridges and finally a cross-linked thermosetting polymer: Bakelite (phenol–formaldehyde resin). Under acid catalysis with excess phenol the linear novolac forms (cured with hexamine); with base and excess HCHO a resol forms.
