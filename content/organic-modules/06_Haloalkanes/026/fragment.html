25.5 Top 25 traps in this chapter

  • 1. Aqueous KOH substitutes; alcoholic KOH eliminates.
  • 2. Neopentyl is primary but SN2-inert; also no E2 (no β-H).
  • 3. SN1 gives racemate but SNi, NGP give retention.
  • 4. Fluoride is the worst leaving group in SN1/SN2 but the best in SNAr.
  • 5. Dipole moment: CH3Cl > CH3F; chlorobenzene (1.69 D) < ethyl chloride (2.05 D).
  • 6. NaI/acetone drives Finkelstein by precipitating NaCl/NaBr.
  • 7. HBr + peroxide (anti-Markovnikov) is for HBr only.
  • 8. t-BuOK gives Hofmann, EtONa gives Zaitsev; with F or N+R3 leaving group, Hofmann.
  • 9. E2 needs anti-periplanar (diaxial in cyclohexane); the cis isomer with axial Cl eliminates faster.
  • 10. Rearrangement happens in SN1 and E1 (including HX + 2° alcohol) but not in SN2, E2, SOCl2, PBr3.
  • 11. Aryl and vinyl halides do not do SN1/SN2 (sp2 C–X, resonance, no back-side).
  • 12. SNAr needs EWG at ortho/para, not meta.
  • 13. In benzyne, both ortho positions blocked → no reaction; unsymmetrical aryne gives isomeric amines.
  • 14. Halobenzenes are o,p-directors but deactivating.
  • 15. Grignards are destroyed by any acidic H (water, alcohols, acids, terminal alkynes, amines).
  • 16. Ethylene oxide adds two carbons; CO2 one; HCHO one.
  • 17. Wurtz with two different halides gives three alkanes.
  • 18. KCN → RCN; AgCN → RNC; KNO2 → RONO; AgNO2 → RNO2.
  • 19. Hunsdiecker product has one carbon fewer; Br2 in CCl4 with silver salt.
  • 20. NBS gives allylic/benzylic bromination, not addition.
  • 21. Benzene + Cl2: FeCl3 → substitution; sunlight → BHC addition.
  • 22. Chloroform: dark bottles, ethanol added to remove phosgene.
  • 23. Iodoform positive: CH3CO– and CH3CH(OH)–; not methanol, not diethyl ketone.
  • 24. Alkyl isocyanide (carbylamine): foul smell; test for 1° amines only.
  • 25. Toluene: Cl2/hν → side chain, Cl2/Fe → ring.
