Problem 138 [Advanced]

Predict the effect of steric inhibition of resonance on N,N-dimethylaniline with 2,6-dimethyl groups.

Worked solution
  1. ortho methyls twist NMe₂ out of plane; the nitrogen lone pair no longer conjugates; behaves as an aliphatic amine: more basic.

Answer: Conclusion: more basic than N,N-dimethylaniline.

Problem 139 [Advanced]

Predict the effect of steric inhibition of resonance on 3,5-dimethyl-4-nitrophenol.

Worked solution
  1. flanking methyls twist NO₂ out of the ring; loss of −M makes it a weaker acid than 4-nitrophenol (7.14).

Answer: Conclusion: weaker acid than 4-nitrophenol.
