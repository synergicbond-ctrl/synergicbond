Further problems: substitution in synthesis and special cases (part B)

These problems extend the earlier material on SN1 and SN2 to situations in which structure, solvent or additives change the mechanism: carbocation stability in cyclic, aromatic and bridged systems, deuterium isotope effects, acid-catalysed reactions of alcohols and ethers, rearrangements, SNi and allylic variants, and epoxide opening.

The second group deals with neighbouring-group participation, where a lone pair, a π bond or a σ bond in the same molecule displaces the leaving group first and is itself displaced afterwards. Look for rate enhancement, retention of configuration, unexpected rearrangement or scrambling of labels, and the geometric requirement that the participating group sit anti to the leaving group. Every problem has a full worked solution.
