41. Pairs that students confuse

PairDifference to remember
Allylic vs vinylic halideAllylic: X on sp3 C next to C=C (reactive). Vinylic: X on C=C (inert to SN).
Benzylic vs aryl halideBenzylic: X on sp3 C next to ring (reactive). Aryl: X on ring carbon (inert, except SNAr/benzyne).
Nucleophile vs baseNucleophile attacks carbon (kinetic); base removes H+ (thermodynamic). t-BuO−: base. I−: nucleophile.
Zaitsev vs HofmannMore substituted vs less substituted alkene; decided by base size and leaving group.
SN1 vs E1Same first step; E1 gains with heat and non-nucleophilic conditions.
SN2 vs SNiSNi (SOCl2 no base): retention; SN2: inversion.
Inversion vs R/S letter changeInversion is spatial; the letter changes only if the priority order of the groups is unchanged.
Sandmeyer vs GattermannCuX (cuprous salt) vs Cu powder + HX; Sandmeyer gives better yields.
Wurtz vs Wurtz–Fittig vs FittigR–R, Ar–R, Ar–Ar respectively; all with sodium in dry ether.
Meisenheimer complex vs benzyneAddition–elimination (activated ring) vs elimination–addition (non-activated, strong base).
Isocyanide vs nitrileR–N≡C (from AgCN or carbylamine) vs R–C≡N (from KCN).
Nitrite vs nitroR–O–N=O (KNO2) vs R–NO2 (AgNO2).
Iodoform vs carbylamineIodoform: I2/NaOH on CH3CO– or CH3CH(OH)–; carbylamine: CHCl3/KOH on RNH2.
