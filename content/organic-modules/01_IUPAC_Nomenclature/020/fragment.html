2.8 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Name CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₃ and state which criterion fixed the direction of numbering.

Show answer

2,3-Dimethylpentane. Numbering from the left gives {2,3}; from the right {3,4}. The first point of difference (2 < 3) fixed the direction.

Concept Check

Why is 1,1,3-trimethylcyclohexane numbered 1,1,3 and not 1,3,3?

Show answer

Both numberings start the set with 1 and 3, but {1,1,3} is lower than {1,3,3} at the second term (1 < 3).

Concept Check

A student names a compound "2-ethyl-4-methylpentane". What is wrong?

Show answer

An ethyl group on C-2 of a pentane chain would make the chain longer; the correct name is 2,4-dimethylhexane (the compound has a six-carbon chain).
