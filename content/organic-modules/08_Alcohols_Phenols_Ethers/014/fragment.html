29.4 Epoxides

Practice 33 L1 EPX

Write the structure of ethylene oxide and state the C–O–C bond angle approximately.

Show answer
  1. A three-membered ring C2H4O; bond angle about 61°, which causes ring strain (about 114 kJ mol−1).
Practice 34 L1 EPX

Why are epoxides much more reactive than ordinary ethers?

Show answer
  1. Ring strain (3-membered ring) is relieved when the ring opens by nucleophilic attack.
Practice 35 L1 EPX

How is ethylene oxide manufactured?

Show answer
  1. Direct oxidation of ethene with O2 over a silver catalyst at about 520–570 K.
Practice 36 L1 EPX

Name two laboratory methods of preparing an epoxide from an alkene.

Show answer
  1. (i) A peroxy-acid (mCPBA or peracetic acid). (ii) Halohydrin formation (X2/H2O) followed by base (NaOH).
Practice 37 L1 EPX

What is the stereochemistry of epoxidation of cis-but-2-ene with mCPBA?

Show answer
  1. Syn addition of oxygen: the cis-epoxide (meso-2,3-dimethyloxirane) forms.
Practice 38 L1 EPX

Write the product of ethylene oxide with water (acid or heat).

Show answer
  1. Ethylene glycol (ethane-1,2-diol).
Practice 39 L1 EPX

Write the product of ethylene oxide with ammonia.

Show answer
  1. 2-Aminoethanol (ethanolamine); further reaction gives diethanolamine and triethanolamine.
Practice 40 L1 EPX

Write the product of ethylene oxide with HCl.

Show answer
  1. 2-Chloroethanol (ethylene chlorohydrin).
Practice 41 L1 EPX

Write the product of ethylene oxide with CH3MgBr (then H3O+).

Show answer
  1. Propan-1-ol.
Practice 42 L1 EPX

In the base-catalysed opening of propylene oxide by CH3O−, which carbon is attacked?

Show answer
  1. The less substituted CH2 (SN2, steric control). Product: 1-methoxypropan-2-ol.
Practice 43 L1 EPX

In the acid-catalysed opening of propylene oxide by CH3OH, which carbon is attacked?

Show answer
  1. The more substituted CH (carries more positive charge). Product: 2-methoxypropan-1-ol.
Practice 44 L1 EPX

What is the stereochemical relationship of the two oxygen groups in the product of cyclohexene oxide with H3O+?

Show answer
  1. Trans (anti): backside attack gives trans-cyclohexane-1,2-diol.
Practice 45 L1 EPX

What is formed when cyclohexene oxide is treated with LiAlH4?

Show answer
  1. Cyclohexanol (hydride opens the ring).
Practice 46 L1 EPX

Name the polymer formed from ethylene oxide with base.

Show answer
  1. Polyethylene glycol (PEG, carbowax), HO(CH2CH2O)nH.
Practice 47 L1 EPX

What is epichlorohydrin used for?

Show answer
  1. With bisphenol A it gives epoxy resins (adhesives such as Araldite).
