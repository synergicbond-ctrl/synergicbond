Example 21 Adv

Distinguish with minimum reagents

How would you distinguish (a) acetaldehyde, acetone and benzaldehyde, (b) methylamine, dimethylamine and trimethylamine, (c) ethanol, propan-2-ol and tert-butyl alcohol?

Show solution
  1. (a) Tollens': acetaldehyde and benzaldehyde positive, acetone negative. Then Fehling's: acetaldehyde positive, benzaldehyde negative.
  2. (b) Carbylamine: only methylamine (smell). Then Hinsberg: dimethylamine gives insoluble sulphonamide; trimethylamine no reaction. (Or HNO2: N2 gas from 1°, yellow oil from 2°, no gas from 3°.)
  3. (c) Lucas: tert-butyl alcohol immediate turbidity; propan-2-ol turbid in 5 min; ethanol clear. (Iodoform: ethanol and propan-2-ol positive, tert-butyl alcohol negative.)
