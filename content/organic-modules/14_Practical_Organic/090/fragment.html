Problem set D: quantitative analysis and molecular formula

Atomic masses: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, S 32, Cl 35.5, Br 80, I 127, Ag 108, Ba 137, Mg 24, P 31, Pt 195 unless stated. Molar volume at STP 22 400 mL.

Practice 93 Foundation

0.20 g of an organic compound containing C, H and O gave 0.44 g CO2 and 0.18 g H2O on complete combustion. Calculate %C, %H and %O.

Show answer
  1. %C = (12/44)(0.44/0.20) × 100 = 60.00 %.
  2. %H = (2/18)(0.18/0.20) × 100 = 10.00 %.
  3. %O = 100 − 60.00 − 10.00 = 30.00 %.

Answer: C 60.00 %, H 10.00 %, O 30.00 %

Practice 94 Foundation

A hydrocarbon contains 85.7 % C and 14.3 % H; its vapour density is 28. Find the molecular formula.

Show answer
  1. C: 85.7/12 = 7.14; H: 14.3/1 = 14.3; ratio 1 : 2 ⇒ CH2 (14).
  2. M = 2 × 28 = 56; n = 56/14 = 4 ⇒ C4H8.

Answer: C4H8

Practice 95 Foundation

0.25 g of an organic compound gave 40.0 mL of N2 at STP in the Dumas method. Find %N.

Show answer
  1. Mass of N = 28 × 40.0/22 400 = 0.0500 g.
  2. %N = 0.0500/0.25 × 100 = 20.0 %.

Answer: 20.0 %

Practice 96 Application

0.35 g of a compound gave 42.5 mL of N2 collected over water at 300 K and 745 mm Hg (aqueous tension at 300 K = 27 mm Hg). Calculate %N.

  3. %N = (28 × 36.54/22 400)/0.35 × 100 = 13.05 %.

Practice 104 Application

A compound (M = 151) on combustion of 0.151 g gives 0.352 g CO2 and 0.0810 g H2O. A separate 0.151 g sample gives 11.2 mL of N2 at STP in the Dumas method. Deduce the molecular formula, assuming the remainder is oxygen.

Practice 109 Application

0.150 g of a volatile liquid displaced 35.0 mL of air measured over water at 300 K and 742 mm Hg (aqueous tension 27 mm Hg). Find the molar mass.

Show answer

  2. n = PV/RT = 0.9408 × 0.0350/(0.0821 × 300) = 1.337 × 10−3 mol.
  3. M = 0.150/1.337 × 10−3 = 112 g mol-1.

Practice 110 Application

20 mL of a gaseous hydrocarbon were exploded with 100 mL of O2. After cooling the volume was 70 mL; on treatment with KOH it became 30 mL. Find the formula.

Show answer
  1. CO2 = 70 − 30 = 40 mL ⇒ x = 40/20 = 2.
  2. Contraction = 120 − 70 = 50 mL = 20(1 + y/4) ⇒ 2.5 = 1 + y/4 ⇒ y = 6.
  3. C2H6. Check: O2 used = 20 × 3.5 = 70 mL, left 30 mL ✓.

Answer: C2H6

Practice 111 Application

30 mL of a mixture of methane and ethane were burnt in excess oxygen; the CO2 formed measured 40 mL. Find the composition of the mixture.

Show answer
  1. Let CH4 = a mL, C2H6 = b mL. a + b = 30; CO2: a + 2b = 40.
  2. Subtract: b = 10, a = 20.

Answer: CH4 20 mL, C2H6 10 mL

Practice 112 Advanced

10 mL of a mixture of ethane and ethyne is exploded with excess oxygen and the contraction (after cooling) is 22.5 mL. Find the composition. (Contraction per mL: C2H6 2.5 mL; C2H2 1.5 mL.)

Show answer
  1. C2H6: 1 + 6/4 = 2.5; C2H2: 1 + 2/4 = 1.5. Let ethane = a, ethyne = b: a + b = 10; 2.5a + 1.5b = 22.5.
  2. 2.5a + 1.5(10 − a) = 22.5 ⇒ a = 7.5, b = 2.5.

Answer: C2H6 7.5 mL, C2H2 2.5 mL
