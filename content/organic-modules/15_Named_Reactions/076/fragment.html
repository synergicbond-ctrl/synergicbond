69. Chugaev elimination and acetate pyrolysis (syn eliminations)

Chugaev: ROH → (NaOH, CS2) → sodium xanthate RO–CS–S−Na+ → (CH3I) → methyl xanthate RO–C(=S)–SCH3 → heat (420–470 K) → alkene + COS + CH3SH through a six-membered syn Ei transition state. Because no carbocation is involved, no skeletal rearrangement happens: this is the way to dehydrate an alcohol that would rearrange under acid (for example neopentyl-type or camphene-forming systems) and to dehydrate without isomerising the product alkene.

Δ (Chugaev)⟶ + +
Isopropyl methyl xanthate → propene + carbonyl sulfide + methanethiol
Key idea Compare dehydration routes to an alkene from an alcohol: H2SO4 (E1, carbocation, rearranges, Saytzeff); Al2O3, 620 K (E2-like, less rearrangement); POCl3/pyridine (E2, anti, no rearrangement); Chugaev (Ei, syn, no rearrangement, Hofmann tendency).
