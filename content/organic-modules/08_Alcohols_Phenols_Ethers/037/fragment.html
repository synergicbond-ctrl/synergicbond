6.1 With hydrogen halides

ROH + HX → RX + H2O. Reactivity of HX: HI > HBr > HCl (strength of acid and nucleophilicity of X−; HF is not useful). Reactivity of ROH: allyl, benzyl > 3° > 2° > 1° > CH3OH. Mechanism depends on the alcohol:

  • 3°, 2° (and allylic/benzylic): SN1 — protonation, loss of water to give the carbocation (slow), capture by X−. Rearrangements occur. Racemisation of chiral 2°/3° alcohols (often with some net inversion, because the ion pair is captured on the side opposite to the leaving water before it leaves).
  • Methyl and 1°: SN2 — X− attacks the protonated alcohol with inversion. HBr from NaBr/H2SO4 or HI from KI/H3PO4 is used. No rearrangement unless the carbon is neopentyl-like, where SN2 is impossibly slow and the reaction goes via a concerted methyl shift (below).
