21. Other reactions specific to aniline

  • Reaction with chloroform/KOH: phenyl isocyanide (carbylamine) – positive test.
  • Diazotisation (Part E) gives the key intermediate benzenediazonium chloride.
  • With CS2, heated: thiocarbanilide (N,N'-diphenylthiourea) + H2S; with HgCl2, phenyl isothiocyanate (mustard-oil).
  • With bleaching powder: transient violet colour. With FeCl3 or K2Cr2O7 (neutral): aniline black.
  • Skraup quinoline synthesis: aniline + glycerol + conc. H2SO4 + nitrobenzene (oxidant)/FeSO4 → quinoline. Glycerol is dehydrated to acrolein, the aniline undergoes Michael addition, cyclisation, and oxidation.
