Block I. Comparisons, scope and limitations

Practice 179 Main

Arrange in order of decreasing rate of the Cannizzaro reaction: p-nitrobenzaldehyde, benzaldehyde, p-methoxybenzaldehyde.

Show answer
  1. The rate depends on OH− addition to C=O and on hydride transfer to an electrophilic carbonyl: electron-withdrawing groups speed up both. Order: p-nitrobenzaldehyde > benzaldehyde > p-methoxybenzaldehyde.
Practice 180 Main

With benzene and AlCl3, which of these do NOT undergo Friedel–Crafts alkylation: nitrobenzene, anisole, aniline, chlorobenzene?

Show answer
  1. Nitrobenzene (ring strongly deactivated) and aniline (the amine complexes with AlCl3, giving –NH2→AlCl3, a strongly deactivating group). Anisole and chlorobenzene react (chlorobenzene slowly, o/p).
Practice 181 Main

Which of benzene, toluene, phenol and nitrobenzene give an aldehyde in the Gattermann–Koch reaction (CO, HCl, AlCl3/CuCl)?

Show answer
  1. Benzene and toluene (toluene gives p-tolualdehyde). Phenol complexes AlCl3; nitrobenzene is too deactivated.
Practice 182 Main

Arrange in order of the speed of the Lucas test: propan-1-ol, propan-2-ol, 2-methylpropan-2-ol, benzyl alcohol.

Show answer
  1. The rate follows SN1 cation stability. 2-Methylpropan-2-ol ≈ benzyl alcohol (turbid at once, tertiary and benzylic) > propan-2-ol (≈ 5 min) > propan-1-ol (no reaction at 25 °C). The test cannot separate tertiary from benzylic/allylic alcohols.
Practice 183 Main

Arrange the groups in order of migratory aptitude in the Baeyer–Villiger oxidation: methyl, ethyl, isopropyl, tert-butyl, phenyl.

Show answer
  1. tert-Butyl > isopropyl > phenyl > ethyl > methyl (the group that best supports positive charge moves). Aryl is placed below secondary alkyl but above primary alkyl in this standard order.
Practice 184 Main

Which alkyl halides can be used with sodium ethoxide for a good Williamson ether synthesis: CH3Br, CH3CH2Br, (CH3)2CHBr, (CH3)3CBr, C6H5Br?

Show answer
  1. CH3Br and CH3CH2Br (SN2). Isopropyl bromide gives mostly propene (E2); tert-butyl bromide gives only isobutene; bromobenzene does not undergo SN2.
Practice 185 Main

Which of these are good dienophiles in the Diels–Alder reaction with buta-1,3-diene: ethene, maleic anhydride, acrolein, ethyl propenoate, cyclohexane?

Show answer
  1. Dienophile reactivity rises with electron-withdrawing groups: maleic anhydride > acrolein > ethyl propenoate >> ethene (needs high T and pressure). Cyclohexane has no π bond.
Practice 186 Main

Which of CH3COOH, C6H5COOH, (CH3)3CCOOH, HCOOH, CH3CH2COOH undergo the HVZ reaction?

Show answer
  1. Only acids with an α-hydrogen: CH3COOH and CH3CH2COOH. Benzoic, pivalic and formic acid lack α-H.
Practice 187 Main

Which of HCHO, CH3CHO, C6H5CHO, (CH3)3CCHO, C6H5CH2CHO undergo the Cannizzaro reaction with conc. NaOH?

Show answer
  1. Aldehydes with no α-H: HCHO, C6H5CHO, (CH3)3CCHO. CH3CHO and PhCH2CHO undergo aldol instead.
Practice 188 Main

Which of HCHO, CH3CHO, C6H5CHO, (CH3)3CCHO, CH3COCH3 undergo aldol condensation with dilute NaOH?

Show answer
  1. Only those with α-H: CH3CHO and CH3COCH3.
Practice 189 Main

Choose the correct pair for the Williamson synthesis of ethyl tert-butyl ether, (CH3)3C–O–CH2CH3.

Show answer
  1. (CH3)3CONa + CH3CH2Br (primary halide, bulky alkoxide). The alternative C2H5ONa + (CH3)3CBr gives isobutene by E2.
Practice 190 Main

Rank the ease of decarboxylation on gentle heating: CH3COOH, CH3COCH2COOH, HOOC–CH2–COOH, C6H5COOH.

Show answer
  1. A β-keto acid and a malonic acid lose CO2 through a cyclic six-membered transition state: CH3COCH2COOH (≈ 350–370 K) > HOOC–CH2–COOH (≈ 400 K) >> CH3COOH, C6H5COOH (which lose CO2 only on soda-lime heating above 600 K).
Practice 191 Main

Which of acetamide, N-methylacetamide, benzamide and N,N-dimethylacetamide give the Hofmann degradation?

Show answer
  1. Only primary amides with –CONH2: acetamide and benzamide. N-Substituted amides cannot form the required N-bromoamide anion (R–CO–N−Br).
Practice 192 Main

Rank the speed of the Baeyer–Villiger oxidation by a peracid: cyclobutanone, cyclohexanone, acetophenone.

Show answer
  1. Ring-strain relief speeds the reaction: cyclobutanone > cyclohexanone > acetophenone.
Practice 193 Main

Which of ethane, propane, 2,3-dimethylbutane, 2-methylbutane and n-hexane can be made as essentially a single product from a single alkyl halide by the Wurtz reaction?

Show answer
  1. Only symmetrical R–R: ethane (from CH3X), 2,3-dimethylbutane (from (CH3)2CHX, although a secondary halide also eliminates), n-hexane (from n-C3H7X). Propane and 2-methylbutane are unsymmetrical and would need two different halides, giving mixtures.
Practice 194 Main

Distinguish the Sandmeyer from the Gattermann (copper-powder) reaction.

Show answer
  1. Both replace N2+ by Cl or Br. Sandmeyer uses a Cu(I) salt (Cu2Cl2/HCl, Cu2Br2/HBr) and gives better yields. Gattermann uses copper powder with HCl or HBr. Both are radical (electron-transfer) processes.
Practice 195 Main

Which of NaBH4, LiAlH4, DIBAL-H (excess, 25 °C), H2/Ni and BH3·THF reduce ethyl benzoate to benzyl alcohol?

Show answer
  1. LiAlH4 (readily), DIBAL-H in excess at 25 °C, and BH3 (slowly, esters react more slowly than acids). NaBH4 does not at 25 °C and H2/Ni does not reduce the ester (it would reduce the ring only under forcing conditions).
Practice 196 Main

Arrange in decreasing acidity of the C–H bond: ethyl ethanoate, diethyl propanedioate (malonate), ethyl 3-oxobutanoate, propanone.

Show answer
  1. pKa: ethyl 3-oxobutanoate ≈ 11 < diethyl malonate ≈ 13 < propanone ≈ 19 < ethyl ethanoate ≈ 25. So acidity: acetoacetate > malonate > propanone > ethyl ethanoate. This is why NaOEt (pKa 16) fully deprotonates the first two but not the last two.
Practice 197 Main

Which alkyl halide gives the greatest proportion of elimination (alkene) with NaOEt in ethanol: 1-bromobutane, 2-bromobutane, 2-bromo-2-methylpropane?

Show answer
  1. 2-Bromo-2-methylpropane (tertiary: no SN2 possible, E2 only). The secondary halide gives a mixture; the primary gives mainly the ether.
Practice 198 Main

Which reagent gives an alkyl isocyanide and which an alkyl cyanide from RBr: KCN or AgCN?

Show answer
  1. KCN → R–C≡N (cyanide); AgCN → R–N+≡C− (isocyanide). Likewise KNO2 gives mainly R–O–N=O and AgNO2 gives R–NO2.
Practice 199 Main

Which of the following reductions cannot be carried out by NaBH4 in ethanol: (i) PhCHO → PhCH2OH, (ii) PhCOOCH3 → PhCH2OH, (iii) acetone → propan-2-ol, (iv) PhCONH2 → PhCH2NH2?

Show answer
  1. (ii) and (iv): NaBH4 reduces only aldehydes and ketones (and acid chlorides); esters and amides need LiAlH4.
