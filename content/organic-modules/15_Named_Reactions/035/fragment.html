31. Lederer–Manasse reaction (hydroxymethylation of phenol)

Phenol + HCHO in the presence of base (or acid) gives o- and p-hydroxybenzyl alcohols by attack of the phenoxide ring on formaldehyde (an aldol-like addition at ring carbon). Further reaction with more phenol (heat, acid or base) links the rings through –CH2– bridges to give the cross-linked thermosetting polymer Bakelite (phenol–formaldehyde resin): see the polymers chapter.

HCHO, dil. NaOH, 320 K⟶
Phenol → 2-hydroxymethylphenol (ortho; the para isomer also forms)
