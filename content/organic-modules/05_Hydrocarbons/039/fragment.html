38. Preparation of dienes

  • Buta-1,3-diene: from butane/butene dehydrogenation (Cr2O3/Al2O3, 600 °C); from ethanol (Lebedev process: 2 C2H5OH → CH2=CH–CH=CH2 + 2 H2O + H2, ZnO/Al2O3, 700 K); by dehydration of butane-1,4-diol, or double dehydrohalogenation of 1,4-dihalobutane/1,3-dihalo compounds with alcoholic KOH.
  • Isoprene (2-methylbuta-1,3-diene): from dehydrogenation of isopentane/isopentene; by cracking.
  • Allenes: CH2=CBr–CH2Br + Zn → allene; base-induced isomerisation of propyne (CH3C≡CH ⇌ CH2=C=CH2).
  • Cyclopentadiene: by cracking of dicyclopentadiene (retro Diels–Alder) at 170 °C.
