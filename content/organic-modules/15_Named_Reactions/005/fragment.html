3. Ullmann reaction

Reagents: 2 Ar–I (or Ar–Br) + Cu powder, high temperature (≈ 500 K) → Ar–Ar. The copper must be activated; the reaction is facilitated by electron-withdrawing groups (especially an o-NO2) on the aryl halide. Mechanism: oxidative addition of ArX to Cu(0) gives ArCu(I)/ArCu(III) species which couple. Used for the synthesis of hindered or symmetrical biaryls.
