Reaction Intermediates and Mechanism

Synergic Bond Chemistry Module · JEE Main and Advanced · Organic Chemistry

Synergic BondChemistry Module · Organic Chemistry · Class 11 and 12 · JEE Main + Advanced

This chapter treats the reactive intermediates of organic chemistry (carbocations, carbanions, free radicals, carbenes, nitrenes and arynes) and the language used to write mechanisms. Every named reaction is built from a small set of elementary steps, and each step passes through a small set of intermediates. For each species the chapter answers four questions: how it is formed, how stable it is and why, what it can do next, and how its electrons are moved on paper. Orbital explanations are given wherever they explain the answer, so that rules such as backside attack or anti-periplanar elimination appear as consequences of orbital overlap and not as facts to memorise.
