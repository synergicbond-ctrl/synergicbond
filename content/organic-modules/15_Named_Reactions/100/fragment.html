Block F. Mechanism-based products

Practice 111 Main

2-Methylpropanal, (CH3)2CHCHO, is treated with dilute NaOH at room temperature. Give the aldol product and say whether it dehydrates.

Show answer
  1. The enolate carbon (the CH carrying two CH3) attacks the C=O of a second molecule: (CH3)2CH–CH(OH)–C(CH3)2–CHO, 3-hydroxy-2,2,4-trimethylpentanal.
  2. The α-carbon is now quaternary (no H), so the E1cB dehydration cannot occur: the β-hydroxy aldehyde is isolated.
Practice 112 Main

Benzaldehyde and pentan-3-one react in NaOH/EtOH on heating. Give the product and its geometry.

Show answer
  1. Only the ketone enolises (α-CH2): the enolate attacks PhCHO and water is lost: PhCH=C(CH3)–CO–CH2CH3, (E)-2-methyl-1-phenylpent-1-en-3-one. The E isomer (phenyl and acyl trans on the C=C) is more stable.
Practice 113 Main

Cyclohexanone reacts with methyl vinyl ketone in the presence of NaOEt, then heat. Give the product, its molecular formula and the number of rings.

Show answer
  1. Michael addition gives 2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexanone; the CH3CO enolate then closes onto the ring C=O (aldol) and water is lost.
  2. Product: the bicyclic enone (octalone), C10H14O, two rings, one C=C, one C=O (four degrees of unsaturation).
+ 1. NaOEt 2. Δ (Robinson)⟶
Net: ketone + MVK − H2O
Practice 114 Main

Acetophenone, formaldehyde and dimethylamine hydrochloride are heated in ethanol. Give the product and name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Mannich reaction: Me2N+=CH2 (iminium) is attacked by the enol of acetophenone at the CH3 carbon: PhCO–CH2CH2–NMe2 (3-(dimethylamino)-1-phenylpropan-1-one). On heating or with base it loses Me2NH to give PhCOCH=CH2.
Practice 115 Main

Diethyl malonate + NaOEt, then acrylonitrile (CH2=CHCN); then H3O+, heat. Give the final organic product.

Show answer
  1. Michael addition gives (EtOOC)2CH–CH2CH2CN. Hydrolysis converts both esters and the nitrile to –COOH; the malonic acid decarboxylates: HOOC–CH2CH2CH2–COOH, pentanedioic (glutaric) acid.
Practice 116 Main

Benzaldehyde is heated with propanedioic acid in pyridine with a trace of piperidine. Give the main product and the two by-products.

Show answer
  1. Doebner–Knoevenagel: E-cinnamic acid + CO2 + H2O.
Practice 117 Main

Propanone + ClCH2COOEt/NaOEt, then NaOH, then H3O+, heat. Give the final carbonyl compound.

Show answer
  1. Darzens gives the glycidic ester (2,2-dimethyl-3-ethoxycarbonyl-oxirane); hydrolysis and decarboxylation open the epoxide to the aldehyde: (CH3)2CH–CHO, 2-methylpropanal.
Practice 118 Main

Cyclohexanone, BrCH2COOEt and zinc in benzene, then H3O+. Give the product and say why a Grignard reagent could not be used.

Show answer
  1. Zinc enolate adds to C=O: ethyl (1-hydroxycyclohexyl)ethanoate. BrMgCH2COOEt would attack the ester group of another molecule (and is hard to make); the zinc enolate is less basic and chemoselective for the ketone.
Practice 119 Main

Benzaldehyde with Ph3P=CHCH3 (salt-free, THF) and with Ph3P=CHCOOEt. Give the alkene and its geometry in each case.

Show answer
  1. Non-stabilised ylide: mainly (Z)-1-phenylpropene. Stabilised ylide: ethyl (E)-cinnamate, because the first betaine can equilibrate and the more stable E-alkene is formed.
Practice 120 Main

N,N-Dimethylpentan-2-amine is treated with excess CH3I, then Ag2O/H2O and heat. How many moles of CH3I are used and what alkene forms mainly?

Show answer
  1. The amine is tertiary, so 1 mol of CH3I gives the quaternary salt. Hofmann elimination removes the most accessible β-H (from the CH3): pent-1-ene (major) + N(CH3)3.
Practice 121 Main

The N-oxide of N,N-dimethylcyclohexylamine is heated to 420 K. Give the products and the geometry requirement.

Show answer
  1. Cyclohexene + (CH3)2NOH (Cope elimination): syn, five-membered cyclic transition state; the oxygen of the N-oxide removes a cis β-hydrogen.
Practice 122 Main

Dehydration of 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol with H2SO4 gives mainly 2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene, but the Chugaev elimination gives 3,3-dimethylbut-1-ene. Explain.

Show answer
  1. Acid: loss of water gives the secondary cation, which takes CH3 from the quaternary carbon (1,2-shift) to the tertiary cation; E1 then gives the most substituted alkene. Chugaev (xanthate pyrolysis) is a concerted syn Ei with no cation, so the skeleton is unchanged and the alkene forms where the H is cis available: 3,3-dimethylbut-1-ene.
Practice 123 Main

Cyclopentadiene is stirred with maleic anhydride at 0 °C. Which stereoisomer of the adduct is formed faster, and why?

Show answer
  1. The endo adduct, because the anhydride C=O groups lie under the diene π system in the transition state and secondary orbital interactions stabilise it. The exo isomer is more stable thermodynamically, but the reaction is kinetically controlled at 0 °C.
Practice 124 Main

2,3-Dimethylbuta-1,3-diene + propenal (acrolein): give the product.

Show answer
  1. 3,4-Dimethylcyclohex-3-ene-1-carbaldehyde; the symmetric diene means no regiochemical issue.
Practice 125 Main

Isoprene + methyl propenoate: give the major regioisomer.

Show answer
  1. 2-Substituted diene (donor at C-2) + acrylate → "para" (1,4) product: methyl 4-methylcyclohex-3-ene-1-carboxylate.
Practice 126 Main

(Z)-But-2-ene and (E)-but-2-ene are each treated with CH2I2/Zn(Cu). Give the products.

Show answer
  1. Syn, concerted carbenoid addition retains geometry. Z → cis-1,2-dimethylcyclopropane (meso). E → trans-1,2-dimethylcyclopropane (racemic pair).
Practice 127 Main

Cyclohexene + CHCl3/KOC(CH3)3. Give the product and the reactive species.

Show answer
  1. 7,7-Dichlorobicyclo[4.1.0]heptane; the reagent is dichlorocarbene :CCl2.
Practice 128 Main

Potassium phenoxide is heated with CO2 at 500 K, then acidified. Give the major product and compare with sodium phenoxide at 400 K.

Show answer
  1. 4-Hydroxybenzoic acid (para, thermodynamic; K+ does not chelate). Sodium phenoxide at 400 K gives salicylic acid (ortho, via Na+ chelation).
Practice 129 Main

p-Cresol + CHCl3/NaOH, then H+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Para is blocked: 2-hydroxy-5-methylbenzaldehyde. (A small amount of the 4-methyl-4-dichloromethylcyclohexadienone also forms.)
Practice 130 Main

Phloroglucinol (benzene-1,3,5-triol) + CH3CN, HCl, ZnCl2, then H2O. Give the product and the name.

Show answer
  1. Houben–Hoesch: 2,4,6-trihydroxyacetophenone (all positions are equivalent and highly activated).
Practice 131 Main

N,N-Dimethylaniline + DMF/POCl3, then H2O. Give the product and the electrophile.

Show answer
  1. 4-(Dimethylamino)benzaldehyde; electrophile: the chloroiminium ion [Me2N=CHCl]+ (Vilsmeier reagent), attacking para.
Practice 132 Main

Phenyl benzoate + AlCl3 at 440 K. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. 2-Hydroxybenzophenone (ortho, thermodynamic chelate; Fries rearrangement).
Practice 133 Main

Allyl 2,6-dimethylphenyl ether is heated at 470 K. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Both ortho positions are blocked, so after the first [3,3] shift (to the ortho carbon, non-aromatic dienone with a quaternary carbon) a Cope shift moves the allyl to the para position: 4-allyl-2,6-dimethylphenol.
Practice 134 Main

3-Chlorobutan-2-one + NaOCH3. Give the product and the intermediate.

Show answer
  1. Intermediate: 2-methylcyclopropanone (from enolate at the CH3 displacing Cl). Methoxide opens it to the more stable (primary) carbanion: methyl 2-methylpropanoate.
Practice 135 Main

Phenanthrene-9,10-dione + KOH, then H3O+. Give the product and the change of ring size.

Show answer
  1. 9-Hydroxyfluorene-9-carboxylic acid; the central six-membered ring contracts to five (benzilic acid rearrangement).
Practice 136 Main

Compare cyclohexanone + HN3/H2SO4 with propanone + HN3/H2SO4.

Show answer
  1. Cyclohexanone → ε-caprolactam (ring N insertion). Propanone → N-methylethanamide (CH3NHCOCH3): the two groups on C=O are identical, so the product is the same whichever migrates.
Practice 137 Main

(E)-Acetophenone oxime (OH anti to phenyl) and (Z)-acetophenone oxime are separately treated with PCl5. Give the two products.

Show answer
  1. The group anti to OH migrates. E (OH anti to Ph): Ph migrates: N-phenylethanamide (acetanilide). Z (OH anti to CH3): CH3 migrates: N-methylbenzamide.
Practice 138 Main

Salicylaldehyde + H2O2/NaOH. Give the product and name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Catechol (benzene-1,2-diol), Dakin oxidation: the electron-rich aryl migrates to oxygen giving the formate that is hydrolysed.
Practice 139 Main

2-Methylcyclohexanone + mCPBA. Give the lactone and justify the regiochemistry.

Show answer
  1. Migration of the more substituted C-2 (secondary alkyl > primary): oxygen is inserted between C=O and C-2: 7-methyloxepan-2-one.
