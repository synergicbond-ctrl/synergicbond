Level 3 · F. Build-up problems

Start from a named compound, replace the stated hydrogens by the stated groups, then name the final compound: parent-chain and numbering rules apply to the final structure, not to the starting material.

Q204 Integer

Begin with prop-2-en-1-ol, CH₂=CH–CH₂OH. Replace one hydrogen on C1 by an isopropyl group. In the final compound let H = locant of OH, D = locant of C=C, M = locant of the methyl branch. Find 100H + 10D + M.

Q205 Integer

Begin with but-1-yne, HC≡C–CH₂–CH₃. Replace both hydrogens on C3 (the CH₂) by a methyl group and a hydroxyl group. In the final compound find the sum of all printed locants and add the number of carbon atoms in the parent chain.
