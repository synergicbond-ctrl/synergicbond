27. The benzoin condensation

Two molecules of an aromatic aldehyde, heated with a catalytic amount of aqueous-alcoholic KCN or NaCN, give an α-hydroxy ketone (benzoin, from the Latin name of gum benzoin).

+ KCN (cat.), EtOH/H₂O, Δ⟶
Benzoin: 2-hydroxy-1,2-diphenylethanone

Mechanism (an example of umpolung, reversal of polarity):

  • Step 1: CN− adds to the C=O of PhCHO to give the cyanohydrin alkoxide.
  • Step 2: proton transfer from the former aldehyde C–H (now acidic: it is α to CN and to phenyl, and the carbanion is stabilised by the nitrile) gives the carbanion PhC−(OH)CN, an acyl anion equivalent: carbon that was electrophilic is now nucleophilic.
  • Step 3: this carbanion attacks the C=O of a second PhCHO, giving the alkoxide.
  • Step 4: proton transfer, then loss of CN− from the cyanohydrin carbon regenerates the catalyst and forms the C=O of benzoin.
CN⁻ (add)⟶ proton shift to give carbanion⟶
Cyanide adds to benzaldehyde; loss of the aldehyde C–H proton gives a nitrile-stabilised carbanion, an acyl anion equivalent
  • Works for aromatic aldehydes (benzaldehyde, furfural). Aliphatic aldehydes with α-H undergo aldol under these basic conditions instead.
  • p-Dimethylaminobenzaldehyde does not give benzoin itself (its carbonyl is too deactivated for the second attack) but acts as the carbanion source in a crossed benzoin with another aldehyde.
  • Thiazolium salts (vitamin B1, thiamine) and N-heterocyclic carbenes catalyse benzoin and Stetter reactions; this is the biochemical role of thiamine in decarboxylation of pyruvate.

Reactions of benzoin. It is an α-hydroxy ketone, so it reduces Tollens' and Fehling's solutions (the CH(OH)CO unit is oxidised, like the one in fructose). It is oxidised by HNO3 or Cu2+ to benzil (PhCOCOPh), which gives benzilic acid with KOH.

HNO₃ or Cu²⁺/NH₄⁺⟶ (i) KOH (ii) H⁺⟶
Benzoin → benzil → benzilic acid
Trap Do not confuse: benzoin (C14H12O2, α-hydroxyketone) from PhCHO + CN−; benzil (C14H10O2, diketone) by oxidation of benzoin; benzilic acid by rearrangement of benzil. The benzoin condensation needs cyanide as a catalyst; the Cannizzaro needs concentrated hydroxide.
