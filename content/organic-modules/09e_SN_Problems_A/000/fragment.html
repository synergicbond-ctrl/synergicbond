Further problems: mechanism, kinetics and stereochemistry (part A)

These additional problems extend the chapter on substitution at saturated carbon. They concentrate on bimolecular displacement and its stereochemical consequences, the effect of substrate structure, nucleophile, leaving group and solvent, ambident nucleophiles and catalysis by iodide, crown ethers and phase-transfer catalysts, and on the reactions of ethers, epoxides and alcohols that rely on the same principles. The unimolecular pathway appears through its kinetics and through the stereochemistry of ion pairs.

Work each question before opening the solution. Every solution is written as a short worked argument: identify the mechanism first, then the factors that control the rate, and finally the stereochemical outcome. Problems labelled 'Worked example' have fuller solutions, often ending in a short take-away line; for stereochemical problems keep track of the spatial change and of the CIP descriptor separately, because inversion of geometry does not always reverse the label.
