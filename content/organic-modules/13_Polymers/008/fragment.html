Classify

Classify (a) polystyrene, (b) Bakelite, (c) Buna-N, (d) PHBV by mode (addition/condensation), structure and physical type.

Show solution
  1. (a) Polystyrene: addition, linear (atactic, amorphous), thermoplastic.
  2. (b) Bakelite: condensation of phenol + HCHO, cross-linked network, thermosetting.
  3. (c) Buna-N: addition copolymer of buta-1,3-diene + acrylonitrile, elastomer (becomes network on vulcanisation).
  4. (d) PHBV: condensation copolyester of 3-hydroxybutanoic and 3-hydroxypentanoic acids (a polyhydroxyalkanoate), linear, thermoplastic, biodegradable.
