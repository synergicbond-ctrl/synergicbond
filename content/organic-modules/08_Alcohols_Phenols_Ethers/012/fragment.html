3.6 Summary: ways to make an alcohol of a given type

To makeBest routes
Primary alcoholRCH2Br + OH− (SN2); hydroboration–oxidation of terminal alkene; LiAlH4 on RCOOH/RCOOR/RCHO; HCHO + RMgX; epoxide + RMgX (adds two C)
Secondary alcoholNaBH4 on ketone; RCHO + R′MgX; HCOOR + 2R′MgX; Hg(OAc)2/NaBH4 on a terminal or internal alkene; acid hydration (with care)
Tertiary alcoholketone + RMgX; ester + 2RMgX; Hg(OAc)2/NaBH4 (Markovnikov) on 1,1-disubstituted alkene; acid hydration of isobutene-type alkenes
Shortcut When a question gives only the target alcohol and says “using a Grignard reagent”, write the carbinol carbon and cut one C–C bond at a time; each cut gives a valid disconnection. Use the symmetric ones (ester + 2RMgX) when two of the three groups are the same.
