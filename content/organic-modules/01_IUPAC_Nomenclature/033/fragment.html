4.4 Aldehydes and ketones

  • Aldehyde: –CHO carbon is C-1 and is part of the chain; suffix -al, no locant. Dialdehydes: -dial (butanedial). On a ring or other fixed skeleton the suffix is -carbaldehyde and the CHO carbon is not counted in the ring.
  • Ketone: suffix -one with locant; lowest locant to C=O. Ring ketones: cyclohexanone (C=O is C-1).
  • When aldehyde and ketone are both present the aldehyde is the suffix and the ketone becomes oxo: 3-oxobutanal.
