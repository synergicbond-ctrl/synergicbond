# F-block chapter verification report

## Scope and source audit

The chapter scope was audited against the available F-block reference PDFs (`f-Block_Decoded.pdf`, `f-Block_Advanced_Mastery.pdf`, `F block.pdf`, `f block SG.pdf`, and the available NCERT F-block PDF/text extraction). The PDFs were used only to identify coverage requirements and teaching relationships. No source artwork, composition, page design, mnemonic, or solved-question wording was copied.

The implemented four-part chapter covers periodic placement; neutral-atom and ionic configurations; occurrence; lanthanoid oxidation states, contraction, colour, magnetism, compounds and reactivity; actinoid oxidation states, actinyl ions, contraction, bonding, hydrolysis, radioactivity and applications; separation; comparison tables; solved examples; exam traps; and an original mixed practice set.

## Scientific checks

- **Element counts:** every displayed lanthanoid/actinoid row contains fourteen filling members. La/Ac versus Lu/Lr placement is stated as a convention rather than disguised as a chemical disagreement.
- **Configurations:** neutral-atom anchor configurations were checked separately from ion configurations. The modern `[Rn] 5f¹⁴ 7s² 7p¹` lawrencium ground state is labelled; the older school-table 6d¹ convention is explicitly noted.
- **Oxidation states:** +3 is shown as dominant for lanthanoids; selected +2/+4 states are presented as medium-dependent and not as consequences of configuration alone. The early-to-late actinoid trend is stated without implying every listed state is equally stable in water.
- **Colour:** f⁰/f¹⁴ ions are described as normally lacking f–f colour, not universally colourless. Charge-transfer and other chromophore exceptions are retained.
- **Magnetism:** the Ln³⁺ unpaired-electron sequence 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 was checked. Quantitative lanthanoid moments use the free-ion L–S–J expression rather than a universal spin-only shortcut.
- **Contraction:** the causal chain uses poor 4f shielding → increasing effective nuclear charge → decreasing size. Consequences are separated from the cause.
- **Structures:** actinyl units are shown as approximately linear O–An–O fragments with equatorial ligand coordination. Oxidation-state arithmetic is included in the notes.
- **Equations:** displayed reactions were checked for atom and charge balance. Ceria oxygen storage is presented as a reversible idealised stoichiometric relationship, and the chapter warns against assuming universal products for hydrides, carbides or mixed-valence oxides.
- **Applications:** Nd–Fe–B and Sm–Co magnets, Eu/Tb phosphors, CeO₂ catalysis/polishing, Nd:YAG, LaNi₅ storage, mischmetal and uranium/plutonium energy uses are connected to the relevant chemical property. No generic MRI claim is used.

## Convention notes and limitations

Colour shades of real ions vary with oxidation state, ligand, solvent, concentration and illumination; the chapter therefore teaches spectral mechanisms instead of unreliable universal swatches. Actinoid chemistry is especially medium-dependent, so the oxidation-state matrix is a JEE-level trend map, not a complete speciation diagram. Numerical radii are not invented where a single coordination-number convention is not specified.
