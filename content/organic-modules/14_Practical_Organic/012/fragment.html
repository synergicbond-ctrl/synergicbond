3.4 Distillation under reduced pressure (vacuum distillation)

A liquid boils when its vapour pressure equals the external pressure. At a lowered pressure it boils at a lower temperature. This is used for liquids that decompose at or below their normal boiling point. Standard example: glycerol decomposes at its normal b.p. (563 K); under reduced pressure it distils unchanged at a much lower temperature. A vacuum pump or water aspirator is connected to the receiver. Other examples: high-boiling esters, vitamin extracts, and the concentration of sugar juice (it caramelises at high temperature).
