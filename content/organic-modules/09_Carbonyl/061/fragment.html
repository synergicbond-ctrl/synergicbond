52. Retrosynthesis of enones and β-hydroxycarbonyls (aldol disconnection)

To plan an aldol-type synthesis, work backwards:

  • Step 1: for a β-hydroxy carbonyl compound, disconnect the bond between the α-carbon and the carbinol (β) carbon: the carbonyl-containing piece (with the α-carbon) is the donor, the carbinol piece (with O) is the acceptor carbonyl compound.
  • Step 2: for an α,β-unsaturated carbonyl compound, add H2O across C=C (restore the β-OH), then disconnect as above. Equivalently, break the C=C between Cα and Cβ and put =O on Cβ.
  • Step 3: choose an acceptor that cannot enolise (ArCHO, HCHO) where possible; otherwise use a preformed enolate (LDA) or an intramolecular version.
