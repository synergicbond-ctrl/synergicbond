36. Hunsdiecker reaction (decarboxylative bromination)

Reagents: the dry silver salt of a carboxylic acid RCOOAg + Br2 in CCl4, reflux → R–Br + CO2 + AgBr. The product has one carbon fewer than the acid. Mechanism (radical chain): RCOOAg + Br2 → RCOOBr (acyl hypobromite) + AgBr; RCOOBr homolyses to RCOO• + Br•; RCOO• → R• + CO2; R• + RCOOBr → R–Br + RCOO• (propagation).

+ CCl₄, Δ⟶ + +
Silver butanoate → 1-bromopropane (C4 → C3)
Trap The salt must be perfectly dry. Yield order: primary R > secondary R > tertiary R (poor). With aryl carboxylates the Hunsdiecker reaction is weak. Silver salts of dicarboxylic acids (half-esters) give ω-bromoesters.
