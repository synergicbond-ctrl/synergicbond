Five short questions on prerequisites. Answers follow each question.

Concept Check C1 Checkpoint

Radical stability

Which is the more stable radical, CH3CH2CH2• or C6H5CH2•, and why?

Show answer

The benzyl radical. The unpaired electron is delocalised over the ring (resonance), spread to the ortho and para carbons; the propyl radical is an unstabilised primary radical.

Concept Check C2 Checkpoint

Carbocation stability

Arrange CH3+, (CH3)2CH+, (CH3)3C+ in decreasing stability.

Show answer

(CH3)3C+ > (CH3)2CH+ > CH3+: more alkyl groups donate by induction and hyperconjugation.

Concept Check C3 Checkpoint

Acyl substitution

Which reacts faster with an amine at room temperature, CH3COCl or CH3COOCH3, and why?

Show answer

CH3COCl. Cl withdraws electron density (a stronger electrophilic carbonyl) and Cl− is a far better leaving group than CH3O−.

Concept Check C4 Checkpoint

Geometry

In but-2-ene, which isomer has the two CH3 groups on the same side of the double bond, and what is its CIP label?

Show answer

cis-But-2-ene, labelled Z.

Concept Check C5 Checkpoint

Mole concept

How many moles are in 56 g of ethene (M = 28)?

Show answer

56/28 = 2.0 mol.
