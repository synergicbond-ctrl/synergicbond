30. Etard reaction (methylarene → aldehyde)

Reagents: ArCH3 + chromyl chloride CrO2Cl2 in CS2 or CCl4, then aqueous work-up (H3O+ with a reducing step, e.g. Zn/H2O or NaHSO3). Product: ArCHO, with no over-oxidation to ArCOOH because the aldehyde is protected as the insoluble brown Etard complex ArCH(OCrCl2OH)2, which is hydrolysed only at the end.

1. CrO₂Cl₂, CS₂ 2. H₃O⁺⟶
Toluene → benzaldehyde
Shortcut Choose the aldehyde route by what the oxidant is: CrO2Cl2 (Etard) or CrO3/(CH3CO)2O (Étard-like, gives the acylal) stops at ArCHO; KMnO4 or K2Cr2O7/H+ gives ArCOOH (benzoic acid) from the same toluene. Benzylic oxidation by KMnO4 needs at least one benzylic H (t-butylbenzene is inert).
