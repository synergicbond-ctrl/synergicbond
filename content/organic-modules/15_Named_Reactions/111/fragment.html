Practice 328 A–R

Assertion: Ethanal undergoes aldol condensation with dilute NaOH. Reason: ethanal has no α-hydrogen.

Show answer
  1. (C). The assertion is true, but ethanal has three α-hydrogens (the reason is false).
Practice 329 A–R

Assertion: Na/NH3(l) converts a terminal alkyne RC≡CH into the trans-alkene. Reason: Na/NH3(l) reduces internal alkynes to trans-alkenes.

Show answer
  1. (D). The reason is true for internal alkynes, but a terminal alkyne is deprotonated to the acetylide RC≡C−, which is not reduced, so the assertion is false.
Practice 330 A–R

Assertion: Phenol undergoes the Gattermann–Koch formylation (CO, HCl, AlCl3) readily. Reason: AlCl3 complexes with the phenolic oxygen.

Show answer
  1. (D). The assertion is false (phenol is not formylated by Gattermann–Koch); the reason (complexation with AlCl3) is true and is why.
