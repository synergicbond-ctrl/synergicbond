Concept Check

1. What is the hybridisation of the carbon in –C≡N, and how many carbon atoms does CH₃CH₂CH(CH₃)CH₂OH contain?

Show answer

sp; five carbons (four in the chain plus one methyl). Revisit §1.6 if either was missed.

Concept Check

2. Write the molecular formula of a saturated acyclic alcohol with four carbons.

Show answer

C₄H₁₀O (CnH2n+2O). See §1.6.

Concept Check

3. Name the functional groups in CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CHO.

Show answer

A hydroxyl group (alcohol) and an aldehyde group. Which one outranks the other is taught in §4.1.

Concept Check

4. How many constitutional isomers does C₄H₁₀ have?

Show answer

Two: butane and 2-methylpropane (§2.5).

Concept Check

5. Are CH₃CH₂CH₂OH and CH₃CH(OH)CH₃ chain, position or functional isomers?

Show answer

Position isomers (the OH is on a different carbon of the same chain). Locants (§2.2) exist to distinguish exactly this.
