Block A. Name the reaction

Practice 1 Name

Benzaldehyde is heated with ethanoic anhydride and sodium ethanoate. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Perkin reaction: the anhydride enolate adds to PhCHO; elimination and hydrolysis give E-cinnamic acid (PhCH=CHCOOH).
Practice 2 Name

PhN2+Cl− is warmed with Cu2Cl2/HCl. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Sandmeyer reaction: chlorobenzene, with loss of N2.
Practice 3 Name

CH3CH2COOH + Br2 with a little red phosphorus gives CH3CHBrCOOH. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky α-bromination. P forms PBr3, which makes the acyl bromide; its enol is brominated at the α-carbon.
Practice 4 Name

Sodium metal in dry ether converts 2 CH3CH2Br into butane. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Wurtz reaction (even-carbon symmetrical alkane; C2 + C2 = C4).
Practice 5 Name

Phenol + CHCl3 + aqueous NaOH, then H+, gives salicylaldehyde. Name the reaction and the reactive intermediate.

Show answer
  1. Reimer–Tiemann reaction; the electrophile is dichlorocarbene, :CCl2 (from CHCl3 + OH−).
Practice 6 Name

Sodium phenoxide + CO2 at 400 K and 4–7 atm, then H+, gives salicylic acid. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Kolbe–Schmitt reaction (carboxylation of the phenoxide at the ortho position).
Practice 7 Name

CH3CH2Cl + NaI in dry acetone gives CH3CH2I. Name the reaction and say what drives it.

Show answer
  1. Finkelstein reaction; NaCl is insoluble in acetone and precipitates, pulling the equilibrium to the right.
Practice 8 Name

CH3CH2Br + AgF gives CH3CH2F. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Swarts reaction (halogen exchange to give an alkyl fluoride; also SbF3, Hg2F2).
Practice 9 Name

C6H5COCl is hydrogenated over Pd/BaSO4 (poisoned with quinoline). Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Rosenmund reduction; product benzaldehyde. Poisoning prevents reduction of the aldehyde to benzyl alcohol.
Practice 10 Name

C6H5CN is treated with SnCl2/HCl and then water. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Stephen reaction; benzaldehyde (via the aldimine tin chloride).
Practice 11 Name

Acetophenone is heated with zinc amalgam and conc. HCl. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Clemmensen reduction; ethylbenzene.
Practice 12 Name

Acetophenone is heated with hydrazine and KOH in ethylene glycol at 473 K. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Wolff–Kishner (Huang–Minlon) reduction; ethylbenzene + N2.
Practice 13 Name

Aniline is shaken with benzoyl chloride and aqueous NaOH. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Schotten–Baumann reaction; benzanilide (N-phenylbenzamide).
Practice 14 Name

Benzene + CH3COCl with AlCl3. Name the reaction and the electrophile.

Show answer
  1. Friedel–Crafts acylation; electrophile is the acylium ion, CH3–C≡O+. Product acetophenone.
Practice 15 Name

Acetamide with Br2/NaOH gives CH3NH2. Name the reaction and the key intermediate.

Show answer
  1. Hofmann bromamide reaction; the intermediate is methyl isocyanate, CH3N=C=O.
Practice 16 Name

Cyclohexanone oxime is heated with conc. H2SO4. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Beckmann rearrangement; ε-caprolactam, the monomer of nylon-6.
Practice 17 Name

2,3-Dimethylbutane-2,3-diol with dilute H2SO4 and heat gives (CH3)3CCOCH3. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Pinacol–pinacolone rearrangement (carbocation 1,2-methyl shift).
Practice 18 Name

Benzil + KOH, then H3O+, gives (C6H5)2C(OH)COOH. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Benzilic acid rearrangement (1,2-aryl shift).
Practice 19 Name

Allyl phenyl ether heated at about 470 K gives 2-allylphenol. Name the reaction and its type.

Show answer
  1. Claisen rearrangement, a [3,3]-sigmatropic shift.
Practice 20 Name

Hydrazobenzene + dilute H2SO4 gives 4,4′-diaminobiphenyl as the major product. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Benzidine rearrangement ([5,5]-sigmatropic, intramolecular); the minor product is diphenyline.
Practice 21 Name

Diethyl hexanedioate (diethyl adipate) is treated with NaOEt. Name the reaction and the product.

Show answer
  1. Dieckmann condensation; ethyl 2-oxocyclopentanecarboxylate.
Practice 22 Name

Ethyl ethanoate + NaOEt, then H+, gives ethyl 3-oxobutanoate. Name the reaction.

Show answer
  1. Claisen (ester) condensation; the driving force is deprotonation of the product (pKa ≈ 11) by ethoxide.
Practice 23 Name

2,2-Dimethylpropanal is treated with concentrated NaOH. Name the reaction and the two organic products.

Show answer
  1. Cannizzaro reaction: (CH3)3CCH2OH (2,2-dimethylpropan-1-ol) and (CH3)3CCOO−Na+ (acid after acidification). No α-H, so no aldol.
Practice 24 Name

Benzaldehyde with a catalytic amount of KCN in aqueous ethanol gives benzoin. Name the reaction and the role of cyanide.

Show answer
  1. Benzoin condensation; CN− forms the cyanohydrin whose carbanion is an acyl-anion equivalent (umpolung) that attacks the second PhCHO.
Practice 25 Name

Cyclohexanone + Ph3P=CH2 gives methylenecyclohexane. Name the reaction and the by-product.

Show answer
  1. Wittig reaction; by-product triphenylphosphine oxide, Ph3P=O, whose P=O bond formation drives the reaction.
