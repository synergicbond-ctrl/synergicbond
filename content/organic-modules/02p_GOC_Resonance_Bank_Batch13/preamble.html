[Akshay Chaudhary topic, master-sheet page 22] Which base has the most acidic conjugate acid? (i) NH₃ (pKb 4.74) (ii) CH₃CH₂NH₂ (pKb 3.19) (iii) aniline (pKb 9.37) (iv) p-nitroaniline (pKb 13.0)

Full solution

  2. Largest pKb = 13.0 for p-nitroaniline, option (iv).
