9.8 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Give the systematic names of acetone, acetaldehyde and acetic acid.

Show answer

Propan-2-one, ethanal and ethanoic acid.

Concept Check

Which two trivial names correspond to but-2-enedioic acid, and how do they differ?

Show answer

Maleic acid (Z, cis) and fumaric acid (E, trans).

Concept Check

What is the difference between a benzyl and a benzoyl group?

Show answer

Benzyl is C₆H₅CH₂–; benzoyl is C₆H₅CO–, which contains a carbonyl group.
