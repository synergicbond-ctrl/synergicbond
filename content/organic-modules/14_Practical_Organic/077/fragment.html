G. MOT Lens and Bonding Insights

  • §7.5 The AgX solubility series is the reverse of the ionic-radius series because AgI is the most covalent salt: the large, highly polarisable I− is polarised most by Ag+ (Fajans' rules), giving the lowest Ksp. The simplified ionic model does not predict this; the Ksp data do. follows the order of Ksp: 1.8 × 10-10, 5 × 10-13, 8.5 × 10-17. The overall equilibrium AgX + 2NH3 ⇌ [Ag(NH3)2]+ + X− has K = KspKf with Kf ≈ 107, so it is only large enough for AgCl (see Example below).
  • §21 The orbital reasons behind the carbonyl shifts. (1) *Conjugation:* the C=O π orbital overlaps with the π system of a C=C or ring, forming delocalised π molecular orbitals; the resonance picture C=C–C=O ↔ ⁺C–C=C–O− shows that the carbonyl π bond order falls below 2, the force constant k decreases, and ν̃ falls. (2) *Amides:* the nitrogen lone pair (nN) donates into π*(C=O); the C–N bond gains double-bond character and C=O loses it, so amide C=O is the lowest. (3) *Acid chlorides:* chlorine is strongly σ-withdrawing but its 3p lone pair overlaps poorly with the carbon 2p, so little donation occurs and C=O stays strong and fast. (4) *Ring strain:* in a small ring the internal C–C–C angle is compressed, so the ring bonds use more p character, leaving more s character in the carbon orbital that bonds the exocyclic oxygen: a stronger, stiffer C=O and a higher ν̃ (cyclobutanone 1780 > cyclohexanone 1715).
  • §22.2 Why ring protons are at δ 7 (orbital picture). In benzene the six π electrons occupy delocalised molecular orbitals. In the applied field B0 they circulate around the ring (a *ring current*); the induced field opposes B0 inside the ring but reinforces it outside, at the position of the ring hydrogens, so these protons feel a larger effective field and are deshielded (δ ≈ 7 rather than 5-6 for an isolated alkene). The same anisotropy argument, together with the electronegative oxygen, places the aldehyde H at δ 9-10. Aromatic stabilisation (4n + 2 π electrons) is thus visible in NMR: [18]annulene inner protons even appear at negative δ.
