22. Graded exercises: Foundation (single-correct and short answer)

Each question has a full explanation. Try before opening it. Where an MCQ is given, options are (A)–(D).

Q1 Foundation

Monomer of PVC

The monomer of polyvinyl chloride is: (A) CH2=CCl2 (B) CH2=CHCl (C) CHCl=CHCl (D) CH3CH2Cl

Show answer

(B). PVC = –[CH2–CHCl]–n from vinyl chloride, CH2=CHCl. (A) is vinylidene chloride (Saran). (D) is saturated and has no π bond to open.

Q2 Foundation

LDPE or HDPE

Match each condition with the polyethene it gives: (i) 350–570 K, 1000–2000 atm, peroxide initiator; (ii) 333–343 K, 6–7 atm, TiCl4 + Al(C2H5)3.

Show answer

(i) LDPE (free-radical, branched, density 0.91–0.93). (ii) HDPE (Ziegler–Natta, linear, density 0.94–0.97). Free-radical chain-end back-biting and transfer to polymer make the branches in (i); coordination polymerisation has no radicals, so no branches in (ii).

Q3 Foundation

Catalyst

The catalyst used to make isotactic polypropene is: (A) benzoyl peroxide (B) TiCl4 + (C2H5)3Al (C) H2SO4 (D) AlCl3

Show answer

(B). Only a stereoregulating Ziegler–Natta (coordination) catalyst gives isotactic PP. Peroxides give atactic polymer; H2SO4 and AlCl3 are cationic initiators suited to isobutene.

Q4 Foundation

Teflon

Teflon is made from (A) CF3–CF3 (B) CF2=CF2 (C) CH2=CF2 (D) CF2=CFCl. What initiator is used?

Show answer

(B), tetrafluoroethene, with a persulphate (free-radical) initiator under high pressure. (C) gives PVDF; (D) gives PCTFE (Kel-F).

Q5 Foundation

Orlon

Orlon (Acrilan) is a polymer of (A) styrene (B) acrylonitrile (C) acrylic acid (D) vinyl acetate

Show answer

(B), CH2=CHCN. Orlon is polyacrylonitrile, used as artificial wool.

Q6 Foundation

Perspex

Perspex (organic glass) is a polymer of (A) methyl acrylate (B) methyl methacrylate (C) vinyl acetate (D) styrene

Show answer

(B). PMMA, –[CH2–C(CH3)(COOCH3)]–.

Q7 Foundation

Buna-S

Buna-S is a copolymer of (A) butadiene and styrene (B) butadiene and acrylonitrile (C) isoprene and styrene (D) chloroprene and styrene

Show answer

(A). “Bu” = butadiene, “na” = sodium catalyst (original), “S” = styrene. Buna-N has acrylonitrile (nitrile).

Q8 Foundation

Neoprene

The monomer of neoprene is (A) isoprene (B) 2-chlorobuta-1,3-diene (C) vinyl chloride (D) buta-1,3-diene

Show answer

(B), chloroprene. Same carbon skeleton as isoprene but Cl instead of CH3 at C-2.

Q9 Foundation

Natural rubber

Natural rubber is a polymer of (A) cis-isoprene (B) trans-isoprene (C) butadiene (D) chloroprene. Which isomer is gutta-percha?

Show answer

(A): cis-1,4-polyisoprene. Gutta-percha is the trans isomer, which is hard and inelastic because the straight chains pack tightly.

Q10 Foundation

Vulcanisation

Vulcanisation of rubber involves heating raw rubber with (A) H2SO4 (B) sulphur (C) carbon (D) chlorine. State the effect.

Show answer

(B), sulphur (3–5 %). S–S bridges between chains give a harder, stronger, non-sticky, more elastic rubber that resists solvents.

Q11 Foundation

Nylon-6,6

Monomers of nylon-6,6 are (A) adipic acid + hexamethylenediamine (B) caprolactam (C) terephthalic acid + ethylene glycol (D) phenol + HCHO

Show answer

(A) (hexanedioic acid + hexane-1,6-diamine). (B) is nylon-6; (C) is terylene; (D) is Bakelite.

Q12 Foundation

Nylon-6

Nylon-6 is made from caprolactam. Which reaction makes caprolactam from cyclohexanone oxime?

Show answer

Beckmann rearrangement (conc. H2SO4): the group anti to the OH migrates from C to N, expanding the six-membered ring to the seven-membered lactam, ε-caprolactam. The ring is opened with a little water at 533 K and polymerised.

Q13 Foundation

Terylene

Terylene is a polymer of (A) ethylene glycol + terephthalic acid (B) ethylene glycol + phthalic acid (C) glycerol + phthalic acid (D) adipic acid + hexamethylenediamine

Show answer

(A). The linkage is –COO– (ester). (B) is glyptal; (C) is cross-linked alkyd glyptal.

Q14 Foundation

Bakelite

Bakelite is made from (A) urea + HCHO (B) melamine + HCHO (C) phenol + HCHO (D) phenol + acetaldehyde

Show answer

(C). Phenol + HCHO → (resol or novolac) → heat → cross-linked Bakelite.

Q15 Foundation

Thermosetting

Which is thermosetting? (A) polythene (B) PVC (C) Bakelite (D) polystyrene

Show answer

(C). Cross-linked, infusible after curing. (A), (B), (D) are thermoplastics.

Q16 Foundation

Fibre

Which polymer is a fibre? (A) PVC (B) nylon-6,6 (C) Buna-S (D) Bakelite

Show answer

(B): amide groups give strong N–H···O=C hydrogen bonds and crystallinity. PVC is a plastic, Buna-S an elastomer, Bakelite a thermoset.

Q17 Foundation

Elastomer

Which pair are both elastomers? (A) neoprene, Buna-S (B) nylon, terylene (C) PVC, PTFE (D) Bakelite, melamine resin

Show answer

(A). Elastomers have weak intermolecular forces and coiled chains; (B) fibres, (C) thermoplastics, (D) thermosets.

Q18 Foundation

Condensation polymers

Which of these are condensation polymers: nylon-6,6, polythene, terylene, PVC, Bakelite, Teflon?

Show answer

Nylon-6,6, terylene, Bakelite (three). Polythene, PVC and Teflon are addition polymers.

Q19 Foundation

Addition polymers

Choose the addition polymers: (i) Buna-N, (ii) glyptal, (iii) neoprene, (iv) nylon-6,6, (v) PMMA

Show answer

(i), (iii), (v). Glyptal (polyester) and nylon-6,6 (polyamide) are step-growth condensation polymers.

Q20 Foundation

Biodegradable polymer

Which is a biodegradable polymer? (A) PVC (B) PHBV (C) polythene (D) Teflon

Show answer

(B), because its ester links are hydrolysed by enzymes. The others have C–C or C–F backbones.

Q21 Foundation

Nylon-2-nylon-6

Name the monomers of nylon-2-nylon-6.

Show answer

Glycine (H2NCH2COOH, the “2”) and 6-aminohexanoic acid (H2N(CH2)5COOH, the “6”), alternating through amide bonds. Biodegradable.

Q22 Foundation

PHBV

Give the monomers of PHBV and the type of link.

Show answer

3-Hydroxybutanoic acid and 3-hydroxypentanoic acid; ester (polyester).

Q23 Foundation

Polymer with C–F bonds

Which polymer has the highest chemical resistance and a low coefficient of friction? Why?

Show answer

PTFE. The C–F bond is very strong (≈ 485 kJ mol-1), the F atoms form a tight non-polarisable sheath around the carbon chain, so acids, bases, solvents and other chemicals do not attack it and little sticks to its surface.

Q24 Foundation

DP of polythene

Polythene of average molar mass 28 000 g mol-1: what is DP?

Show answer

Repeat unit –CH2CH2– = 28. DP = 28 000/28 = 1000.

Q25 Foundation

Water molecules

How many water molecules are lost in forming a nylon-6,6 molecule containing 50 repeat units (i.e. 100 monomer molecules)?

Show answer

100 monomers → 99 links → 99 H2O.

Q26 Foundation

Polyvinyl alcohol

Why is PVA not made from vinyl alcohol? How is it made?

Show answer

Vinyl alcohol (an enol) tautomerises immediately to acetaldehyde, so it cannot be isolated. PVA is made by polymerising vinyl acetate and then hydrolysing the ester with NaOH or acid (or methanolysis).

Q27 Foundation

Cross-linked polymers

Name two cross-linked polymers and one branched polymer.

Show answer

Cross-linked: Bakelite, melamine–formaldehyde (also vulcanised rubber). Branched: LDPE (also amylopectin, glycogen).

Q28 Foundation

Plasticiser

What is the function of dioctyl phthalate in PVC?

Show answer

A plasticiser: small molecules that sit between chains, weaken chain–chain forces, lower Tg and so make rigid PVC flexible.

Q29 Foundation

H-bonding

Which polymer shows intermolecular hydrogen bonding? (A) polythene (B) PVC (C) nylon-6 (D) Teflon

Show answer

(C): N–H···O=C between amide groups.

Q30 Foundation

Kevlar

Kevlar is an aromatic polyamide. Name its monomers and one use.

Show answer

Terephthalic acid (or its chloride) and p-phenylenediamine. Bullet-proof vests, helmets, tyre cord.

Q31 Foundation

Glyptal

Name the monomers of glyptal and the use.

Show answer

Ethylene glycol and phthalic acid (the cross-linked form from glycerol and phthalic anhydride). Paints, lacquers and adhesives.

Q32 Foundation

Melamine resin

Which two monomers give melamine–formaldehyde resin? Name a use.

Show answer

Melamine and methanal (formaldehyde). Unbreakable crockery, laminates.

Q33 Foundation

Expanded polystyrene

How is thermocol (expanded polystyrene) made?

Show answer

Polystyrene beads containing a volatile blowing agent (pentane) are heated with steam; the vapour expands the softened polymer into a closed-cell foam.

Q34 Foundation

Radical initiator

Which can serve as a free-radical initiator? (A) benzoyl peroxide (B) AlCl3 (C) n-BuLi (D) H2SO4

Show answer

(A): the weak O–O bond cleaves on heating to give two C6H5COO•. The others are cationic (B, D) or anionic (C) initiators.

Q35 Foundation

Carothers

In a step-growth polymerisation, the extent of reaction is 0.95. Find Xn.

Show answer

Xn = 1/(1 − 0.95) = 20.

Q36 Foundation

Homo or co

Classify as homopolymer or copolymer (treat a condensation polymer made from two different monomers as a copolymer): PVC, Buna-S, nylon-6, nylon-6,6, PHBV, PTFE.

Show answer

Homopolymers: PVC, nylon-6, PTFE. Copolymers: Buna-S, PHBV and nylon-6,6 (two different monomers, hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid).

Q37 Foundation

Thermoplastic or thermoset

Classify polythene, Bakelite, PVC, urea–formaldehyde, polystyrene, nylon-6.

Show answer

Thermoplastics: polythene, PVC, polystyrene, nylon-6. Thermosets: Bakelite, urea–formaldehyde.

Q38 Foundation

PVC repeat unit

Which is the repeat unit of PVC? (A) –CHCl–CHCl– (B) –CH2–CHCl– (C) –CH=CCl– (D) –CCl2–CH2–

Show answer

(B). Addition polymerisation consumes the C=C; no double bond remains, and the head-to-tail structure puts Cl on alternate carbons.

Q39 Foundation

PDI of a monodisperse sample

What is PDI of a polymer in which every chain has the same mass? Name two natural examples.

Show answer

PDI = Mw/Mn = 1. Proteins and nucleic acids (templated, identical chains).

Q40 Foundation

Mn and Mw

For a polydisperse sample which is bigger, Mn or Mw? Which is measured by osmotic pressure?

Show answer

Mw > Mn. Osmotic pressure is a colligative property (counts molecules) and gives Mn.

Q41 Foundation

Elastomer behaviour

Why can rubber be stretched and return?

Show answer

Chains are random coils held by weak van der Waals forces; stretching uncoils them (entropy falls), and when released they return to the more probable coiled state. cis-Configuration (kinks) and light cross-linking prevent permanent slipping.

Q42 Foundation

Repeat unit from the monomer

Write the repeat unit of the polymer from CH2=C(CH3)COOCH3.

Show answer

–[CH2–C(CH3)(COOCH3)]–n: PMMA.

Q43 Foundation

Teflon preparation

How is TFE made industrially and polymerised?

Show answer

CHClF2 (Freon-22) pyrolysed at about 700 °C gives CF2=CF2 + HCl. TFE is polymerised in water with a persulphate initiator under pressure to PTFE.

Q44 Foundation

Linear or branched

Why is HDPE denser than LDPE?

Show answer

HDPE chains are linear and pack in crystallites (≈ 80–90 % crystalline); LDPE’s short and long branches (from back-biting and chain transfer) block close packing.

Q45 Foundation

Natural vs synthetic rubber

Give two disadvantages of raw natural rubber and how each is overcome.

Show answer

(1) Soft and sticky, low strength and elasticity range → vulcanisation with sulphur. (2) Attacked by oils, petrol and oxidising agents (ozone adds across C=C) → use synthetic rubbers such as neoprene or Buna-N, which resist oil.

Q46 Foundation

Identify the polymer

–[NH–(CH2)5–CO]–n: name and monomer.

Show answer

Nylon-6 (polycaprolactam), from caprolactam by ring-opening.

Q47 Foundation

Identify the polymer

–[O–CH2–CH2–O–CO–C6H4–CO]–n (para ring): name, monomers, link.

Show answer

Terylene (PET, Dacron). Ethylene glycol + terephthalic acid. Ester.

Q48 Foundation

Identify the polymer

–[CH2–CH=CH–CH2–CH2–CH(C6H5)]–n

Show answer

Buna-S (SBR) segment: 1,4-butadiene unit + styrene unit. Butadiene and styrene are the monomers.

Q49 Foundation

Which is not a monomer of a condensation polymer

(A) adipic acid (B) hexamethylenediamine (C) ethene (D) ethylene glycol

Show answer

(C). Condensation monomers need two reactive functional groups (acid/amine/alcohol); ethene is a chain-growth monomer.

Q50 Foundation

Natural polymer

Which is a natural polymer? (A) nylon (B) cellulose (C) PVC (D) Teflon

Show answer

(B): cellulose, a β-1,4-linked glucose polymer.

Q51 Foundation

Semi-synthetic polymer

Give two semi-synthetic polymers.

Show answer

Cellulose acetate (acetate rayon) and cellulose nitrate (celluloid / guncotton). Also viscose rayon (regenerated cellulose).

Q52 Foundation

Polymer from natural gas

Ethene → polythene; what is made from propene and from vinyl chloride?

Show answer

Polypropene (isotactic PP) and PVC.

Q53 Foundation

Cationic polymerisation

Which monomer is best polymerised by a cationic mechanism? (A) CH2=CHCN (B) CH2=C(CH3)2 (C) CH2=CHCl (D) CF2=CF2

Show answer

(B): isobutene gives a tertiary cation stabilised by two methyl groups (hyperconjugation and induction). –CN and –F destabilise a cation.

Q54 Foundation

Anionic polymerisation

Which monomer is best polymerised by an anionic mechanism? (A) CH2=C(CH3)2 (B) CH2=CHCN (C) CH2=CH–OCH3 (D) CH2=CH–CH3

Show answer

(B): the –CN group stabilises the carbanion by resonance and induction. The others give destabilised carbanions (alkyl, alkoxy donate electrons).

Q55 Foundation

Chain steps

Name the steps in a radical addition polymerisation in order, and give one termination mode.

Show answer

Initiation → propagation → termination. Termination: coupling of two chain radicals (or disproportionation).

Q56 Foundation

Gutta-percha

Why is gutta-percha not elastic?

Show answer

trans-1,4-Polyisoprene has straight chains that crystallise, hold tightly by van der Waals forces and cannot coil: hard, inelastic.

Q57 Foundation

Isotactic

In isotactic PP where are the CH3 groups?

Show answer

All on the same side of the plane of the zig-zag carbon chain (regular, crystalline, m.p. ≈ 165 °C).

Q58 Foundation

Recycling

Which recycling code is PET and which is HDPE?

Show answer

1 = PET; 2 = HDPE (3 PVC, 4 LDPE, 5 PP, 6 PS, 7 other).

Q59 Foundation

Urea resin use

Name a polymer used as plywood adhesive.

Show answer

Urea–formaldehyde resin (also phenol–formaldehyde resol).

Q60 Foundation

Acetylene to chloroprene

Give the monomer sequence from ethyne to neoprene.

Show answer

2 HC≡CH → CH2=CH–C≡CH (vinylacetylene) → (+ HCl, CuCl) CH2=C(Cl)–CH=CH2 (chloroprene) → (radical emulsion) neoprene.

Q61 Foundation

Repeat-unit mass

Write the formula of the repeat unit of nylon-6,6 and its molar mass.

Show answer

–[NH(CH2)6NHCO(CH2)4CO]–: C12H22N2O2, M = 12×12.011 + 22×1.008 + 2×14.007 + 2×15.999 = 226.3 g mol-1.
