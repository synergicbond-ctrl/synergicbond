F. Dipole moments

Problem 101 [Advanced]

Two group moments of 1.5 D and 1.5 D make an angle of 60° at the centre of a benzene ring (both drawn away from the ring). Find the resultant μ.

Worked solution
  1. μ = √(μ₁² + μ₂² + 2μ₁μ₂ cos θ).
  2. μ = √(2.25 + 2.25 + 2(1.5)(1.5)cos 60°) = √6.750

Answer: μ ≈ 2.60 D.
