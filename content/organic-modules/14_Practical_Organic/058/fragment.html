19.2 Colligative properties

A weighed amount of solute in a known mass of solvent: M = Kf × 1000 × wsolute/(wsolvent × ΔTf) (also for elevation of boiling point with Kb).

Example 39 Adv

Freezing point depression

0.25 g of a non-electrolyte lowers the freezing point of 25.0 g benzene by 0.512 K (Kf = 5.12 K kg mol-1). Find M.

Show solution
  1. M = Kf × w × 1000/(W × ΔTf) = 5.12 × 0.25 × 1000/(25.0 × 0.512) = 100 g mol-1.
