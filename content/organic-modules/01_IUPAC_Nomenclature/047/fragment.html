5.8 Topic checkpoint

Counterexample A longer chain is not always the parent. In citric acid a five-carbon chain through the two terminal COOH groups exists, but the three-carbon chain that carries all three acid groups as suffix groups wins: 2-hydroxypropane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid.
Concept Check

Name OHC–CH₂–CH₂–COOH.

Show answer

4-Oxobutanoic acid: the acid is the principal group, and the aldehyde carbon is in the chain, so it is expressed as oxo.

Concept Check

Why is HOCH₂CH₂CH(OH)CH₃ named butane-1,3-diol and not 3-hydroxybutan-1-ol?

Show answer

Both OH groups can be in the parent chain, and the chain with the maximum number of principal groups is chosen: butane-1,3-diol.

Concept Check

Name CH₃COCH₂COOCH₂CH₃.

Show answer

Ethyl 3-oxobutanoate: the ester is the principal group, and the ketone is an oxo prefix on C-3 of the butanoate chain.

Think Further

Explain why the amino acid H₂N–CH₂–COOH is named 2-aminoethanoic acid and not aminomethanoic acid.

Show solution

The acid carbon is C-1 of an ethanoic acid chain; the CH₂ is C-2 and bears the amino group. Methanoic acid has only one carbon, which is the acid carbon itself, so it cannot carry a substituent other than OH.
