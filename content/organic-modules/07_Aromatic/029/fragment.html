Appendix: Sources cross-checked

This chapter was written mainly from the author's working knowledge of the topic, and every structure, formula, count and number in it was checked by computer (RDKit and Python; script `verify_07.py`). The following sources were actually opened to compare topics, traps and question types. No text or problems were copied: all explanations and all problems are original wording.
