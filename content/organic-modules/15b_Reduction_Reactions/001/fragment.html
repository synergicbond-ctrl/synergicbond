Practice 30 Numerical

How many moles of H₂ are consumed when 1 mol of buta-1,3-diene is hydrogenated completely over Ni?

Show answer

Each C=C takes one H₂: 2 mol.

Answer: 2

Practice 31 Numerical

How many moles of H₂ are consumed when 1 mol of benzene is completely hydrogenated over Pt at high pressure?

Show answer

Three C=C bonds in the Kekulé structure: 3 mol of H₂ give cyclohexane.

Answer: 3

Practice 32 Numerical

How many moles of H₂ are consumed when 1 mol of but-1-en-3-yne (CH₂=CH–C≡CH) is hydrogenated completely over Pd/C?

Show answer

One C=C (1 mol) and one C≡C (2 mol): 3 mol.

Answer: 3

Practice 33 Numerical

How many moles of H₂ (or the equivalent in electrons ÷ 2) are needed to reduce 1 mol of nitrobenzene completely to aniline?

Show answer

Answer: 3

Practice 34 Numerical

How many moles of LiAlH₄ are needed, in theory, to reduce 1 mol of ethyl benzoate completely (each LiAlH₄ supplies four hydrides, and an ester takes two)?

Show answer

Two hydrides per ester: 2/4 = 0.5 mol LiAlH₄.

Answer: 0.5
