Example 9 Adv

Method of purification

Choose a method: (a) aniline from tar, (b) naphthalene + NaCl, (c) glycerol from soap lye, (d) benzoic acid from a hot aqueous solution, (e) acetone + methanol, (f) o-nitrophenol + p-nitrophenol, (g) a mixture of two amino acids, (h) caffeine from tea leaves.

Show solution
  1. (a) steam distillation. (b) sublimation. (c) distillation under reduced pressure. (d) crystallisation (cooling). (e) fractional distillation (b.p. 329 K and 338 K). (f) steam distillation (ortho distils). (g) paper or column/ion-exchange chromatography. (h) extraction into hot water and then dichloromethane (differential extraction), then sublimation or crystallisation.
