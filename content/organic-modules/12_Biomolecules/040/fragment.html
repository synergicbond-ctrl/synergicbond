Level 1: Main-type problems (single correct or numerical)

Attempt each problem before opening the solution. Level 1 covers, in order, carbohydrates, amino acids and peptides, proteins and enzymes, nucleic acids, and vitamins, hormones and lipids.

Carbohydrates

Practice 1 L1 · carbohydrates

The D or L designation of a sugar is decided by the configuration at

(a) C-1 (b) C-2 (c) the highest-numbered stereocentre, farthest from the carbonyl group (d) the anomeric carbon

Show answer

(c). D/L refers to the stereocentre farthest from the carbonyl: C-5 in a hexose, C-4 in a pentose. OH on the right = D.

Practice 2 L1 · carbohydrates

How many stereocentres are present in (i) open-chain D-glucose, (ii) β-D-glucopyranose, (iii) open-chain D-fructose, (iv) β-D-fructofuranose?

(a) 4, 5, 3, 4 (b) 4, 4, 3, 3 (c) 5, 5, 4, 4 (d) 4, 5, 4, 4

Show answer

(a). Glucose open chain: C-2 to C-5 = 4. Pyranose adds C-1 = 5. Fructose open chain: C-3, C-4, C-5 = 3. Fructofuranose: C-2 (anomeric), C-3, C-4, C-5 = 4.

Practice 3 L1 · carbohydrates

The total number of stereoisomers of an open-chain aldopentose is

(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 2

Show answer

(b). Three stereocentres: 23 = 8 (4 D + 4 L).

Practice 4 L1 · carbohydrates

D-Glucose and D-mannose are

(a) anomers (b) enantiomers (c) C-2 epimers (d) C-4 epimers

Show answer

D-Glucose and D-galactose differ in configuration at

Practice 7 L1 · carbohydrates

Which pair gives the same osazone?

(a) glucose, galactose (b) glucose, mannose (c) glucose, allose (d) mannose, galactose

Show answer

(b). Osazone formation destroys the configuration at C-1 and C-2 only. Glucose and mannose (C-2 epimers) share C-3 to C-5.

Practice 8 L1 · carbohydrates

Practice 11 L1 · carbohydrates

Glucose forms a pentaacetate with acetic anhydride. This proves

Practice 12 L1 · carbohydrates

α-D-Glucose has [α] = +112° and the β-anomer +18.7°. The equilibrium mixture shows +52.7°. The percentage of the α-anomer at equilibrium is nearest to

(a) 36 % (b) 64 % (c) 50 % (d) 25 %

Show answer

(a). x(112) + (1 − x)(18.7) = 52.7 → x = 34.0/93.3 = 0.364 → 36 % α.

Practice 13 L1 · carbohydrates

A pyranose has [α] = +150° (α-form) and +50° (β-form). At equilibrium a solution shows +75°. The percentage of the β-form is

(a) 25 % (b) 50 % (c) 75 % (d) 40 %

Show answer

(c). x(150) + (1 − x)(50) = 75 → 100x = 25 → x = 0.25 α, so 75 % β.
