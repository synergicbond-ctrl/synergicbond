25. Pinacol coupling (reductive dimerisation of ketones)

Reagents: 2 ketones (or aldehydes) + Mg–Hg (or Mg/TiCl4, or Na) in a non-protic solvent (benzene or ether), then H3O+. Mechanism: single-electron transfer gives a ketyl radical anion R2C•–O−; two ketyl radicals couple to the vicinal diolate. Acetone gives pinacol (2,3-dimethylbutane-2,3-diol); benzophenone gives benzopinacol. In a protic solvent the same metals instead give the simple alcohol (R2CH–OH).

Key idea Pinacol coupling makes the diol; the pinacol rearrangement (Section 33) is its acid-catalysed continuation to the ketone pinacolone: the two reactions are a classic two-step sequence in JEE: 2 acetone → pinacol → pinacolone (3,3-dimethylbutan-2-one).
