Trap Terminal alkynes are not reduced by Na/NH3 to alkenes: they first form the acetylide (deprotonated), which resists further reduction. Na/NH3 is used for internal alkynes only.
Example 49 Advanced

Make the stereoisomer

Starting from but-2-yne, make (a) cis-but-2-ene, (b) trans-but-2-ene, (c) meso-2,3-dibromobutane, (d) (±)-2,3-dibromobutane, (e) meso-butane-2,3-diol.

Show solution
  1. (a) H2, Lindlar. (b) Na/NH3(l).
  2. (c) trans-but-2-ene (b) + Br2/CCl4 (anti on trans gives meso).
  3. (d) cis-but-2-ene (a) + Br2 (anti on cis gives racemic).
  4. (e) cis-but-2-ene (a) + OsO4/NaHSO3 (syn on cis gives meso).
