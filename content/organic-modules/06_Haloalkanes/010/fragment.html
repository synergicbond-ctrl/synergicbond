9. SNi: substitution nucleophilic internal

In the reaction of an alcohol with thionyl chloride in the absence of base, the first-formed alkyl chlorosulfite ROS(=O)Cl loses SO2 as a tight ion pair R+[OS(=O)Cl]− → the chloride ion is delivered from the same side as the departing oxygen: retention. There is no free, solvent-separated carbocation; hence no racemisation (and, unlike SN1, rearrangement is rare).

SOCl2, no base⟶
SN-i: retention. (S)-Butan-2-ol to (S)-2-chlorobutane. Also seen in decomposition of alkyl chloroformates ROCOCl and with some chlorosulfites.
  • With pyridine: pyridine reacts with the chlorosulfite HCl (or forms the pyridinium chloride) and supplies free Cl−; SN2 attack at the back: inversion.
  • Evidence: reaction is first order; rate increases little with solvent polarity; stereospecific retention.
Trap Retention in SNi means retention of spatial arrangement (and of the letter if the priority order is unchanged). The fate of the CIP letter must be checked from the priorities of the product (e.g. alcohol with –OH to chloride –Cl has same order in 2-butanol).
