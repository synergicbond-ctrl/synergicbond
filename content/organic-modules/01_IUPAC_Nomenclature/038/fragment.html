Concept Check

Name CH₃CH₂CH₂C≡N and CH₃CH₂CH₂N⁺≡C⁻.

Show answer

Butanenitrile (the nitrile carbon is part of the four-carbon chain) and 1-isocyanopropane (propane chain plus the isocyano prefix).

Concept Check

Which group is the suffix in H₂N–CH₂CH₂–OH?

Show answer

The –OH group: alcohols outrank amines, so the compound is 2-aminoethanol.

Concept Check

Why is cyclohexanecarbonitrile a seven-carbon compound while butanenitrile has four?

Show answer

On the chain the nitrile carbon is counted in the root (butane = 4 C including it); on a ring it is added by the suffix, so six ring carbons plus one nitrile carbon give seven.
