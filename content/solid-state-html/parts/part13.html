◆Concept

This part next derives the contact geometry connecting cube edge length a and atomic radius r for SC, FCC and BCC.

1. Simple Cubic

◆Concept

Nearest neighbours touch along an edge. One edge contains two radii.

∑Formula

a = 2r ⇒ r = a/2.

2. Face-Centred Cubic

◆Concept

Atoms touch along a face diagonal, not along an edge.

∑Formula

Face diagonal = a√2.

∑Formula

4r = a√2 ⇒ a = 2√2 r ⇒ r = a/(2√2).

3. Body-Centred Cubic

◆Concept

Atoms touch along the body diagonal.

∑Formula

Body diagonal = a√3.

∑Formula

4r = a√3 ⇒ a = 4r/√3 ⇒ r = (√3/4)a.

⚠JEE Advanced traps & edge cases

  • SC contact: edge; FCC contact: face diagonal; BCC contact: body diagonal.
  • Corner atoms in FCC/BCC do not touch along the cube edge.
  • Use centre-to-centre distances, not visible surface gaps, when deriving geometry.

▶Solved examples

Worked example 1

Example 1

A BCC metal has r=125 pm. Find a.

  1. Use a=4r/√3.
  2. a=500/√3 pm.

a ≈ 288.7 pm.

Worked example 2

Example 2

An FCC face diagonal is 600 pm. Find r.

  1. Along the face diagonal, total contact length = 4r.
  2. 4r=600 pm.

r = 150 pm.
