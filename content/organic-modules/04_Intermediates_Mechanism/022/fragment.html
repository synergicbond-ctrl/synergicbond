Source notes

The sources of all quoted data and experimental observations are listed below; author and book names appear only here. All numerical data (energies, bond dissociation enthalpies, pKa and pKR+ values, relative rates, product ratios) and the experimental observations quoted in this chapter were taken from the books below, which were opened and read for this purpose. Page and section numbers refer to the editions listed.

  1. Clayden, J.; Greeves, N.; Warren, S. Organic Chemistry, 2nd ed., Oxford University Press, 2012. Used for: pKa values of inorganic and carbon acids, acetylene, nitromethane and hybridisation (chapter 8, pp. 170 to 176); the Bürgi–Dunitz trajectory and the reactivity of aldehydes and ketones (chapter 6, pp. 128 to 129); the enolate HOMO, C- and O-alkylation (chapters 20 and 26); the benzyne mechanism, regiochemistry with substituted halides, generation from anthranilic acid and mass-spectrometric observation (chapter 22, pp. 523 to 525); carbene structure, singlet and triplet states, bond angles, singlet–triplet gap, formation by α-elimination, diazo and tosylhydrazone routes, cyclopropanation and its stereochemistry, the Wolff rearrangement, Curtius and Hofmann rearrangements, nitrenes (chapter 38, pp. 1005 to 1021); the pinacol rearrangement and the choice of migrating group, the Baeyer–Villiger reaction and its migratory order (chapter 36, pp. 946 to 953); the 14C labelling experiment on chlorobenzene (pp. 1036 to 1037); kinetic and thermodynamic control; Diels–Alder endo selectivity.
  2. Wade, L. G. Organic Chemistry, 9th ed., Pearson, 2017. Used for: bond dissociation enthalpies and the enthalpy of chlorination of methane (sections 4-6 and 4-7, Table 4-2); selectivity of chlorination and bromination of propane (section 4-13, Figures 4-5 and 4-9); kinetic versus thermodynamic control in the addition of HBr to buta-1,3-diene (section 15-6); the tropylium ion and the pKa of cycloheptatriene (section 16-8); acidity trends in the periodic table (chapter 2, Figure 2-11).
  3. Carey, F. A.; Sundberg, R. J. Advanced Organic Chemistry, Part A: Structure and Mechanisms, 4th ed., Kluwer/Plenum, 2000.
