1. How this chapter is organised and why it decides your organic score

General Organic Chemistry (GOC) is not a list of reactions. It is the set of six or seven ideas from which every later organic mechanism is derived. A student who truly owns GOC can predict the product of a reaction that has never been seen before; a student who has only memorised reactions cannot. Every later chapter leans on a particular part of this one.

IMP The one habit that separates 90 % scorers from the rest: for every question, first write the conjugate base (or the intermediate) on paper, show where the charge or the lone pair sits, and only then compare. Almost every GOC question is the same question in disguise: where does the charge go, and how well can the molecule accommodate it?
