2.7 Halogen and nitro compounds in substitutive nomenclature

Fluoro, chloro, bromo, iodo and nitro are only ever prefixes: they never outrank a functional group, never form a suffix, and are numbered like alkyl groups. Name chains by the usual rules (Section 2.1) with halogens counted among the substituents.

  • Functional-class names such as ethyl chloride and isopropyl bromide are converted by alkyl + halide → haloalkane: methyl chloride is chloromethane; tert-butyl bromide is 2-bromo-2-methylpropane.
  • Nitro compounds R–NO₂ are nitroalkanes (the nitrogen is bonded to carbon). The isomeric nitrites R–O–N=O are alkyl nitrites.
