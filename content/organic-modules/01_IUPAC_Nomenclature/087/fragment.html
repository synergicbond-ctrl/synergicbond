10.5 Summary sheet — traps

  • Lowest locants: first point of difference.
  • Principal group → multiple bond → prefix set → alphabetical first.
  • Chain with max principal groups beats a longer chain with fewer.
  • Alphabetical order ignores di/tri/sec/tert, includes iso/neo/cyclo and complex-group initials.
  • 2013 prefers the longest chain; JEE answer keys keep the double bond in the chain.
  • Always redraw the structure from your final name.
