Practice 188 L2 MCQ multi

Markovnikov or not

Which additions give the product where X is on the terminal carbon (anti-Markovnikov-looking)? (A) CH3CH=CH2 + HBr/ROOR (B) CF3CH=CH2 + HBr (C) CH2=CH–COOH + HBr (D) CH2=CHCl + HBr

Show answer

A, B, C. (D) gives CH3CHClBr (X on the carbon already bearing Cl, Markovnikov).

Practice 189 L2 MCQ multi

Practice 201 L2 Draw

Ozonolysis of a ring

Alkene A (C7H12) adds 1 mol H2 to give methylcyclohexane. Ozonolysis (Zn/H2O) gives one product, a keto-aldehyde. (i) Identify A. (ii) What is the product of ozonolysis? (iii) What is the hydroboration–oxidation product?

Practice 211 L2 Paragraph

Passage: Alkene B

Hydrocarbon B (C5H10) decolourises Br2. On ozonolysis (Zn/H2O) it gives a mixture of an aldehyde and a ketone. With HBr/ROOR it gives a primary bromide. (i) Name B. (ii) Name the aldehyde and ketone. (iii) Name the bromide.

Show answer

B must have a terminal =CH2 so that HBr/peroxide gives a primary bromide, and ozonolysis must give one aldehyde and one ketone: 2-methylbut-1-ene, CH2=C(CH3)CH2CH3. (i) B = 2-methylbut-1-ene. (ii) methanal (aldehyde) and butan-2-one (ketone). (iii) 1-bromo-2-methylbutane.
