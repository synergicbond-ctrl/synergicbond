Example 16 Main

Chain building

Starting from 1-bromopropane, propose a route to (a) butan-1-amine (b) butanoic acid (c) 1-azidopropane (d) 2-hexyne.

Show solution
  1. (a) KCN → butanenitrile; reduce with LiAlH4 (or H2/Ni): CH3CH2CH2CH2NH2.
  2. (b) KCN → butanenitrile; hydrolyse with H3O+/heat → CH3CH2CH2COOH. Rule: Cn–X → Cn+1–CN → Cn+1 acid, so a bromide with n carbons gives an acid with n + 1 carbons.
  3. (c) NaN3, DMF.
  4. (d) CH3C≡CH + NaNH2 → CH3C≡C−; + CH3CH2CH2Br → CH3C≡C–CH2CH2CH3 (hex-2-yne).
Example 17 Adv

Order of products

Ethyl bromide is treated with excess ammonia (A) and with a limited quantity of ammonia (B). Write the major products and the number of different amines possible.

Show solution
  1. The reaction is successive SN2: EtNH2 (more nucleophilic than NH3) → Et2NH → Et3N → Et4N+Br−.
  2. (A) large excess NH3: mostly ethylamine.
  3. (B) limited NH3: all four, with tertiary amine and quaternary salt significant.
  4. Three amines (1°, 2°, 3°) plus the quaternary ammonium salt.
