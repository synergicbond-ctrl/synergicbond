5. Chromatography

Chromatography (Greek "colour writing", introduced by Tswett for plant pigments) separates the components of a mixture by their different distribution between a stationary phase and a mobile phase that moves over it. A component that is strongly held by the stationary phase moves slowly; one that prefers the mobile phase moves quickly. It is the most powerful purification and analysis technique for small amounts.
