Example 44 Adv

Stereo-defined alkene

Prepare (a) cis-hex-3-ene and (b) trans-hex-3-ene from but-1-yne and bromoethane.

Show solution
  1. Build hex-3-yne: but-1-yne + NaNH2 → CH3CH2C≡C− → + CH3CH2Br (SN2) → hex-3-yne, CH3CH2C≡CCH2CH3.
  2. (a) Hex-3-yne + H2, Lindlar → cis-hex-3-ene (syn). (b) Hex-3-yne + Na, liq. NH3 → trans-hex-3-ene (anti).
