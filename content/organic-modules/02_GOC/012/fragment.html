Summary sheet: electronic effects

IMP Summary sheet — Inductive, mesomeric, electromeric, hyperconjugation.
  • Inductive: σ-bonds, permanent, decays rapidly, depends on electronegativity and s-character.
  • Mesomeric: π or lone pair conjugated, permanent, alternate positions, does not decay with distance but needs planarity.
  • Electromeric: one multiple bond, temporary, reagent needed.
  • Hyperconjugation: σ(C–H) next to π, p, or radical; the number of hyperconjugative structures equals the number of α-hydrogens.
  • +M groups: –O⁻ > –NR₂ > –NH₂ > –OH ≈ –OR > –NHCOR > –OCOR > halogens.
  • −M groups: –NO₂ > –CN ≈ –SO₃H > –CHO > –COR > –COOH ≈ –COOR > –CONH₂ > –COO⁻.
  • When −I and +M oppose, +M wins for –OH, –OR, –NH₂ (activating) and −I wins for halogens (deactivating).
  • Resonance requires planarity; a twisted donor/acceptor loses its M effect (SIR).
