2.1 α-Amino acids: structure and stereochemistry

An α-amino acid has an amino group and a carboxyl group on the same (α) carbon: H2N–CH(R)–COOH. About 500 amino acids occur in nature; 20 are coded by DNA and make up the proteins (two more, selenocysteine and pyrrolysine, are special cases).

  • Chirality. Every protein amino acid except glycine has a stereogenic α-carbon. All natural protein amino acids belong to the L-series: in the Fischer projection (COOH at the top, R at the bottom) the –NH2 is on the left. D-Amino acids occur only in bacterial cell walls and some peptide antibiotics (gramicidin).
  • CIP labels. L-Amino acids are (S) at the α-carbon, except L-cysteine, which is (R), because –CH2SH outranks –COOH (S > O at the first point of difference: –CH2SH has (S,H,H); –COOH has (O,O,O) — the first point of difference compares the highest atoms in each set, S beats O). The same happens with selenocysteine.
  • Two stereocentres: isoleucine (2S,3S) and threonine (2S,3R). Each has four stereoisomers: L, D and the two allo diastereomers. Natural ones: L-isoleucine, L-threonine.
  • Glycine has two H on the α-carbon: achiral.
Key idea The S/R label and the D/L label do not correlate in general. L-Alanine is (S); L-cysteine is (R); L-serine is (S) although its side chain also has a heteroatom: –CH2OH (O,H,H) loses to –COOH (O,O,O).

Classification

  • By side chain: non-polar (Gly, Ala, Val, Leu, Ile, Pro, Met), aromatic (Phe, Tyr, Trp), polar neutral (Ser, Thr, Cys, Asn, Gln), acidic (Asp, Glu), basic (Lys, Arg, His).
  • By nutrition: essential (cannot be synthesised by the body; must come from food): Val, Leu, Ile, Lys, Met, Phe, Thr, Trp (these eight are called "essential" in most Indian texts); His and Arg are essential for infants and in growth ("semi-essential"). Non-essential: Gly, Ala, Ser, Cys, Tyr, Pro, Asn, Gln, Asp, Glu.
  • By the net charge at pH 7: acidic (−1), neutral (0), basic (+1; His is about 10 % protonated).
Trap Do not confuse "essential" with "important". All twenty are needed to make proteins; the essential ones are simply those that must be supplied by the diet.
Shortcut Memory aid for the essential amino acids: PVT TIM HALL = Phe, Val, Thr, Trp, Ile, Met, His, Arg, Leu, Lys (the last of these, His and Arg, are the semi-essential pair).
