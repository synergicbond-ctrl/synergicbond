Example 19 Adv

Group identification

A neutral compound C4H8O2 (a) is hydrolysed by hot NaOH; the distillate gives a yellow ppt with I2/NaOH; the residue on acidification gives an acid B that reduces Tollens' reagent. Identify the compound.

Show solution
  1. The neutral hydrolysable compound is an ester. Acid B reduces Tollens' ⇒ formic acid. The alcohol distils and gives iodoform ⇒ CH3CH(OH)–R: here propan-2-ol. So the ester is isopropyl formate HCOOCH(CH3)2 (C4H8O2: yes).
  2. Check: the DBE of C4H8O2 is (8 + 2 − 8)/2 = 1 (the C=O).
Concept check

Which of acetaldehyde, benzaldehyde, acetone gives (i) a silver mirror, (ii) Fehling's ppt, (iii) iodoform?

Show answer

(i) acetaldehyde and benzaldehyde; (ii) acetaldehyde only; (iii) acetaldehyde and acetone.

Concept check

Why does a carboxylic acid dissolve in NaHCO3 with CO2 evolution but phenol does not?

Show answer

RCOOH (pKa ≈ 4-5) is stronger than H2CO3 (pKa1 6.4), so the reaction goes to completion; phenol (pKa 10) is weaker, K ≈ 10-3.6.
