H. Aromaticity

Problem 119 [Application]

Classify tropylium cation as aromatic, anti-aromatic or non-aromatic.

Worked solution
  1. 6π, planar, cyclic: aromatic.

Answer: Aromatic.

Classify cyclooctatetraene (tub) as aromatic, anti-aromatic or non-aromatic.

Worked solution
