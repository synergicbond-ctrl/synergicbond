G. Bond lengths

Problem 116 [Application]

Compare the bond lengths C–C (alkane) and C=C (alkene) and explain the order.

Worked solution
  1. C–C (alkane) ≈ 154 pm; C=C (alkene) ≈ 133 pm.
  2. Higher bond order and more s-character give a shorter bond; resonance averages a single bond toward a double bond.

Answer: Order: C–C (alkane) is longer than C=C (alkene).
