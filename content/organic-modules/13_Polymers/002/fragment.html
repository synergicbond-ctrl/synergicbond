Chapter map and prerequisites

This module covers polymer chemistry for JEE Main and JEE Advanced. Assumption stated: the chapter scope is the standard Class 12 polymers chapter (classification, addition and condensation polymers, rubbers, biodegradable polymers, molecular mass) extended with the mechanisms, tacticity, copolymer and molecular-mass-distribution material that JEE Advanced style problems draw on. Items beyond the usual Class 12 text are marked Enrichment; no claim is made about their exact syllabus status.

Prerequisites (each is used explicitly in this chapter):

  • Stability of radicals and carbocations; hyperconjugation and resonance (monomer–mechanism matching, §5).
  • Addition to alkenes and conjugated dienes, 1,2 vs 1,4 addition (§6).
  • Nucleophilic acyl substitution of acids, acid chlorides and esters; amide resonance (§13–14).
  • Electrophilic substitution of phenol and the aldol-type attack on HCHO (§15).
  • cis/trans (E/Z) isomerism and CIP labels (§3, §6).
  • Mole concept, averages and logarithms (§12, §21).
