54. Mechanism: Stephen reduction of a nitrile to an aldehyde

  • Step 1 – imidoyl chloride. HCl protonates the nitrile N and Cl− adds to the activated carbon: R–C(Cl)=NH (an imidoyl chloride).
  • Step 2 – electron transfer from Sn(II). SnCl2 reduces the C=N (with HCl): the aldimine–tin complex (RCH=NH)·SnCl4, precipitated as the "aldimine stannichloride".
  • Step 3 – hydrolysis. Water adds to the imine carbon (carbinolamine), NH3 is lost: RCHO. Why the reaction stops at the aldehyde stage: the imine is trapped as a stable tin complex and is hydrolysed only on work-up; the carbonyl is never present with the reductant.
