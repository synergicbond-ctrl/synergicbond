47. Mechanism-writing checklist

  • Draw lone pairs on O and N where they matter.
  • Curved arrows begin at electron-rich centres (lone pair, π bond, carbanion) and end at electron-poor atoms.
  • Check charge balance on every line; the number of H atoms and charges must be conserved.
  • Under basic conditions, avoid writing strongly acidic species (R–OH2+) and under acidic conditions avoid strongly basic species (R–O−, carbanion).
  • For equilibria, mark steps as reversible with two arrows.
  • Identify the rate-determining step when asked about rate or substituent effect.
