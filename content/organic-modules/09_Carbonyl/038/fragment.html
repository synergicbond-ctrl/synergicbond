33. Darzens, Henry and pinacol reactions

Darzens glycidic ester condensation. An aldehyde or ketone with an α-chloroester and a base (NaOEt, NaNH2) gives an α,β-epoxy ester (glycidic ester). Hydrolysis and decarboxylation give an aldehyde (or ketone) with the carbon chain lengthened by one carbon.

+ NaOEt⟶ (i) NaOH (ii) H₃O⁺, Δ, −CO₂⟶
Cyclohexanone → glycidic ester → cyclohexanecarbaldehyde

Henry (nitroaldol) reaction. An aldehyde or ketone and a nitroalkane with a base (the α-H of RCH2NO2 has pKa about 10) give a β-nitro alcohol; dehydration gives a nitroalkene (a Michael acceptor).

+ NaOH or Et₃N⟶
Benzaldehyde + nitromethane → 2-nitro-1-phenylethanol

Pinacol coupling and rearrangement. Reduction of a ketone (acetone) with Mg–Hg in benzene (or Mg/TiCl4) gives the 1,2-diol pinacol (2,3-dimethylbutane-2,3-diol) through a ketyl radical anion dimerisation. Pinacol on heating with acid undergoes the pinacol–pinacolone rearrangement to 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-one (pinacolone) by carbocation formation and a 1,2-methyl shift.

+ Mg–Hg, benzene; then H₂O⟶ H₂SO₄, Δ⟶
Acetone → pinacol → pinacolone (pinacol rearrangement)
Shortcut In the pinacol rearrangement: protonate the OH that gives the more stable carbocation; the group that migrates is the one with the higher migratory aptitude (aryl and H migrate in preference to alkyl); after the shift the oxygen carries the positive charge (oxocarbenium) and loses H+ to give the C=O.
Example 38 Adv

Pinacol rearrangement outcome

Predict the product of acid-catalysed rearrangement of (a) 2,3-dimethylbutane-2,3-diol; (b) 2,3-diphenylbutane-2,3-diol.

Show solution
  1. (a) Symmetric diol: protonate and lose either OH to give the tertiary cation; a methyl group migrates to the cationic carbon; the oxygen on the neighbouring carbon becomes C=O: 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-one (pinacolone).
  2. (b) Loss of OH from one carbon gives a tertiary, benzylic cation. Phenyl (better migrating group than methyl) shifts from the neighbouring carbon, which becomes the carbonyl carbon: 3,3-diphenylbutan-2-one.
