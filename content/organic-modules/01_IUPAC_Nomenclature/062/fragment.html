7.7 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Name the compound with COOH, OH and NO₂ on benzene, where OH is ortho and NO₂ para to COOH.

Show answer

2-Hydroxy-4-nitrobenzoic acid: the acid is the principal group (C-1), then {2,4}; hydroxy is cited before nitro.

Concept Check

Is C₆H₅CH₂Br named bromobenzene?

Show answer

No. Bromobenzene is C₆H₅Br. C₆H₅CH₂Br is (bromomethyl)benzene (benzyl bromide).

Concept Check

Why is p-cresol 4-methylphenol and not 1-hydroxy-4-methylbenzene?

Show answer

OH is the principal group (phenol), so the parent is phenol; the retained parent already contains the OH, which is C-1.
