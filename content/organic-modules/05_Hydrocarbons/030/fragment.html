29. Summary sheet: alkenes

  • Alkene = nucleophile: electrophile attacks first; regiochemistry from the more stable cation (Markovnikov); radical HBr from the more stable radical (anti-Markovnikov).
  • Cation intermediate ⇒ rearrangement possible (HX, H3O+); bridged ion ⇒ no rearrangement, anti addition (Br2, Hg(OAc)2); concerted ⇒ syn (BH3, OsO4, RCO3H, carbenes, H2).
  • Anti addition to cis alkene → racemic; syn addition to cis alkene → meso (for symmetric 2-butene type).
  • Ozonolysis: every C=C becomes two C=O; reductive vs oxidative work-up differ only for aldehyde carbons.
  • Stability: more substituted and trans alkenes are more stable; E2 is anti-periplanar; Saytzeff with small bases, Hofmann with bulky bases, NR3+, F.
