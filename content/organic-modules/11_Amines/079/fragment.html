79. Level 3, set E — multi-step synthesis

Practice 286 L3 Synthesis

Convert benzene into 1,3,5-tribromobenzene. Give reagents and explain why direct bromination cannot be used.

HNO₃/H₂SO₄⟶ Sn/HCl; NaOH⟶ Br₂/H₂O⟶ NaNO₂/HCl, 273 K; H₃PO₂⟶
Show answer
  1. Bromine is an o/p director, so successive bromination of benzene gives 1,2-, 1,4-, 1,2,4-... patterns, never the 1,3,5 isomer. NH2 (o/p) places the three Br atoms at 2, 4 and 6; deamination removes the nitrogen leaving the 1,3,5 pattern.

Benzene → PhNO2 → PhNH2 → 2,4,6-tribromoaniline → diazotise, H3PO2 → 1,3,5-tribromobenzene.

Practice 287 L3 Synthesis

Convert benzene into p-nitrophenol using the amino group as an intermediate.

Show answer
  1. Direct nitration of phenol gives o- and p-nitrophenol mixtures with oxidation; the route through p-nitroaniline is regiospecific for para.

Benzene → HNO3/H2SO4 → PhNO2 → Sn/HCl → PhNH2 → Ac2O → PhNHAc → HNO3/H2SO4, 288 K → p-nitroacetanilide → H3O+ → p-nitroaniline → NaNO2/H2SO4, 273 K → diazonium → H2O, warm → p-nitrophenol.

Practice 288 L3 Synthesis

Convert p-nitroaniline into 3,5-dibromonitrobenzene.

Br₂/AcOH (2 equiv.)⟶ NaNO₂/HCl, 273 K; H₃PO₂⟶
Show answer
  1. The NO2 (meta director) and Br atoms are meta to one another only after the NH2, which is para to NO2, is removed.

Brominate p-nitroaniline (NH2 directs ortho: 2,6-dibromo-4-nitroaniline), then diazotise and deaminate with H3PO2: 3,5-dibromonitrobenzene.

Practice 289 L3 Synthesis

Convert aniline into benzylamine.

Show answer
  1. One carbon (the nitrile carbon) is added to the ring through the Sandmeyer cyanation.

Aniline → NaNO2/HCl, 273 K → PhN2+Cl− → CuCN/KCN → PhCN → LiAlH4 (or H2/Ni) → PhCH2NH2.

Practice 290 L3 Synthesis

Convert acetophenone into 1-phenylethanamine and into N-methyl-1-phenylethanamine.

Show answer
  1. Reductive amination: carbonyl + amine → imine → reduction; the product is racemic.

(a) PhCOCH3 + NH3, H2/Ni (or NaBH3CN) → PhCH(NH2)CH3. (b) PhCOCH3 + CH3NH2, NaBH3CN → PhCH(CH3)NHCH3.

Practice 291 L3 Synthesis

Convert nitrobenzene into m-nitrobenzoic acid.

Show answer
  1. The cyano group is introduced where the NH2 was. Direct nitration of benzoic acid also gives the meta isomer, but the route above shows how to use selective Zinin reduction.

PhNO2 → fuming HNO3/H2SO4, 373 K → m-dinitrobenzene → (NH4)2S → m-nitroaniline → NaNO2/HCl, 273 K → CuCN → m-nitrobenzonitrile → H3O+, heat → m-nitrobenzoic acid.

Practice 292 L3 Synthesis

Convert p-toluidine into (a) p-cresol, (b) p-tolunitrile, (c) p-fluorotoluene.

Show answer

(a) NaNO2/H2SO4, 273 K; H2O, warm. (b) NaNO2/HCl, then CuCN/KCN. (c) NaNO2/HBF4; filter ArN2+BF4−; heat.

Practice 293 L3 Synthesis

Prepare methyl orange from aniline and N,N-dimethylaniline (give the route to each partner).

Show answer
  1. Sulphanilic acid is a zwitterion insoluble in acid, so it is dissolved first in aqueous Na2CO3 before NaNO2/HCl.

Sulphanilic acid: aniline + conc. H2SO4, 453 K. Diazotise it (NaNO2, Na2CO3, then HCl, 273 K). N,N-Dimethylaniline: aniline + HCHO/HCOOH (Eschweiler–Clarke) or excess CH3I/base controlled. Coupling at pH 4–5 followed by NaOH gives sodium p-[4-(dimethylamino)phenylazo]benzenesulphonate (methyl orange).

Practice 294 L3 Synthesis

Show how ethanol can be converted into (a) methylamine, (b) ethylamine, (c) propylamine.

Show answer

(a) EtOH → K2Cr2O7/H+ → CH3COOH → SOCl2; NH3 → CH3CONH2 → Br2/KOH → CH3NH2. (b) CH3CONH2 → LiAlH4 → CH3CH2NH2. (c) EtOH → PBr3 → EtBr → KCN → CH3CH2CN → LiAlH4 → CH3CH2CH2NH2.

Practice 295 L3 Synthesis

Using any reagents, prepare 4-aminobenzoic acid (PABA) from toluene.

Show answer
  1. Oxidise before reducing: an NH2 group would be destroyed by KMnO4. (Alternative: reduce, acetylate, oxidise, hydrolyse.)

Toluene → HNO3/H2SO4 → p-nitrotoluene (separate from o) → KMnO4/OH−, heat; H+ → p-nitrobenzoic acid → Sn/HCl, then NaOH/neutralise → p-aminobenzoic acid.
