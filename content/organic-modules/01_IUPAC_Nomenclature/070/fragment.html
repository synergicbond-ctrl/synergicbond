8.7 A bridged example: camphor

Special Case (+)-Camphor is (1R,4R)-1,7,7-trimethylbicyclo[2.2.1]heptan-2-one. The one-carbon bridge ties the two bridgeheads together, so only two of the four formal combinations of R and S are geometrically possible: (1R,4R) and (1S,4S), which are enantiomers.
(1R,4R)-1,7,7-trimethylbicyclo[2.2.1]heptan-2-one (1S,4S)-1,7,7-trimethylbicyclo[2.2.1]heptan-2-one
(+)-Camphor (1R,4R) and its enantiomer (−)-camphor (1S,4S)
