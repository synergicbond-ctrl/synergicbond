SNi mechanism of thionyl chloride. ROH + SOCl2 → ROS(=O)Cl (chlorosulphite) + HCl. In a non-nucleophilic solvent the chlorosulphite collapses as a tight ion pair: Cl− is delivered from the same face from which SO2 leaves, hence retention. If pyridine is present, pyridinium chloride supplies free Cl− that attacks from the back (inversion).

Example 16 L3 Advanced

Stereochemical outcome

(S)-Butan-2-ol is converted to 2-chlorobutane by three routes: (A) conc. HCl/ZnCl2; (B) SOCl2 alone; (C) TsCl/pyridine followed by LiCl in DMF. What is the stereochemical result of each?

Show solution
  1. (A) SN1: free planar secondary cation; attack from both faces gives mostly racemic 2-chlorobutane (slight excess of inversion from ion-pair effects).
  2. (B) SNi: retention → (S)-2-chlorobutane (the CIP priority order of OH and Cl groups is parallel: X > C2H5 > CH3 > H, so the descriptor is unchanged).
  3. (C) Tosylation: retention; SN2 with Cl−: inversion → (R)-2-chlorobutane.
