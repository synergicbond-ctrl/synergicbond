5.5 Same group more than once; groups on a ring and on its side chain

  • Several identical principal groups: -diol, -dione, -dioic acid, -dinitrile, with all locants listed; the chain must contain as many of them as possible. A chain that must include both end carbons (dialdehydes, diacids, dinitriles) has no locants for the end groups: butanedioic acid, hexanedinitrile.
  • Ring bearing the principal group → ring is the parent; principal group on a chain attached to a ring → chain is the parent and the ring is a cycloalkyl or phenyl prefix.
  • Both ring and chain carry the same principal group once each: the 2013 rule makes the ring senior; older texts count carbons. Problems normally avoid this case.
