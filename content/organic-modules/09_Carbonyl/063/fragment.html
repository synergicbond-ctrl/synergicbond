54. Cyanohydrins in carbohydrate chemistry (Kiliani–Fischer)

HCN adds to an aldose to give two epimeric cyanohydrins (the new stereocentre is C-2 of the longer chain); hydrolysis and lactone reduction (or Pd/BaSO4-catalysed reduction of the nitrile to the imine and its hydrolysis) lengthen the sugar chain by one carbon. This is the same cyanohydrin chemistry in a polyhydroxy setting; the details belong to the biomolecules chapter.
