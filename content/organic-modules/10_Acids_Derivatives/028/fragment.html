Practice 124 L2 · Dibasic acids

The Ka1 of maleic acid is 1.2 × 10−2 and of fumaric acid 9.3 × 10−4; Ka2 of maleic is 5.9 × 10−7 and of fumaric 3.6 × 10−5. Explain both comparisons.

Show answer
  1. Maleic: the cis arrangement lets the monoanion form a strong intramolecular O–H···O− bond: the first proton is lost easily (larger Ka1) but the second is held by the same H-bond (smaller Ka2).   2. Hence Ka1: maleic > fumaric; Ka2: fumaric > maleic.
