Example 38 Main

Van Slyke

A sample containing 0.25 g of glycine (75 g mol−1) is treated with excess HNO2. Find the volume of N2 at STP (22.4 L mol−1).

Show solution
  1. 0.25/75 = 3.33 × 10−3 mol glycine → 3.33 × 10−3 mol N2 (one NH2 each).
  2. V = 3.33 × 10−3 × 22.4 = 74.7 mL.
Example 39 Adv

Nitrogen counting

How many moles of N2 are evolved per mole of (a) lysine, (b) proline, (c) arginine, (d) glutamine with HNO2?

Show solution
  1. (a) Lysine has α-NH2 and ε-NH2 (both primary): 2.
  2. (b) Proline: secondary amine gives N-nitrosamine, no N2: 0.
  3. (c) Arginine: the α-NH2 reacts (1); the guanidine group does not, and the δ-N is not a free primary amine: 1.
  4. (d) Glutamine: α-NH2 (1); the side-chain amide –CONH2 reacts only slowly and is not counted in Van Slyke: 1.
Example 40 Adv

Strecker planning

Which aldehyde and which reagents prepare (a) valine, (b) 2-amino-2-methylpropanoic acid (α-aminoisobutyric acid)?

Show solution
  1. (a) Valine = (CH3)2CH–CH(NH2)COOH. The CHO carbon becomes C-α, so the aldehyde is isobutyraldehyde, (CH3)2CHCHO; NH3/HCN, then H3O+.
  2. (b) (CH3)2C(NH2)COOH has a quaternary α-carbon: the carbonyl partner must be acetone, (CH3)2C=O, with NH3/HCN then hydrolysis.
Example 41 Adv

Thermal products

What is formed when each of the following is heated: (a) glycine, (b) β-alanine, (c) 4-aminobutanoic acid, (d) 6-aminohexanoic acid (high temperature, high dilution)?

Show solution
  1. (a) 2,5-Diketopiperazine (cyclic dimer, 6-ring).
  2. (b) Acrylic acid + NH3 (β-elimination).
  3. (c) 2-Pyrrolidone (γ-lactam, 5-ring).
  4. (d) ε-Caprolactam (7-ring) at high dilution; neat melt gives nylon-6.
Example 42 Adv

Decarboxylation

Decarboxylation of an amino acid Write the product of decarboxylation of (a) histidine and (b) lysine.

Show solution
  1. Histidine (CO2 loss): histamine, 2-(imidazol-4-yl)ethylamine (C5H9N3); release in allergic reactions.
  2. Lysine: cadaverine, pentane-1,5-diamine, H2N(CH2)5NH2 (the smell of decaying flesh); ornithine gives putrescine, 1,4-butanediamine.
Example 43 Main

Ninhydrin test

Which of the following give a purple colour with ninhydrin: glycine, proline, N-acetylglycine, glycylglycine, ammonia?

Show solution
  1. Glycine: purple. Proline: yellow-orange (secondary amine). N-Acetylglycine: no free amine, no colour. Glycylglycine: purple (free N-terminal NH2). Ammonia: a weak purple (the chromophore contains only the N of ammonia/amine).
