Learning objectives and entry check

After this chapter you should be able to:

  • choose a purification method from the physical properties of a compound and its impurity, and justify the choice;
  • calculate Rf, extraction efficiency and the steam-distillation mass ratio;
  • predict the result of Lassaigne's test for any compound, including N + S and N + halogen compounds, and explain every step;
  • use a reagent test to identify or distinguish alcohols, phenols, aldehydes, ketones, acids, esters, amides, amines and nitro compounds, and plan a separation of a binary mixture;
  • compute the percentage of C, H, N, halogens, S and P from experimental data and derive empirical and molecular formulae;
  • compute the degree of unsaturation, and combine it with IR, NMR and test results to deduce a structure.

Concept check

How many moles of CO2 are formed by complete combustion of 0.5 mol of C3H8?

Show answer

3 C atoms per molecule give 3 CO2: 1.5 mol.

Concept check

Which is the stronger acid: benzoic acid (pKa 4.2) or carbonic acid (pKa1 6.4)? Will benzoate be protonated by H2CO3?

Show answer

Benzoic acid is stronger (lower pKa); so benzoate is not protonated by H2CO3.

Concept check

Is it correct to say that a mixture of two immiscible liquids boils at a temperature where the sum of their vapour pressures equals the external pressure?

Show answer

Yes: each liquid exerts its own vapour pressure independently when they are immiscible.
