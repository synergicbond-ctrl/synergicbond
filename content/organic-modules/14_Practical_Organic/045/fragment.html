Exam Trap Proline and hydroxyproline give a yellow (not purple) colour with ninhydrin because the amine is secondary. The biuret test is named after biuret but needs no biuret in the sample: it detects the –CO–NH– groups.
Concept check

Aniline gives a coloured dye after diazotisation and coupling, ethylamine does not. Why?

Show answer

Aromatic diazonium ions are stable at 0-5 °C and couple with β-naphthol; aliphatic diazonium ions decompose at once to N2 and carbocation products.
