11. Isomerism in aromatic compounds

A benzene ring has six equivalent carbon atoms, so isomer counts reduce to how many distinct arrangements of substituents exist, with rotation and reflection of the ring not producing new compounds. For a disubstituted benzene there are three arrangements, ortho (1,2), meta (1,3) and para (1,4), whether the two substituents are the same or different. Thus there are three dichlorobenzenes, three xylenes and three chlorotoluenes (and three chloronitrobenzenes).

o-, m- and p-dichlorobenzene.

For trisubstituted benzenes the count depends on how many substituents are identical: three identical (X, X, X) give 3 isomers (1,2,3; 1,2,4; 1,3,5); two identical and one different (X, X, Y) give 6; three different (X, Y, Z) give 10. For tetrasubstituted benzenes: four identical, 3 isomers (equal to the disubstituted case, since two hydrogens remain); two of one kind and two of another (X2Y2), 11 isomers.

The three trichlorobenzenes (1,2,3-; 1,2,4-; 1,3,5-).

Formula. Substituted benzenes, distinct isomers: 2 groups: 3 (identical or different). 3 identical: 3. Two alike + one different: 6. All three different: 10. 4 identical: 3. X2Y2: 11.

Aromatic isomers of a given formula combine ring-substituent isomerism with side-chain isomerism. C8H10 arenes include ethylbenzene and three xylenes; C9H12 has eight benzene-ring isomers: two propyl-type (propylbenzene, cumene), three ethylmethylbenzenes (o-, m-, p-), and three trimethylbenzenes. C7H8O contains cresols (3), benzyl alcohol and anisole. A phenolic OH and an alcoholic OH on the ring or side chain are functional isomers of each other and of the ethers.

The eight benzene-ring isomers of C9H12.

Key idea. Fix a reference substituent at position 1 and place the others on the remaining positions, then remove arrangements that are the same by ring symmetry.
Trap. Do not forget the side-chain isomers when the question says 'containing a benzene ring' for a larger formula: for example C7H6O2 contains hydroxybenzaldehydes (3), benzoic acid and phenyl methanoate.
