55. Mechanism: Skraup quinoline synthesis (aniline + glycerol)

  • Step 1 – dehydration of glycerol. Hot conc. H2SO4 dehydrates glycerol to acrolein (CH2=CH–CHO).
  • Step 2 – conjugate addition. The nucleophilic N of aniline attacks the β-carbon of acrolein (Michael addition; HOMO nN → LUMO π* of the C=C–C=O system, whose largest coefficient is at the β carbon) to give β-anilinopropionaldehyde.
  • Step 3 – cyclisation. Under acid the aldehyde is protonated; the ortho carbon of the aniline ring (activated by N) attacks the carbonyl carbon (intramolecular electrophilic substitution) to close the six-membered ring.
  • Step 4 – dehydration and oxidation. Loss of water gives 1,2-dihydroquinoline; the mild oxidant (nitrobenzene, which is reduced to aniline and recycled) aromatises it to quinoline.
