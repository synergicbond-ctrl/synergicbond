16. Estimation of nitrogen: the Dumas method

The organic compound is heated with excess copper(II) oxide in a combustion tube in an atmosphere of pure CO2. Carbon → CO2, hydrogen → H2O and nitrogen → free N2 together with a little oxides of nitrogen. The gases then pass over a heated copper gauze, which reduces nitrogen oxides to N2. The mixture goes into a nitrometer containing 40 % KOH solution, which absorbs CO2; the nitrogen is collected over the KOH and its volume is measured at room temperature and atmospheric pressure.

Formula CxHyNz + CuO → xCO2 + (y/2)H2O + (z/2)N2 + Cu 2NO + 2Cu → N2 + 2CuO
IMP Because the gas is collected over an aqueous solution it is saturated with water vapour: the pressure of dry nitrogen = atmospheric pressure − aqueous tension at that temperature. Convert to STP with combined gas law, then:
Formula %N = (28 g × VSTP(mL) / 22 400 mL) / w × 100 = (28 V0)/(22 400 w) × 100 V0 = (P − paq) V × 273 / (760 × T)

Dumas works for all nitrogen-containing organic compounds (including nitro, azo and ring nitrogen) because the combustion at high temperature converts every form of nitrogen to N2.

Example 27 Main

Dumas without correction

0.28 g of an organic compound gave 44.8 mL of N2 at STP in a Dumas determination. Find %N.

Show solution
  1. Moles N2 = 44.8/22 400 = 2.00 × 10-3; mass of N = 2.00 × 10-3 × 28 = 0.0560 g.
  2. %N = 0.0560/0.28 × 100 = 20.0 %.
Example 28 Adv

Dumas with aqueous tension

0.30 g of a nitrogenous compound gave 31.7 mL of nitrogen collected over water at 288 K and 756 mm Hg. The aqueous tension at 288 K is 12.7 mm Hg. Find %N.

Show solution
  1. Pressure of dry N2 = 756 − 12.7 = 743.3 mm Hg.
  2. V0 = (743.3 × 31.7 × 273)/(760 × 288) = 29.39 mL at STP.
  3. Mass of N = 28 × 29.39/22 400 = 0.03674 g; %N = 0.03674/0.30 × 100 = 12.25 %.
IMP Dumas' method needs no carbon in the compound (it only collects N2), unlike Lassaigne's, which needs carbon to make NaCN. Hence hydrazine fails Lassaigne's test but can be analysed by Dumas.
Exam Trap Forgetting aqueous tension is the commonest error; and never use the volume of CO2 in the nitrometer: it is absorbed by KOH. Nitrogen volume is measured at the temperature of the gas burette, not at room temperature of the furnace.

Concept check

Why is the nitrogen volume in the Dumas method corrected for aqueous tension?

Show answer

Nitrogen is collected over aqueous KOH and is saturated with water vapour; the pressure of dry N2 is Patm minus the vapour pressure of water.
