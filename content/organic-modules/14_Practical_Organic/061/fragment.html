20. Degree of unsaturation (double-bond equivalent, DBE)

Once the molecular formula is known, the number of rings plus π bonds is found without knowing the structure. A saturated acyclic hydrocarbon CnH2n+2 has the maximum number of hydrogens. Every ring or π bond removes two hydrogens (one "unsaturation"). So we compare the actual formula with the saturated one.

Formula DBE = (2C + 2 + N − H − X)/2 (C, N, H, X = numbers of carbon, nitrogen, hydrogen and halogen atoms; O and S do not appear; a triply bonded atom counts as 2 DBE; trivalent P counts like N)

Why O and S are ignored: inserting a divalent atom between two atoms (C–C → C–O–C, or C–H → C–OH) does not change the number of hydrogens needed for saturation. Why N is added: a trivalent atom brings one extra valence, so the saturated formula has one more hydrogen (CH3NH2 = C1H5N, 5 = 2 × 1 + 2 + 1). Why halogens are subtracted: X replaces H one-for-one.

Example 44 Main

Computing DBE

Find the DBE of (a) C4H8O, (b) C6H5Cl, (c) C7H7NO2, (d) C8H8O2, (e) C10H8.

Show solution
  1. (a) (8 + 2 − 8)/2 = 1 (one C=O, one C=C, or one ring).
  2. (b) (12 + 2 − 5 − 1)/2 = 4 (benzene ring; chlorobenzene).
  3. (c) (14 + 2 + 1 − 7)/2 = 5 (benzene ring 4 + one of C=O/NO2).
  4. (d) (16 + 2 − 8)/2 = 5 (benzene ring 4 + C=O, e.g. methyl benzoate or phenylacetic acid).
  5. (e) (20 + 2 − 8)/2 = 7: naphthalene (two rings + 5 π bonds).
Example 45 Adv

Using DBE to restrict structures

An optically active open-chain compound C4H8O decolourises bromine water and liberates H2 with sodium. Find its DBE and identify it.

Show solution
  1. DBE = (8 + 2 − 8)/2 = 1. Decolourising bromine water uses that unsaturation as C=C, so there is no C=O and no ring.
  2. Reaction with Na ⇒ –OH, so the compound is an unsaturated alcohol. Among the open-chain unsaturated alcohols C4H7OH only but-3-en-2-ol, CH2=CH–CH(OH)–CH3 has a stereocentre (the carbon bearing H, OH, CH3 and vinyl).
Validity Check The DBE formula treats S as divalent and N as trivalent: S=O bonds of sulphoxides and sulphones are therefore not counted, and the formula gives no information about which rings or π bonds exist, only how many. Nitro groups count one DBE.
Concept check

Find the DBE of C9H8O4 and state a structure type that fits.

Show answer

(18 + 2 − 8)/2 = 6: a benzene ring (4) plus two C=O, e.g. aspirin.
