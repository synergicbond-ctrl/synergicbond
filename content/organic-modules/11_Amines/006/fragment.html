6. Map of the preparative routes

Every route to an amine falls into one of four logical families. Learn them as a map, because exam questions that ask "how many carbons?" point at once to the correct family.

FamilyReactionsCarbon count of the amine
SubstitutionRX + NH3 (ammonolysis), Gabrielsame as RX
ReductionRNO2, RCONH2, oximes, imines (reductive amination)same carbon skeleton
Reduction of nitrileRC≡N → RCH2NH2one carbon more than the alkyl halide that made the nitrile
Degradation (rearrangement)Hofmann bromamide, Curtius, Schmidt, Lossenone carbon less than the amide/acid
Key idea To go up one carbon: RX → RCN → RCH2NH2. To go down one carbon: RCONH2 → RNH2 (Hofmann). To keep the same carbons: RCONH2 + LiAlH4 → RCH2NH2.
