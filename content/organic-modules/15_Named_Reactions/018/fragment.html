15. Robinson annulation (Michael then intramolecular aldol)

A ketone enolate adds to methyl vinyl ketone (Michael), and the resulting 1,5-diketone then undergoes intramolecular aldol condensation to build a new six-membered cyclohexenone ring fused to the old one. Annulation = ring-building. Steps: (i) Michael addition, (ii) enolate of the methyl ketone attacks the original ring carbonyl, (iii) dehydration gives the conjugated enone.

+ NaOEt (Michael step)⟶
Step 1: 2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexanone (a 1,5-diketone)
NaOEt, Δ (aldol, –H₂O)⟶
Step 2: ring closure and dehydration → bicyclic enone (4,4a,5,6,7,8-hexahydronaphthalen-2(3H)-one, an octalone)
Key idea Robinson ring count: new ring contains the four carbons from MVK plus the ketone's carbonyl carbon and its α-carbon = six atoms. The nucleophilic carbon in the aldol step is the CH3 of the MVK part (the only site that gives a six-membered ring), and it ends up as the =CH– of the enone.
Example 24 Adv

Product of Robinson annulation with a substituted ketone

2-Methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione + methyl vinyl ketone, base, then acid. Give the structure type and name the reaction sequence.

Show solution
  1. The doubly activated C-2 (flanked by two C=O, pKa ≈ 5) is the Michael donor. Michael addition to MVK gives 2-methyl-2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexane-1,3-dione (a quaternary carbon). Intramolecular aldol of the CH3CO– enolate on one ring carbonyl followed by dehydration gives the bicyclic Wieland–Miescher ketone (an enedione with an angular methyl group).
