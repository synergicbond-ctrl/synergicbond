3. Chain geometry: tacticity

In a vinyl polymer –CH2–CHX–, every CHX carbon is a pseudo-asymmetric centre. How the groups X sit relative to the zig-zag chain is its tacticity, and it controls crystallinity.

isotactic (X all on one side) syndiotactic (X alternate)
The drawn model is a PVC-like trimer: isotactic = meso (R,S) diads; syndiotactic = racemic diads. Atactic = random.
  • Isotactic: all X on the same side of the plane of the zig-zag chain. Regular, packs into helices, highly crystalline, high m.p. Isotactic polypropene (m.p. ≈ 165 °C) is the commercial fibre and plastic.
  • Syndiotactic: X alternate regularly above and below. Also crystalline, with different melting point.
  • Atactic: X randomly placed. No regular packing, amorphous, soft, rubbery or sticky. Atactic polypropene is used as adhesive, asphalt modifier.
IMP Ziegler–Natta (heterogeneous TiCl4/Al(C2H5)3) catalysts control tacticity because the monomer coordinates and inserts at a chiral surface site in a fixed orientation. Free-radical polymerisation of propene or styrene gives atactic polymer (the radical is planar and has no preference).
Example 1 Main

Verifying tacticity with CIP labels

In the model trimer drawn above (2,4,6-trichloroheptane-like segment, drawn as CH3–CHCl–CH2–CHCl–CH2–CHCl–CH3), the isotactic isomer has centres (R,S,R) or (S,R,S) in the first drawing. Explain why equivalent centres alternate in CIP label but are the "same" in tacticity.

Show solution
  1. In a meso-type diad the two centres are mirror images in CIP labels (R and S) but the geometric relationship is the same: both Cl on the same side when the chain is in the zig-zag plane. The label reverses because the substituents on the neighbouring carbons are ordered the other way along the chain (C2 sees CH3 on one side and CH2 on the other; C4 sees the reverse).
  2. Therefore isotactic = (R,S) alternation in the model = meso diads (m), and syndiotactic = same label repeating, (R,R) or (S,S) = racemic diads (r). RDKit check: C[C@H](Cl)C[C@H](Cl)C gives (S,R) = meso; C[C@H](Cl)C[C@@H](Cl)C gives (S,S) = racemic.

Concept Check C7 Checkpoint

Density

Explain in one sentence why LDPE is less dense than HDPE.

Show answer

LDPE chains carry short and long branches that prevent close packing, so it is less crystalline and less dense; HDPE chains are linear and pack in crystallites.
