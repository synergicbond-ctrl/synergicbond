AC-067 [Foundation] · Akshay topic Q67 · Status: Verified (standard definition)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, master-sheet page 25] Which is NOT a carbene? (a) :CH₂ (b) :CCl₂ (c) :C(Br)Cl (d) :CCl₃

Full solution
  1. Carbenes are neutral, divalent carbons with six electrons: :CH₂, :CCl₂ and :C(Br)Cl all fit.
  2. :CCl₃ has three bonds to a carbon with a lone pair: it is an anion (CCl₃⁻), not a neutral carbene.
  3. Option (d).

Answer: (d)

AC-066 [Application] · Akshay topic Q66 · Status: Verified (standard mesomeric-effect rules)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 25] In which compound does the mesomeric effect NOT operate? (a) anilinium with N⁺(CH₃)₃ (b) anisole-type O–CH₃ (c) benzenediazonium N≡N⁺ (d) acetanilide NH–C(=O)CH₃

Full solution
  1. (a) has no lone pair on the attached N (it is N⁺(CH₃)₃): no +M, and no π-acceptor either.
  2. (b), (c), (d) have an attached atom with a lone pair or π bond that conjugates with the ring.
  3. Option (a).

Answer: (a)

AC-074 [Application] · Akshay topic Q74 · Status: Partially verified (1,3-diketone trend from Ref. [2]; the dione pKₐ itself is not in the opened text)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 25] Strongest carbon acid among (a) cyclohexane-1,3-dione (b) CH₂(COOEt)₂ (c) CH₃COCH₂COOEt (d) bridged bicyclic diketone (no enol possible)

Full solution
  1. The most acidic C–H is between two C=O groups in a 1,3-diketone, whose enolate is strongly stabilised.
  2. The bridged bicyclic diketone (d) cannot enolise (anti-Bredt), so it is not acidic in this sense.
  3. Ordering from Ref. [2], p. 1182: 1,3-diketone (pKₐ ≈ 9) more acidic than ketoester (≈ 11) and malonate (≈ 13).

Answer: (a)
