1.7 Fructose, pentoses and other monosaccharides worth knowing

β-D-fructofuranose (in sucrose) D-fructose (open chain) β-D-ribofuranose 2-deoxy-β-D-ribofuranose
Ketose and pentose rings
  • D-Fructose (fruit sugar, laevulose; [α]D −92.4°). A 2-ketohexose; C-3 to C-5 same pattern as glucose. Free fructose is mostly β-pyranose (≈ 70 %) with a significant furanose share (β ≈ 22 %); in sucrose and inulin it is locked as β-D-fructofuranose. Gives the same osazone as glucose, a positive Seliwanoff test, and is not oxidised by Br2/H2O. Reduction with NaBH4 gives a mixture of D-glucitol and D-mannitol (the new C-2 centre can form either epimer).
  • D-Ribose (RNA) and 2-deoxy-D-ribose (DNA): aldopentoses present as β-furanoses. Deoxyribose has CH2 in place of C-2 CHOH; hence its formula is C5H10O4, not Cn(H2O)m. Test: ribose gives a blue-green Bial reaction.
  • D-Galactose: C-4 epimer of glucose; in lactose, cerebrosides. D-Mannose: C-2 epimer; in glycoproteins.
  • Deoxy sugars: L-fucose (6-deoxy-L-galactose), L-rhamnose (6-deoxy-L-mannose; C6H12O5).
  • Amino sugars: D-glucosamine (2-amino-2-deoxy-D-glucose); N-acetyl-D-glucosamine is the monomer of chitin (β-1,4-linked).
  • Uronic and aldonic acids: gluconic (C-1 oxidised), glucuronic (C-6 oxidised, detoxification by conjugation), glucaric (both).
  • Sugar alcohols (alditols): sorbitol, mannitol, xylitol, ribitol (in riboflavin): reduction products. Alditols from D-allose, D-galactose, D-ribose, D-xylose (palindromic codes) are meso.
  • Phosphorylated sugars: glucose-6-phosphate, fructose-1,6-bisphosphate; the C-6 (or C-1) –OH is esterified, never the anomeric –OH of the ring in a stable biologic.
Trap Ketoses give a positive Tollens/Fehling test only because in base the α-hydroxyketone tautomerises to an aldehyde (enediol). So the common statement "all reducing sugars have a free CHO group" is wrong: the test needs a free (or readily freed) hemiacetal/hemiketal OH.
Example 20 Adv

Alditols from the reduction of aldoses and ketoses

Reduction of which of these gives an optically inactive alditol: D-glucose, D-galactose, D-ribose, D-fructose, D-xylose?

Show solution
  1. An alditol HOCH2–[code]–CH2OH is meso if the code is a palindrome.
  2. D-Galactose RLLR ✓ (galactitol), D-ribose RRR ✓ (ribitol), D-xylose RLR ✓ (xylitol). D-Glucose RLRR gives glucitol (active). D-Fructose gives glucitol + mannitol (both active; the mixture shows rotation).
  3. Inactive: galactose, ribose, xylose.
Example 21 Main

Fructose or glucose?

Which of the two (a) is oxidised by bromine water to an acid containing the same number of carbon atoms, (b) forms a cherry-red colour with resorcinol/HCl in under two minutes?

Show solution
  1. (a) Glucose gives gluconic acid (C6H12O7); fructose is not oxidised.
  2. (b) Fructose (Seliwanoff).
