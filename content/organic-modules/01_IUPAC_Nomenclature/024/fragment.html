3.3 Cycloalkenes, cycloalkynes

  • The C=C carbons are C-1 and C-2 (the locant 1 is not written for a single ring double bond: cyclohexene).
  • Number through the double bond; choose the direction that gives substituents the lowest locant set.
  • With two double bonds, cyclohexa-1,3-diene (the a is inserted); with one double bond and a principal group, the principal group is C-1 and the double bond gets the lowest possible locant: cyclohex-2-en-1-ol (Part 4).
