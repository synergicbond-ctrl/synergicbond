8.6 Rings: cis/trans and R/S together

  • Ring substituents are described by cis (same face) and trans (opposite faces), which is acceptable, or by the full descriptors such as (1R,2S).
  • cis-1,2-Dimethylcyclohexane is (1R,2S) (meso). trans-1,2-Dimethylcyclohexane exists as (1R,2R) and (1S,2S), an enantiomeric pair.
  • For 1,4-disubstituted rings (1,4-dimethylcyclohexane) there are no stereocentres, but cis and trans isomers still exist (one plane of symmetry in both).
(1R,2S)-1,2-dimethylcyclohexane (1S,2S)-1,2-dimethylcyclohexane
cis-1,2-dimethylcyclohexane = (1R,2S), meso; trans-1,2-dimethylcyclohexane = (1S,2S) (and its enantiomer (1R,2R))
