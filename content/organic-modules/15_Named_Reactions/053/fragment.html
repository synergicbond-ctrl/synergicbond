48. Appel and Mitsunobu reactions (alcohol to halide or ester with inversion)

Appel: ROH + PPh3 + CBr4 (or CCl4) → R–Br (R–Cl) + Ph3P=O + CHBr3; the alcohol oxygen is activated as an alkoxyphosphonium ion, which the halide displaces by SN2 (inversion). Mitsunobu: ROH + PPh3 + DEAD (diethyl azodicarboxylate) + a pronucleophile H–Nu (a carboxylic acid, phthalimide, HN3) → R–Nu with inversion at a secondary carbinol carbon. Both are mild alternatives to SOCl2/HBr, and both are the classic ways to invert a stereocentre.

Example 45 Adv

Inverting an alcohol

Convert (R)-butan-2-ol into (S)-butan-2-ol in two steps.

Show solution
  1. Step 1 (Mitsunobu): PPh3, DEAD, benzoic acid gives (S)-sec-butyl benzoate (inversion at the carbinol carbon). Step 2: aqueous NaOH hydrolyses the ester by attack at the acyl carbon (the C–O bond of the stereocentre is not broken, retention) to give (S)-butan-2-ol and benzoate.
