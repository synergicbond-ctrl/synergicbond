Q206 Integer

Q207 Integer

Among the groups methylidene, ethylidene, ethenyl, ethynyl, prop-2-yn-1-yl and prop-2-en-1-yl: let A = number of alkylidene groups (attached by a double bond), B = number of alkenyl groups, K = number of alkynyl groups. Find A + 2B + 3K.

Q208 Integer

Q209 Integer
