16.6 Phthalein reaction

Phenol + phthalic anhydride + conc. H2SO4 (or ZnCl2), heated → phenolphthalein, which is colourless in acid and pink in alkaline solution (pH 8.3–10); it is the indicator for strong-base titrations. With resorcinol the product is fluorescein (strong green fluorescence). With the 1:2 stoichiometry two phenol molecules condense with the anhydride carbonyl.
