7.4 Aromatic side chains: benzene as parent or as substituent?

  • A chain attached to a ring: alkylbenzene (ethylbenzene, propylbenzene, isopropylbenzene = cumene). The ring is the parent.
  • When the principal group is on the chain, the chain is the parent and the ring is a phenyl prefix: 2-phenylethanol, 1-phenylpropan-2-ol, phenylmethanol (benzyl alcohol).
  • Unsaturated chains: ethenylbenzene (styrene), ethynylbenzene (phenylacetylene), (prop-2-en-1-yl)benzene (allylbenzene).
  • Several rings: diphenylmethane, triphenylmethane, biphenyl (1,1′-biphenyl), stilbene = 1,2-diphenylethene.
  • Benzyl is C₆H₅CH₂– and phenyl is C₆H₅–. Benzyl chloride is (chloromethyl)benzene; chlorobenzene is an aryl chloride.
Concept Link Benzyl chloride (halogen on an sp³ carbon next to the ring) undergoes substitution readily by SN1 or SN2, whereas chlorobenzene (halogen on an sp² ring carbon) is inert under the same conditions; the two names therefore label compounds with very different reactivity.
