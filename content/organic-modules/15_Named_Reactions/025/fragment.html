22. Darzens glycidic ester condensation

Reagents: an aldehyde or ketone + an α-halo ester (ClCH2CO2Et) + NaOEt or t-BuOK. Product: an α,β-epoxy ester (glycidic ester). Mechanism: the base makes the α-halo ester enolate, which adds to C=O (aldol step); the new alkoxide then displaces the neighbouring chloride intramolecularly (3-exo-tet SN2), closing the epoxide. Hydrolysis of the glycidic ester gives a glycidic acid which decarboxylates on heating with ring opening to give an aldehyde or ketone one carbon longer than the starting carbonyl compound (a chain-lengthening route to aldehydes).
