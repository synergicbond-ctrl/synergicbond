Mechanism (radical): the initiator adds to C-1 of the diene to give an allylic radical; the allylic radical adds the next monomer by its C-4 (1,4-addition) or C-2 (1,2). The 1,4 polymer has a C=C in each repeat unit (cis or trans), which is why rubbers can be vulcanised and why they react with ozone.
