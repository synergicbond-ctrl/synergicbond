How to use this chapter

This chapter is written as a mini-book. Part I (carbohydrates) is the largest because it carries the most JEE Advanced weight: Fischer and Haworth projections, structure proofs, osazone and chain-modification reactions, glycosides, di- and polysaccharides. Part II is amino acids and peptides (acid–base behaviour, pI calculations, synthesis and sequencing), Part III is proteins and enzymes, Part IV is nucleic acids, and Part V covers vitamins, hormones and lipids. Part VI gives every key mechanism step by step with an orbital view, and Part VII is the practice set: Level 1 (Main style, single answer or numerical), Level 2 (Advanced style: multiple correct, integer, matrix match) and Level 3 (paragraph and multi-step structure elucidation), every problem with a full solution.

Key idea Every structure in this chapter is drawn from SMILES strings that were checked with RDKit (CIP labels, formulae, stereo-isomer counts). Fischer and Haworth diagrams are generated from one Fischer code per sugar, and the codes were validated against the known CIP descriptors of glucose, mannose, galactose, ribose, arabinose, xylose, lyxose, erythrose, threose and fructose.
Shortcut Open the contents list above and work through a Part in order. Do the Examples with the solutions hidden first; every solution is written to show the reasoning, not just the answer.
