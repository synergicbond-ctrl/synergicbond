26. Thiols and thioethers (sulphur analogues)

Replacing O by S gives thiols (R–SH; formerly mercaptans) and thioethers/sulphides (R–S–R′). Named as alkanethiols (CH3SH methanethiol, C2H5SH ethanethiol, C6H5SH benzenethiol/thiophenol; –SH as prefix is “sulfanyl” or “mercapto-”) and as alkylsulfanylalkanes or dialkyl sulphides (CH3SCH3 dimethyl sulphide, methylsulfanylmethane).

Trap Thiols are more acidic than alcohols, although sulphur is less electronegative than oxygen. The S–H bond is weaker (longer, poorer orbital overlap) and the thiolate ion spreads its charge over a large, polarisable atom. Thiolate ions are excellent nucleophiles but weak bases, so they undergo SN2 even with secondary halides and cause little elimination.
Preparation of thiols: (i) RX + NaSH (excess) → RSH (excess HS− prevents RSR formation); (ii) thiourea route: RX + (NH2)2C=S → isothiouronium salt → NaOH → RSH + urea; (iii) Grignard + S8 then H3O+: RMgX + S → RSMgX → RSH; (iv) reduction of disulphides (Zn/H+).

  • Sulphonium salts: sulphides are good nucleophiles (S is soft): R2S + R′X → R2R′S+X−; trimethylsulphonium iodide, (CH3)3S+I−. S-Adenosylmethionine, a biological methylating agent, is a sulphonium ion.
  • Mustard gas, S(CH2CH2Cl)2 (bis(2-chloroethyl) sulphide): a vesicant (blistering agent). The sulphur displaces the β-chloride intramolecularly (neighbouring-group participation) to give an episulphonium (thiiranium) ion, a strained, very reactive alkylating agent. Prepared from ethylene and S2Cl2, or from thiodiglycol and HCl.
  • Thioacetals (dithianes): an aldehyde + 1,3-propanedithiol (BF3) gives 1,3-dithiane, whose C–H is acidic (pKa about 31) and can be alkylated: carbonyl umpolung. Raney Ni then removes sulphur (desulphurisation: C=O → CH2).

Example 74 L1 Foundation

Thiol or alcohol?

Which is (a) a stronger acid: C2H5OH or C2H5SH; (b) higher boiling; (c) a better nucleophile as its anion; (d) more easily oxidised to a disulphide?

Show solution
  1. (a) C2H5SH (pKa about 10.5 vs 16). (b) C2H5OH (78 °C vs 35 °C; the O–H···O hydrogen bond is much stronger). (c) C2H5S− (larger, more polarisable). (d) C2H5SH (alcohols are not oxidised to peroxides in this way; mild oxidants give RSSR).
Example 75 L2 Main

Sulphur oxidation ladder

Give the products when dimethyl sulphide is treated with (a) one equivalent of H2O2 or NaIO4; (b) excess H2O2; (c) CH3I.

Show solution
  1. (a) Dimethyl sulphoxide (DMSO), (CH3)2S=O. (b) Dimethyl sulphone, (CH3)2SO2. (c) Trimethylsulphonium iodide, (CH3)3S+I−, by SN2 on CH3I (S is a good nucleophile).
