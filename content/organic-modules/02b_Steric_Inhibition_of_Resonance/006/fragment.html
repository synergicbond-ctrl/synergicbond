B. Basicity

Problem 39 [Application]

Arrange in decreasing base strength in water: p-Nitroaniline, Ethylamine, Ammonia, Triethylamine. Use pKₐ of the conjugate acid (larger = stronger base).

Worked solution
  1. Strength of a base = pKₐ of its conjugate acid (larger = stronger base).
  2. Triethylamine: pKₐH 10.76.
  3. Ethylamine: pKₐH 10.64.
  4. Ammonia: pKₐH 9.26.
  5. p-Nitroaniline: pKₐH 1.

Answer: Decreasing basicity: Triethylamine > Ethylamine > Ammonia > p-Nitroaniline.
