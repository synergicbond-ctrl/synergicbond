Block O. Paragraph-based problems (296–313)

Practice 296 Paragraph 1

Compound X, C8H8O, gives no silver mirror but gives a yellow precipitate with I2/NaOH. X with Zn–Hg/HCl gives Y. X with mCPBA gives Z, which on hydrolysis gives phenol and W. Q: X is (A) benzaldehyde (B) acetophenone (C) phenylethanal (D) 4-methylbenzaldehyde.

Show answer
  1. The iodoform test shows CH3CO–; the negative Tollens test excludes an aldehyde; C8H8O with a phenyl: (B) acetophenone. The aldehydes (A), (C), (D) would give a silver mirror and no iodoform.
Practice 297 Paragraph 1

(Same X as above.) Y is: (A) ethylbenzene (B) 1-phenylethanol (C) styrene (D) toluene.

Show answer
  1. Clemmensen reduction converts C=O to CH2: (A) ethylbenzene.
Practice 298 Paragraph 1

(Same X.) In X → Z the group that migrates, and W, are: (A) CH3, benzoic acid (B) phenyl, ethanoic acid (C) phenyl, benzoic acid (D) CH3, methanol.

Show answer
  1. Baeyer–Villiger on acetophenone: phenyl > methyl, giving phenyl ethanoate (Z); hydrolysis gives phenol + ethanoic acid (W). Answer (B).
Practice 299 Paragraph 2

An aldehyde A (C5H10O) reduces Tollens' reagent but does not undergo aldol reaction. With concentrated NaOH it gives B and C. Q: A is (A) pentanal (B) 3-methylbutanal (C) 2,2-dimethylpropanal (D) 2-methylbutanal.

Show answer
  1. No α-H: (C) 2,2-dimethylpropanal, (CH3)3CCHO. The others have α-hydrogens.
Practice 300 Paragraph 2

(Same A.) B (alcohol) and C (acid, after acidification) are: (A) neopentyl alcohol and pivalic acid (B) 2-methylbutan-1-ol and 2-methylbutanoic acid (C) pentan-1-ol and pentanoic acid (D) neopentyl alcohol and 3-methylbutanoic acid.

Show answer
  1. Cannizzaro: (A) (CH3)3CCH2OH and (CH3)3CCOOH.
Practice 301 Paragraph 2

(Same A.) A is heated with HCHO and concentrated NaOH. Which pair forms: (A) (CH3)3CCH2OH + HCOO− (B) (CH3)3CCOO− + CH3OH (C) (CH3)3CCH2OH + (CH3)3CCOO− only (D) no reaction.

Show answer
  1. Crossed Cannizzaro: HCHO is oxidised and A is reduced: (A).
Practice 302 Paragraph 3

Aniline is diazotised (A), treated with CuCN to give B, hydrolysed to C, converted by SOCl2 to D, by NH3 to E, and finally treated with Br2/NaOH to give F. Q: the molecular formula of B is (A) C6H5N (B) C7H5N (C) C7H7N (D) C6H4N2.

Show answer
  1. B = benzonitrile, C6H5CN = C7H5N (B).
Practice 303 Paragraph 3

(Same sequence.) E is (A) benzoic anhydride (B) benzamide (C) benzylamine (D) phenylacetamide.

Show answer
  1. D = benzoyl chloride; E = benzamide (B).
Practice 304 Paragraph 3

(Same sequence.) F is (A) benzylamine (B) aniline (C) benzamide (D) phenol. What is the net carbon change from aniline to F, and what does the sequence illustrate?

Show answer
  1. Hofmann degradation removes the carbon that Sandmeyer cyanation had added: F = aniline (B); net change 0 carbons: C6 → C7 (CuCN) → C6 (Hofmann). It shows that Sandmeyer cyanation adds one carbon and Hofmann removes one.
Practice 305 Paragraph 4

Acetone is reduced by Mg–Hg in benzene to P (C6H14O2); P with H2SO4, heat, gives Q (C6H12O); Q with I2/NaOH gives R and a yellow solid. Q: P is (A) hexane-2,5-diol (B) 2,3-dimethylbutane-2,3-diol (C) 2-methylpentane-2,4-diol (D) hexane-1,6-diol.

Show answer
  1. Pinacol coupling: (B) (CH3)2C(OH)C(OH)(CH3)2.
Practice 306 Paragraph 4

(Same.) Q is (A) 2,3-dimethylbutanal (B) 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-one (C) hexan-2-one (D) 4-methylpentan-2-one.

Show answer
  1. Pinacol rearrangement: (B) pinacolone (CH3CO–C(CH3)3); it has CH3CO–, in agreement with the iodoform test.
Practice 307 Paragraph 4

(Same.) R is (A) pivalic acid (2,2-dimethylpropanoic acid) as its sodium salt (B) ethanoic acid (C) 2-methylpropanoic acid (D) benzoic acid. Moles of CHI3 per mole of Q?

Show answer
  1. Haloform: (CH3)3C–COONa (A), with 1 mol CHI3 per mole of Q.
Practice 308 Paragraph 5

A tertiary alcohol, 3-methylhexan-3-ol, has three different groups on the carbinol carbon: CH3, C2H5, C3H7. Q: how many distinct ketone + Grignard routes make it?

Show answer
  1. A ketone has two of the three groups and the Grignard supplies the third: 3 routes: butan-2-one + C3H7MgX; pentan-2-one + C2H5MgX; hexan-3-one + CH3MgX.
Practice 309 Paragraph 5

(Same alcohol.) How many ester + excess Grignard routes exist?

Show answer
  1. An ester gives a tertiary alcohol with two identical groups from the Grignard; the target has three different groups, so 0 routes.
Practice 310 Paragraph 5

3-Ethylpentan-3-ol, (C2H5)3COH, can be made from how many different types of carbonyl compound (ketone, ester, carbonate) with ethylmagnesium bromide?

Show answer
  1. Ketone (pentan-3-one + 1 EtMgBr), ester (ethyl propanoate + 2 EtMgBr), carbonate (diethyl carbonate + 3 EtMgBr): 3 types.
Practice 311 Paragraph 6

9.8 g of cyclohexanone (M = 98) is converted by methyl vinyl ketone/NaOEt (Robinson annulation) into the bicyclic enone C10H14O (M = 150) in 60 % yield. Q: the moles of water formed in the overall Robinson sequence per mole of product?

Show answer
  1. Michael step: no water; aldol condensation: one water. 1 mol H2O per mole of product.
Practice 312 Paragraph 6

(Same.) Mass of the enone formed?

Show answer
  1. n = 9.8/98 = 0.100 mol; × 0.60 = 0.0600 mol × 150 = 9.0 g.
Practice 313 Paragraph 6

(Same.) Number of rings and π bonds in the product?

Show answer
  1. 2 rings and 2 π bonds (C=C and C=O).
