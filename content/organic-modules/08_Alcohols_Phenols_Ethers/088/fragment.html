Example 64 L3 Advanced

Structure from cleavage data

A compound A (C9H12O) does not dissolve in NaOH and does not react with Na. With excess HI it gives a phenol B (C6H6O, mass 94) and an alkyl iodide C (C3H7I); C with alcoholic KOH gives propene, and with NaOH(aq) it gives an alcohol that is oxidised by PCC to propanal. Identify A, B and C.

Show solution
  1. A is an aryl alkyl ether (no acidic H, not an alcohol). C9H12O − C6H5O = C3H7: a propyl group.
  2. The alkyl iodide C is converted by aqueous NaOH to an alcohol that PCC oxidises to propanal; so the alcohol is propan-1-ol and C = 1-iodopropane (this excludes isopropyl). Elimination with alcoholic KOH gives propene ✓ (consistent).
  3. B = phenol. A = propoxybenzene (phenyl n-propyl ether), C6H5OCH2CH2CH3: C9H12O ✓.
