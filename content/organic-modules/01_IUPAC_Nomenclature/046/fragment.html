5.7 Common mistakes and how to test for them

  • Choosing the amino or hydroxy group as the suffix when an acid, ester, nitrile, aldehyde or ketone is present. Test: write the seniority list before naming.
  • Using oxo for a CHO group on a branch. If the CHO carbon is not in the parent chain, use formyl.
  • Counting the nitrile carbon inconsistently. With the nitrile as the principal group its carbon is in the chain (ethanenitrile has two carbons). As the prefix cyano (because a higher group is present) its carbon is outside the chain: in 4-cyanobutanoic acid the chain has four carbons and the CN carbon is a fifth, extra carbon.
  • Taking the longest chain without checking the number of principal groups (citric acid, 2-ethylpropane-1,3-diol).
  • Wrong direction from "lowest sum" (Section 2.2).
