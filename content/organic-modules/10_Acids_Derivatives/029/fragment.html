Which of the following are stronger acids than benzoic acid (pKa 4.20)? (A) o-toluic acid (B) p-toluic acid (C) m-nitrobenzoic acid (D) p-methoxybenzoic acid

Show answer
  1. (A) o-Toluic acid: ortho effect (steric inhibition of resonance), pKa 3.91 < 4.20: stronger. (B) p-Toluic: +I/hyperconjugation weakens (4.36). (C) m-Nitro: only –I acts, pKa 3.45: stronger. (D) p-Methoxy: +M dominates at para (4.47): weaker.
  2. A and C.
