Most multi-step questions are paths between alkane ⇄ alkene ⇄ alkyne ⇄ alcohol/halide/carbonyl. Keep this chain of "oxidation levels" in mind:

43.1 Retrosynthesis habits that win marks

  • Identify carbon count first: coupling (Wurtz, Kolbe, acetylide alkylation, Corey–House) adds carbons; decarboxylation, ozonolysis (=CH2), hot KMnO4 (=CH2) remove one.
  • Identify the position of the functional group: Markovnikov (H3O+, Hg(OAc)2) vs anti-Markovnikov (BH3, HBr/ROOR); to move a halogen from C-2 to C-1, go via the alkene (alc. KOH then HBr/ROOR).
  • Identify the stereochemistry: syn (H2, OsO4, BH3, carbenes) vs anti (X2, epoxide opening, Na/NH3); cis vs trans from alkyne reductions.
  • Watch for rearrangement: avoid carbocation routes if the skeleton can rearrange; use oxymercuration, hydroboration or radical routes.
Example 57 Advanced

Move the functional group

Convert propan-1-ol into propan-2-ol; then propan-2-ol back into propan-1-ol.

Show solution
  1. Propan-1-ol → propene (conc. H2SO4, 170 °C) → propan-2-ol (H3O+, Markovnikov; or Hg(OAc)2/H2O, NaBH4).
  2. Propan-2-ol → propene (conc. H2SO4, 100 °C) → propan-1-ol (BH3·THF; H2O2, OH−).
Example 58 Advanced

Chain extension by two carbons

Make but-2-yne from ethyne, and from it (a) cis-but-2-ene (b) butan-2-one (c) racemic 2,3-dibromobutane (d) meso-butane-2,3-diol.

Show solution
  1. HC≡CH → (NaNH2; CH3I) → CH3C≡CH → (NaNH2; CH3I) → CH3C≡CCH3.
  2. (a) H2/Lindlar. (b) H2O, HgSO4, H2SO4. (c) cis-but-2-ene (a) then Br2/CCl4 (anti on cis → racemic). (d) cis-but-2-ene then OsO4, NaHSO3 (syn on cis → meso).
Example 59 Advanced

An aldehyde with two more carbons

Convert 1-bromopropane into pentanal using alkyne chemistry.

Show solution
  1. CH3CH2CH2Br + NaC≡CH → CH3CH2CH2C≡CH (pent-1-yne, 5 carbons).
  2. Hydroboration with 9-BBN (or Sia2BH), then H2O2/NaOH, puts OH on the terminal carbon; the enol tautomerises to pentanal, CH3CH2CH2CH2CHO.
  3. (Hg2+/H3O+ hydration would give pentan-2-one instead.)
Example 60 Advanced

Isobutane → tert-butyl alcohol and isobutyl alcohol

How can 2-methylpropane be converted to (a) 2-methylpropan-2-ol (b) 2-methylpropan-1-ol?

Show solution
  1. (a) Br2, hν (3° selective: tert-butyl bromide) → aq. KOH (SN1 substitution): tert-butyl alcohol.
  2. (b) tert-butyl bromide → alc. KOH → 2-methylpropene → BH3; H2O2, OH− → 2-methylpropan-1-ol.
Example 61 Advanced

Propane to propyne and to acetone

Propane → propyne (three steps), then → propanone.

Show solution
  1. Propane + Br2, hν → 2-bromopropane (major) → alc. KOH → propene → Br2/CCl4 → 1,2-dibromopropane → 3 NaNH2, then H2O → propyne. Hydration (HgSO4/H2SO4) gives propanone.
