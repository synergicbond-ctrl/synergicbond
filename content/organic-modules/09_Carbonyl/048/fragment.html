41. Summary sheet for Parts F and G

  • C=O → CH2: Clemmensen (Zn–Hg/HCl; acid-stable), Wolff–Kishner (N2H4/KOH, 473 K; base-stable), Mozingo (thioacetal/Raney Ni; neutral).
  • C=O → CHOH: NaBH4 (aldehydes, ketones), LiAlH4 (also esters, acids), MPV, H2/Ni.
  • RCHO → RCOO−: Tollens', Fehling's (aliphatic only), KMnO4, Br2/H2O; ketones inert.
  • Baeyer–Villiger: ketone → ester (O inserted); migrating group = better carbocation stabiliser; retention; H > t-alkyl > cHex > s-alkyl ≈ benzyl > Ph > p-alkyl > Me.
  • α,β-Unsaturated: hard Nu → 1,2; soft Nu → 1,4; Cu catalysis converts RMgX to 1,4; Luche → 1,2 reduction; H2/Pd reduces C=C first.
