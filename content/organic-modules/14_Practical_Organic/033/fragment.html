10.2 Alkyl and aryl halides

Tests for halides
TestReagentObservationInterpretation
Alcoholic AgNO3AgNO3 in ethanol, warmPrecipitate of AgX (white/yellow)Reactivity by SN1: 3° > 2° > 1°; RI > RBr > RCl; allyl, benzyl, tertiary give ppt in the cold. Aryl and vinyl halides: no ppt

Alcoholic AgNO3 and NaI/acetone give opposite orders for 1° vs 3° because the first is an SN1-type test and the second an SN2-type test.

Example 16 Adv

Order of reactivity in two tests

Arrange in decreasing speed of precipitate formation with (i) alcoholic AgNO3, (ii) NaI in acetone: benzyl chloride, chlorobenzene, tert-butyl chloride, n-butyl chloride.

Show solution
  1. (i) SN1 type (carbocation): benzyl chloride (stabilised benzylic cation) ≈ tert-butyl chloride > n-butyl chloride > chlorobenzene (no reaction: C(sp2)–Cl, the lone pair is delocalised and the aryl cation is unstable).
  2. (ii) SN2 type: benzyl chloride > n-butyl chloride > tert-butyl chloride (hindered) > chlorobenzene (no reaction).
  3. The two tests rank n-butyl and tert-butyl chloride in opposite orders.
