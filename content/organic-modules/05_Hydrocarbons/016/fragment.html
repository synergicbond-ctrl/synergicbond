15. Summary sheet: cycloalkanes

  • Baeyer: planar rings, d = ½(109°28′ − internal angle). Valid for 3–5, fails for ≥ 6 because rings pucker.
  • Strain (kJ mol-1): cyclopropane 115, cyclobutane 110, cyclopentane 26, cyclohexane 0, cycloheptane 26, cyclooctane 40. Heat of combustion per CH2: 697, 686, 664, 659.
  • Cyclopropane reacts like an alkene (H2/Ni 80 °C, HBr, Br2/Lewis acid: ring-opening); cyclobutane needs 200 °C H2/Ni; cyclopentane, cyclohexane do not open.
  • Cyclohexane chair: no strain; axial and equatorial interchange by ring flip (barrier 45 kJ). Substituents prefer the equatorial position (A-values: CH3 1.74, t-Bu > 4 kcal mol-1).
  • 1,2 / 1,4: trans = ee; 1,3: cis = ee. Energy difference between the cis and trans dimethyl isomers ≈ 7.6 kJ.
  • trans-Decalin is rigid, more stable; cis-decalin flips.
