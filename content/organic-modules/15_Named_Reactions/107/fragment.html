Practice 231 Adv MCQ

In which of the following rearrangements is an isocyanate an intermediate? (A) Hofmann (B) Curtius (C) Beckmann (D) Lossen

Show answer
  1. A, B, D. The migrating group moves from carbon to nitrogen with loss of Br−, N2 or a carboxylate, giving R–N=C=O. The Beckmann rearrangement gives a nitrilium ion R–C≡N+–R′, then an amide.
Practice 232 Adv MCQ

Ethanal is added slowly to an excess of 2,2-dimethylpropanal in dilute NaOH. Which are formed (the second after warming)? (A) (CH3)3C–CH(OH)–CH2CHO (B) (CH3)3C–CH=CH–CHO (C) CH3CH(OH)CH2CHO as the major product (D) (CH3)3CCH2OH

Show answer
  1. A, B. Only ethanal can form the enolate; with the non-enolisable aldehyde in excess it attacks that aldehyde. The aldol dehydrates on warming to 4,4-dimethylpent-2-enal. (C) is the minor self-aldol. (D) is a Cannizzaro product that needs conc. NaOH.
