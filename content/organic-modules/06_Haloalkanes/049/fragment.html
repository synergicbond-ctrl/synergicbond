44. Appendix: sources cross-checked

Every item listed here was actually opened during the preparation of this chapter. Examples and practice problems are original; numerical data and rules were cross-checked against these sources, not copied.

  • Organic Chemistry, 9th edition, L. G. Wade (PDF). Pages around 209 (activation energies and relative rates of halogen-atom abstraction from methane), 363–369 (stereochemistry of E2, Zaitsev/Hofmann examples with ethoxide and tert-butoxide, comparison table of E1 and E2). Used to cross-check: the 71 : 29 and 28 : 72 product ratios for 2-bromo-2-methylbutane; the anti-coplanar requirement; the kinetic isotope effect in chlorination (12 : 1); the types of substitution-versus-elimination problem for tertiary and cyclic halides.
  • Organic Chemistry, 2nd edition, Clayden, Greeves and Warren (PDF). Pages around 426 (E2 in a cyclohexyl chloride, hindered-base orientation, 69 : 31 and 28 : 73 ethoxide/tert-butoxide ratios), 550–552 (benzyne mechanism, regiochemistry with methoxy substituents, benzyne from diazotised anthranilic acid, steric and electronic reasons), 959–960 (neighbouring-group participation in the acetolysis of trans-2-acetoxycyclohexyl tosylate). Used to cross-check the benzyne and NGP sections.
  • David Klein assignment set: "Alkyl Halides: Nucleophilic Substitution and Elimination Reactions" (problem list). Used only to identify problem types (drawing transition states, counting alkenes, E2 stereochemistry, retrosynthesis of ethers, SN2 dealkylation of esters, ambident naphthoxide alkylation, solvolysis of substituted benzyl halides); none of its problems is reproduced.
  • Internal brief (LONG_BRIEF.md) and the earlier chapter notes (org/06_haloalkanes.txt). Used for the style, build rules and the original short list of topics.

Opened but not found useful for this chapter: Himanshu Pandey, Advanced Problems (no text hits for alkyl-halide topics in the text layer; possibly scanned images, so it could not be searched). Not opened: Peter Sykes, March, Nasipuri, M. S. Chouhan, McMurry, Finar, Eliel, Kalsi, the ICHO compilations, the user's coaching-test PDFs and any JEE Advanced past papers. Statements in this chapter that are not tied to the pages above come from the author's own knowledge and from RDKit/Python checks (structures, CIP labels, isomer counts, numerical results); literature numbers such as relative rates and product percentages are quoted as approximate.
