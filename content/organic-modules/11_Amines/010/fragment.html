10. Hofmann bromamide (Hofmann degradation)

A primary amide RCONH2 (R = alkyl or aryl) is heated with Br2 and aqueous NaOH or KOH (equivalently NaOBr). The product is a primary amine with one carbon less.

Br₂ + 4 NaOH, heat⟶
Ethanamide → methylamine: RCONH₂ + Br₂ + 4 NaOH → RNH₂ + Na₂CO₃ + 2 NaBr + 2 H₂O

Mechanism (high-yield for JEE Advanced).

  • Step 1: base removes N–H from the amide; the anion attacks Br2 giving the N-bromoamide RCONHBr.
  • Step 2: base removes the second N–H. The anion RCON−Br loses Br− while R migrates from carbon to nitrogen in one concerted step, giving the isocyanate R–N=C=O (the key intermediate; no free nitrene).
  • Step 3: hydroxide adds to the isocyanate to give the carbamate R–NH–COO−.
  • Step 4: the carbamate loses CO2 (taken up by base as carbonate) to give RNH2.
OH⁻, −Br⁻ (CH₃ migrates)⟶ OH⁻, H₂O⟶ −CO₂⟶
N-Bromoethanamide → methyl isocyanate → carbamate → methylamine

Key facts:

  • The migrating carbon keeps its configuration (retention): (S)-RCONH2 gives an amine of the same spatial arrangement.
  • Alkyl and aryl groups both migrate; the carbonyl carbon is lost as carbonate. n carbons in the amide → n − 1 in the amine.
  • If the isocyanate is trapped by methanol (NaOMe/MeOH) the product is a carbamate RNHCOOMe, not the amine.
  • The same R–N=C=O intermediate appears in Curtius, Lossen and Schmidt reactions.
Br₂, NaOH⟶
2-Methylpropanamide → propan-2-amine (the isopropyl group migrates)
Br₂, KOH⟶
Benzamide → aniline
Trap Count carbons in the amide, not in the acid chain you imagine. Phenylacetamide, PhCH2CONH2 (8 C), gives benzylamine, PhCH2NH2 (7 C). Benzamide (7 C) gives aniline (6 C). Hofmann degradation therefore cannot give a benzylic amine from benzamide.
Example 12 Main

Carbon bookkeeping

Compound A (C3H7NO) on heating with Br2/KOH gives B (C2H7N), which gives a positive carbylamine test. Identify A and B.

Show solution
  1. A has one carbon more than B, so A is an amide RCONH2 with R = C2H5: propanamide, CH3CH2CONH2 (C3H7NO ✓).
  2. B = ethanamine, CH3CH2NH2 (C2H7N ✓), a 1° amine, hence carbylamine positive.
Example 13 Adv

Retention of configuration

(S)-2-Methylbutanamide undergoes Hofmann degradation. Give the amine and its descriptor.

Show solution
  1. The sec-butyl group migrates with retention. The amine is butan-2-amine.
  2. In the amide the priorities at the stereocentre are CONH2 > C2H5 > CH3 > H; in the amine they are NH2 > C2H5 > CH3 > H. The new N occupies exactly the position the carbonyl carbon had and still has highest priority, so the descriptor is unchanged: (S)-butan-2-amine.
