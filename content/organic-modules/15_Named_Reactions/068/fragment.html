62. Claisen rearrangement ([3,3]-sigmatropic)

Aromatic Claisen: allyl aryl ether, heated alone or in N,N-diethylaniline at ≈ 473 K (200 °C) → o-allylphenol. Aliphatic (allyl vinyl ether): gives a γ,δ-unsaturated aldehyde or ketone. The reaction is a concerted [3,3]-sigmatropic shift through a chair-like six-membered transition state: the C–O bond breaks while a new C–C bond forms between the allyl terminal (γ) carbon and the ortho carbon (or the vinyl terminus). Features: (1) Allylic inversion: the carbon that ends up on the ring is the former γ-carbon of the allyl group: a crotyl (but-2-enyl) ether gives the *branched* 1-methylallyl (but-3-en-2-yl) product, and a 1-methylallyl ether gives the linear crotyl product. (2) The first product is a non-aromatic cyclohexa-2,4-dienone which enolises (tautomerises) to the phenol: this is the driving force. (3) If both ortho positions are occupied the allyl group moves on by a second [3,3] shift (Cope) to the para position. (4) Unlike the Fries, it needs no catalyst; and unlike carbocation rearrangements, it is intramolecular and concerted.

473 K, N,N-diethylaniline⟶
Allyl phenyl ether → 2-allylphenol
Δ⟶
Crotyl phenyl ether → 2-(but-3-en-2-yl)phenol: branched product (allylic inversion at the γ-carbon)
Δ⟶
Both ortho positions blocked: the allyl group arrives at the para position (4-allyl-2,6-dimethylphenol) through a Claisen then a Cope shift
Δ (aliphatic Claisen)⟶
Allyl vinyl ether → pent-4-enal
Shortcut Number the atoms of the six-atom array 1-2-3 / 1′-2′-3′: C=C–C–O–C=C (vinyl–O–allyl). The bond between atoms 3 and 3′ (the C–O bond) breaks, and a bond between atoms 1 and 1′ (the two terminal carbons) forms; the double bonds shift to 2–3 and 2′–3′ positions. This recipe also applies to the Cope.
Example 57 Main

Claisen products

Predict the product of heating (a) allyl phenyl ether, (b) 1-methylallyl phenyl ether PhO–CH(CH3)CH=CH2.

Show solution
  1. (a) 2-Allylphenol (o-allylphenol; further heating or catalysts can give para only if both ortho positions are blocked).
  2. (b) The allyl terminal carbon (CH2=) bonds to the ring ortho carbon, and the double bond moves into the middle: PhO–CH(CH3)–CH=CH2 → 2-(CH2–CH=CH–CH3)phenol = 2-(but-2-enyl)phenol (crotyl, trans).
