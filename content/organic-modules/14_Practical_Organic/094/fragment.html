Practice 205 Test 2 · numerical

0.42 g of a compound gave 39.0 mL of N2 collected over water at 298 K and 752 mm Hg (aqueous tension 23.8 mm Hg). Find %N.

Show answer
  1. P(N2) = 752 − 23.8 = 728.2 mm Hg.
  2. V0 = (728.2 × 39.0 × 273)/(760 × 298) = 34.23 mL.
  3. %N = 28 × 34.23/22 400/0.42 × 100 = 10.19.

Answer: 10.19

Practice 206 Test 2 · numerical

0.350 g of an amine was subjected to Kjeldahl digestion. The ammonia was absorbed in 30.0 mL of 0.250 M H2SO4; the excess acid needed 15.0 mL of 0.400 M NaOH. Find %N.

Show answer
  1. meq acid = 30.0 × 0.500 = 15.0; meq NaOH = 15.0 × 0.400 = 6.00.
  2. meq NH3 = 15.0 − 6.00 = 9.00 ⇒ N = 126 mg.
  3. %N = 0.126/0.350 × 100 = 36.00.

Answer: 36.00

Practice 207 Test 2 · single-correct

A compound contains C 54.54 %, H 9.09 %, O 36.36 %; its vapour density is 44. Its molecular formula is: (A) C2H4O (B) C4H8O2 (C) C3H6O2 (D) C4H8O

Show answer
  1. C 4.545; H 9.09; O 2.27 ⇒ 2 : 4 : 1 ⇒ C2H4O (44). M = 88 ⇒ n = 2 ⇒ C4H8O2.

Answer: (B)

Practice 208 Test 2 · numerical

0.5000 g of an organic compound containing bromine only as halogen gave 0.4700 g AgBr by the Carius method. Find %Br (two decimals).

Show answer
  1. %Br = (80/188)(0.4700/0.5000) × 100 = 40.00.

Answer: 40.00

Practice 211 Test 2 · matching

Match each compound with the IR band that best identifies it. P: ethanol; Q: acetone; R: acetic acid; S: propanenitrile. 1: 2250 cm-1; 2: very broad 2500-3300 with 1710; 3: broad 3300 with no C=O; 4: strong 1715 with no O–H

  1. Ethanol: O–H only (3). Acetone: C=O (4). Acetic acid: broad acid O–H and C=O (2). Propanenitrile: C≡N (1).

Practice 212 Test 2 · paragraph

C4H8O2: IR 1740 cm-1; no O–H. ¹H NMR: δ 4.1 (q, 2H), 2.05 (s, 3H), 1.25 (t, 3H). (i) DBE? (ii) Functional group? (iii) Structure? (iv) How many ¹³C signals? (v) Product on alkaline hydrolysis that gives iodoform?

Practice 213 Test 2 · numerical

10 mL of a gaseous hydrocarbon was exploded with 70 mL of O2; after cooling the volume was 55 mL, and 25 mL after KOH. Find the number of hydrogen atoms per molecule.

Show answer
  1. CO2 = 55 − 25 = 30 mL ⇒ x = 3. Contraction = 80 − 55 = 25 mL = 10(1 + y/4) ⇒ y = 6 (C3H6). Check: O2 used = 10(3 + 1.5) = 45 mL; left 25 mL ✓.

Answer: 6

Practice 214 Test 2 · numerical

0.9150 g of the silver salt of a dibasic acid on ignition left 0.5990 g of silver. Find the molar mass of the acid (Ag = 108).

Show answer
  1. Equivalent mass of salt = (0.9150/0.6555) × 108 = 165.0.
  2. Equivalent mass of acid = 165.0 − 107 = 58.0; dibasic ⇒ M = 116.

Answer: 116

Practice 215 Test 2 · numerical

0.30 g of a solute in 20 g of benzene lowers the freezing point by 0.64 K (Kf = 5.12 K kg mol-1). Find the molar mass.

Show answer
  1. M = 5.12 × 0.30 × 1000/(20 × 0.64) = 120.

Answer: 120

Practice 216 Test 2 · single-correct

The mass spectrum of an organic compound shows M+• at m/z 112 and 114 in a 3 : 1 ratio. It contains: (A) one bromine (B) one chlorine (C) two chlorines (D) one iodine

Show answer
  1. 3 : 1 for M and M+2 is the ³⁵Cl : ³⁷Cl ratio for one chlorine (chlorobenzene, 112/114). Two Cl would give 9 : 6 : 1; Br gives about 1 : 1; I has no M+2.

Answer: (B)

The C=O stretching frequency is highest in: (A) acetamide (B) acetone (C) acetyl chloride (D) acetophenone

  1. Acid chloride (~1800 cm-1) > acetone (1715) > acetophenone (1685) > amide (~1650).

Practice 219 Test 2 · descriptive

A compound C9H10O gives an orange ppt with 2,4-DNP, no silver mirror, a yellow ppt with I2/NaOH; ¹H NMR δ 7.2 (m, 5H), 3.7 (s, 2H), 2.1 (s, 3H). Deduce the structure, show DBE and explain each observation.

Show answer
  1. DBE = (18 + 2 − 10)/2 = 5 (ring + C=O). 2,4-DNP: C=O; no Tollens': ketone; iodoform: CH3CO–. NMR: monosubstituted phenyl (5H), CH2 between phenyl and C=O (3.7, s), CH3CO (2.1, s). Structure: C6H5CH2COCH3, phenylacetone (1-phenylpropan-2-one).
