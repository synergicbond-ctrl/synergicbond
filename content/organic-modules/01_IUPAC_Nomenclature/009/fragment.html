The first four roots (meth, eth, prop, but) are historical, from methyl alcohol, ether, propionic acid and butyric acid; from five upward they are Greek numerals (non- is Latin). The root for 20 is icos- in the 2013 spelling (eicosane in older books).

Exam Trap bis, tris and tetrakis are used for complex (substituted) groups and whenever di, tri would be ambiguous. Two 2-chloroethyl groups are bis(2-chloroethyl), never di(2-chloroethyl). Two dimethylamino groups are bis(dimethylamino): plain di would read as "two methyl groups on the amino group".
