  • Azo dyes are the largest class of synthetic dyes. They are made on the fibre (ingrain dyes: cotton is dipped in β-naphthol then in a diazonium salt, e.g. "para red" from p-nitroaniline).
  • Dyes need solubility (–SO3Na), fixing groups, and a chromophore–auxochrome system.
