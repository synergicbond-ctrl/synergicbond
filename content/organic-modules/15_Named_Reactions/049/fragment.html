44. Birch reduction (arene → 1,4-cyclohexadiene)

Reagents: Na or Li in liquid NH3 (at −33 °C), with an alcohol (EtOH or t-BuOH) as proton source. Mechanism: electron transfer to the ring gives a radical anion (blue colour of solvated electrons), protonation by ROH, a second electron, a second protonation: net addition of two H atoms at the 1,4-positions to give a non-conjugated 1,4-cyclohexadiene (the product is not reduced further, because isolated C=C is inert to Na/NH3). Trap Do not confuse the Birch reduction (Na/NH3 on an aromatic ring, 1,4-diene) with Na/NH3 on an alkyne (trans-alkene) or with catalytic hydrogenation of benzene (cyclohexane at high pressure/temperature, no intermediate stops).
