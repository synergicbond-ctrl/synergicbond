E. Hyperconjugation

Problem 91 [Application]

How many hyperconjugative (no-bond) structures can be written for CH₃CH₂⁺?

Worked solution
  1. Count the α-hydrogens (H on the carbon attached to the cationic, sp² or ring π-system).

Answer: Total = 3.

Problem 98 [Advanced]

Compare the number of hyperconjugative structures of (CH₃)₃C⁺ and CH₃CH₂⁺ and state which is more stabilised by hyperconjugation.

Worked solution
  1. (CH₃)₃C⁺: 9; CH₃CH₂⁺: 3.

Answer: (CH₃)₃C⁺ is more stabilised by hyperconjugation.
