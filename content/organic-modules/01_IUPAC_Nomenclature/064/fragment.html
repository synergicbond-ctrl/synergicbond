8.1 Where descriptors go

Definition. A name that does not specify the configuration names every stereoisomer of the constitution. Stereodescriptors are written in parentheses at the front of the name, each with the locant of the atom or bond it describes; the locant is omitted when only one stereo-element exists. Examples: (E)-but-2-ene, (2E,4Z)-hexa-2,4-diene, (2R,3S)-2,3-dibromobutane, (R)-butan-2-ol. When several descriptors appear, they are listed in ascending order of locant.

IMP Three different kinds of descriptor exist: E/Z for double bonds (based on priorities), cis/trans for ring substituents or for a clearly defined same-side relationship (based on geometry), and R/S for stereocentres (priorities and handedness). The signs (+) and (−) record the measured direction of rotation of plane-polarised light and have no fixed relation to R and S.
