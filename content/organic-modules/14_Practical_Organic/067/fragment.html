Example 48 Main

Counting signals

How many signals do the following give in ¹H NMR and in ¹³C NMR: (a) p-xylene, (b) 2-bromopropane, (c) ethyl acetate, (d) tert-butyl alcohol?

Show solution
  1. (a) ¹H 2 (aromatic 4H singlet, methyl 6H singlet); ¹³C 3 (CH3, C–CH3 ring carbons, ring CH).
  2. (b) ¹H 2 (CH septet, 2 × CH3 doublet); ¹³C 2.
  3. (c) ¹H 3; ¹³C 4 (C=O, OCH2, CH3C=O, CH3CH2).
  4. (d) ¹H 2 (9H singlet, OH singlet); ¹³C 2 (CH3 ×3 equivalent, quaternary C–O).
Example 49 Adv

Deducing structure from NMR

A compound C4H8O2 (DBE 1) has IR 1740 cm-1. ¹H NMR: δ 4.1 (q, 2H), 2.0 (s, 3H), 1.25 (t, 3H). Identify.

Show solution
  1. DBE 1 and 1740 ⇒ ester C=O. The quartet-triplet pair ⇒ CH3CH2–; the quartet at 4.1 is on oxygen (O–CH2). The 3H singlet at 2.0 is CH3–C=O. So: CH3COOCH2CH3, ethyl acetate.
  2. Isomers excluded: methyl propanoate would show a singlet at 3.7 (OCH3, 3H) and a quartet at 2.3 (CH2C=O); propyl formate would show an HC(=O)O singlet at ~8.
Example 50 Adv

Same formula, different spectra

C2H6O has two isomers. One shows a single NMR signal; the other three signals (3 : 2 : 1). Identify both and state a chemical test.

Show solution
  1. One signal: CH3OCH3 (six equivalent H). Three signals: CH3CH2OH (triplet 3H, quartet 2H, singlet 1H, which vanishes on D2O).
  2. Test: ethanol reacts with Na (H2) and gives iodoform; dimethyl ether does neither.
Validity Check The n + 1 rule holds for first-order spectra (Δν ≫ J) and for non-equivalent neighbours on the adjacent atom; O–H and N–H protons usually do not couple because of exchange, and equivalent protons never split each other.
