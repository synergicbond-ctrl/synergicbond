A. Acidity

Problem 1 [Application]

Arrange in decreasing acid strength (water, 25 °C): Chloroacetic acid, Nitroacetic acid, p-Chlorobenzoic acid, 3-Chlorobutanoic acid.

Worked solution
  1. Use the verified pKₐ value of each acid (lower pKₐ = stronger acid).
  2. Nitroacetic acid: pKₐ 1.68.
  3. Chloroacetic acid: pKₐ 2.86.
  4. p-Chlorobenzoic acid: pKₐ 3.98.
  5. 3-Chlorobutanoic acid: pKₐ 4.05.

Answer: Decreasing acidity: Nitroacetic acid > Chloroacetic acid > p-Chlorobenzoic acid > 3-Chlorobutanoic acid.
