10.4 A mental flow chart for any naming question

  • Step A – what is the senior group? Use the table; if none, the compound is a hydrocarbon/halide/ether: name the longest chain (or ring).
  • Step B – chain. Containing the senior group(s); max count of principal groups; max multiple bonds; longest; more substituents.
  • Step C – number. Senior group lowest → multiple bonds → set of all prefixes → first-cited prefix.
  • Step D – prefixes. Choose forms (oxo/formyl/acyl; amino; cyano; alkoxy); alphabetise; use di/tri/bis/tris correctly.
  • Step E – descriptors. E/Z, R/S, cis/trans at the front.
  • Step F – sanity check. Redraw the structure from your name; if it differs from the question, an error has crept in.
