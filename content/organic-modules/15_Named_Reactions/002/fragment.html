Part A. Carbon–carbon bond formation (coupling and organometallics)

Every C–C forming reaction can be filed under one of four electronic patterns, and recognising the pattern predicts the outcome. (i) Nucleophilic carbon + electrophilic carbon: Grignard, organocuprate, enolate, cyanide, acetylide. (ii) Radical coupling: Kolbe, Wurtz (partly), pinacol coupling. (iii) Electrophilic carbon + nucleophilic ring: Friedel–Crafts, Reimer–Tiemann. (iv) Pericyclic: Diels–Alder, Claisen, Cope.
