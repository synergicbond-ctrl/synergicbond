20. Ethers: structure, nomenclature, isomerism and properties

An ether has an oxygen bonded to two carbon groups, R–O–R′. The oxygen is sp3; the C–O–C angle is about 112° in dimethyl ether (it is larger than the H–O–H angle of water because of repulsion between the two alkyl groups, and about 118° in anisole where O is partly conjugated with the ring), and the C–O bond length is about 141 pm. The dipole moment is about 1.2–1.3 D. Ethers have no O–H, so they cannot donate hydrogen bonds, but the oxygen lone pairs accept hydrogen bonds and coordinate to Lewis acids.
