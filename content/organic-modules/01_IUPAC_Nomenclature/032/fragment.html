4.3 Ethers: the alkoxy prefix

  • An ether R–O–R′ has no suffix. The oxygen and the smaller group form an alkoxy prefix (methoxy, ethoxy, propoxy, butoxy; isopropoxy = propan-2-yloxy; tert-butoxy); the longer (or more senior) chain is the parent.
  • Phenyl ethers are named on benzene: methoxybenzene (anisole), ethoxybenzene (phenetole).
  • Common (functional class) names: ethoxyethane = diethyl ether; methoxymethane = dimethyl ether; 2-methoxy-2-methylpropane = methyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE).
