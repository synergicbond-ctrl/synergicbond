12. Counting substituted derivatives by symmetry

The number of monochloro derivatives of a hydrocarbon equals the number of sets of equivalent hydrogens, that is, hydrogens that are interchanged by a symmetry operation of the molecule (or, for free-rotating alkyl groups, belong to chemically identical carbons). Group hydrogens by carbon; carbons that are related by symmetry are equivalent; then count the groups.

  1. Step 1: draw the full skeleton and look for symmetry planes or axes.
  2. Step 2: label carbons that map onto each other by symmetry as equivalent; all H on equivalent CH3 carbons are equivalent.
  3. Step 3: count the types of hydrogen: that is the number of monochloro structural isomers.
  4. Step 4: if stereoisomers are also to be counted, add the extra stereoisomers for every product that has a stereocentre or a stereogenic double bond or ring.

Neopentane (1 monochloro product), 2-methylpropane (2), 2,2,3-trimethylbutane (3).

For dichloro derivatives, first count the structural isomers by choosing the first Cl on each type of carbon, then add the second Cl on every remaining position, and discard duplicates. 2-Methylpropane gives three dichloro structural isomers: 1,1-, 1,2- and 1,3-dichloro-2-methylpropane (both Cl on one CH3; one Cl on a CH3 and one on the CH; one Cl on each of two different CH3 groups).

In aromatic polycycles the same approach holds, but it uses the symmetry of the ring system. Naphthalene has two types of hydrogen (α and β), so two monochloro derivatives and ten dichloro derivatives. Anthracene has three types of hydrogen (positions 1, 2 and 9), so three monochloro and fifteen dichloro derivatives. Benzene has all six H equivalent, so one monochloro; the dichloro count is three.

The two monochloronaphthalenes (1-chloro and 2-chloro).

Key idea. Number of monosubstituted isomers = number of sets of equivalent H. Methylcyclohexane has five (ring positions 1, 2, 3, 4 and the methyl group), and 3-methylpentane has four.
Trap. Chlorination of an alkane at a stereo-centre or creation of a new stereocentre raises the product count if enantiomers and diastereomers are included. 3-Methylpentane gives 4 structural monochloro isomers but 8 stereoisomeric products altogether.
