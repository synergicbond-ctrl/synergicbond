5.3 Tosylates and mesylates: turning OH into a leaving group without breaking C–O

ROH + p-CH3C6H4SO2Cl (TsCl) with pyridine → ROTs + pyridinium chloride. The C–O bond of the alcohol is not broken, so the configuration is retained in the tosylate. The tosylate anion (pKa of TsOH about −2.8) is an excellent leaving group; a nucleophile then displaces it by SN2 with inversion. Two steps therefore give net inversion; if the same sequence is used twice, net retention.

Key idea Alcohol → tosylate → SN2 product is the standard way to invert a stereocentre in a secondary alcohol or to introduce Nu− (CN−, N3−, SR−, I−, H−) without acidic conditions (no carbocation, no rearrangement).
