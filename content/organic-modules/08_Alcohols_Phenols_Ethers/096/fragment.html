Example 76 L3 Advanced

Mixed puzzle

A compound X (C8H8O) is an epoxide. It reacts with CH3OH/H+ to give Y (C9H12O2) and with CH3ONa/CH3OH to give Z (C9H12O2). Y is oxidised by PCC to an aldehyde, Z to a ketone. Identify X, Y, Z.

Show solution
  1. C8H8O epoxide with a phenyl group: styrene oxide (2-phenyloxirane).
  2. Acid: methanol attacks the benzylic carbon (stabilised cation character): Y = PhCH(OCH3)CH2OH, a primary alcohol; PCC gives the aldehyde PhCH(OCH3)CHO ✓.
  3. Base: methoxide attacks the CH2: Z = PhCH(OH)CH2OCH3, a secondary benzylic alcohol; PCC gives the ketone PhCOCH2OCH3 ✓.
  4. X = styrene oxide; Y = 2-methoxy-2-phenylethanol; Z = 2-methoxy-1-phenylethanol.
