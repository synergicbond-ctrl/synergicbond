Block E. Reagent selection

Practice 91 Reagent

Reduce the ketone but not the ester in CH3COCH2CH2COOEt.

Show answer
  1. NaBH4 in ethanol at 25 °C.
Practice 92 Reagent

Convert an ester RCOOEt into the aldehyde RCHO.

Show answer
  1. DIBAL-H (1 equiv.) at −78 °C, then water.
Practice 93 Reagent

Convert RCOCl into RCHO (two methods).

Show answer
  1. H2/Pd–BaSO4 (Rosenmund) or LiAlH(O-t-Bu)3 at −78 °C.
Practice 94 Reagent

Convert an alkene into the syn-1,2-diol.

Show answer
  1. OsO4/NMO or cold dilute alkaline KMnO4.
Practice 95 Reagent

Convert an alkene into an epoxide.

Show answer
  1. A peracid such as mCPBA.
Practice 96 Reagent

Convert RC≡CR′ into the trans-alkene.

Show answer
  1. Na (or Li) in liquid NH3.
Practice 97 Reagent

Convert a primary alcohol into the aldehyde without over-oxidation.

Show answer
  1. PCC in CH2Cl2 (also PDC, Dess–Martin).
Practice 98 Reagent

Convert ArNO2 into ArNH2.

Show answer
  1. Sn/HCl, Fe/HCl or H2/Pd.
Practice 99 Reagent

Brominate the allylic position of cyclohexene.

Show answer
  1. NBS, hν or peroxide, CCl4: gives 3-bromocyclohexene.
Practice 100 Reagent

Reduce RCOOH to RCH2OH.

Show answer
  1. LiAlH4 (ether, then H2O) or BH3·THF.
Practice 101 Reagent

Convert phenol into benzene.

Show answer
  1. Zinc dust, heat (distillation).
Practice 102 Reagent

Oxidise an aldehyde to the acid under mild conditions that spare C=C.

Show answer
  1. Tollens' reagent [Ag(NH3)2]+.
Practice 103 Reagent

Convert RCl or RBr into RI.

Show answer
  1. NaI in dry acetone (Finkelstein).
Practice 104 Reagent

Introduce CHO on toluene's methyl group.

Show answer
  1. CrO2Cl2 in CS2, then H3O+ (Etard).
Practice 105 Reagent

Form the kinetic enolate of 2-methylcyclohexanone.

Show answer
  1. LDA, THF, −78 °C (less substituted enolate).
Practice 106 Reagent

Hydrate 1-hexene to hexan-1-ol.

Show answer
  1. BH3·THF; then H2O2/NaOH (anti-Markovnikov).
Practice 107 Reagent

Hydrate 3,3-dimethylbut-1-ene to 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol without rearrangement.

Show answer
  1. Hg(OAc)2, H2O; then NaBH4 (oxymercuration–demercuration).
Practice 108 Reagent

Protect a ketone as a cyclic ketal before reducing an ester with LiAlH4.

Show answer
  1. HOCH2CH2OH, H+ (remove water); deprotect with dilute H3O+.
Practice 109 Reagent

Cleave a vicinal diol into two carbonyl compounds.

Show answer
  1. HIO4 (or NaIO4, Pb(OAc)4).
Practice 110 Reagent

Test whether an amine is primary using a foul-smelling product.

Show answer
  1. CHCl3 + alcoholic KOH, warm (carbylamine test): primary amines give isocyanides.
