L. Equilibrium constants

Problem 144 [Advanced]

Calculate K for HA(Chloroacetic acid) + A⁻(Acetic acid) ⇌ A⁻(Chloroacetic acid) + HA(Acetic acid) using the verified pKₐ values.

Worked solution
  1. pKₐ(Chloroacetic acid) = 2.86; pKₐ(Acetic acid) = 4.74.
  2. K = Kₐ(Acetic acid)/Kₐ(Chloroacetic acid) = 10^(pKₐ(Acetic acid) − pKₐ(Chloroacetic acid)) = 10^(1.88).

Answer: K ≈ 75.9. The acid with the smaller pKₐ (Chloroacetic acid) is the stronger acid, so the reaction lies to the right.
