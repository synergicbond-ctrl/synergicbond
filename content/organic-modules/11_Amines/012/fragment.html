Synthesis planning

Convert (a) ethyl bromide into propylamine, (b) propanamide into ethylamine, (c) ethanamide into methylamine, (d) toluene into benzylamine.

Show solution
  1. (a) CH3CH2Br → KCN → CH3CH2CN → LiAlH4 → CH3CH2CH2NH2.
  2. (b) Br2/NaOH (Hofmann): CH3CH2CONH2 → CH3CH2NH2.
  3. (c) Br2/NaOH: CH3CONH2 → CH3NH2.
  4. (d) Toluene → Cl2/hν → PhCH2Cl → potassium phthalimide → N2H4 → PhCH2NH2.
