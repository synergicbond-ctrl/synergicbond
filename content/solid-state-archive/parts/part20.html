This part treats the standard ionic structure types in detail and also includes diamond packing, corundum and a structure-type index. This part consolidates those topics into coordinate-resolved JEE Advanced notes.

1. NaCl / Rock-Salt Type — 6:6

  • Cl⁻ forms an FCC/CCP array.
  • Na⁺ occupies all octahedral voids.
  • Cations per cell = 4; anions per cell = 4; Z = 4 formula units.
  • CN(Na⁺)=6 and CN(Cl⁻)=6.
  • Tetrahedral-site occupancy = 0%; octahedral-site occupancy = 100%.

2. CsCl Type — 8:8

  • One ion forms a simple-cubic array; the other occupies the cubic body-centre site.
  • Z = 1 formula unit per conventional cubic cell.
  • CN = 8:8.
SpeciesCoordinate

∑Formula

Nearest unlike-ion contact: r₊ + r₋ = (√3/2)a.

⚠Critical distinction

CsCl is not a BCC Bravais lattice because corner and body-centre sites contain different chemical species.

3. ZnS — Zinc Blende / Sphalerite — 4:4

  • S²⁻ forms FCC.
  • Zn²⁺ occupies half of the tetrahedral voids in an alternating/non-adjacent set.
  • Cations per cell = 4; anions per cell = 4; Z = 4.
  • CN = 4:4; tetrahedral sites occupied = 50%; octahedral = 0%.

4. Wurtzite ZnS — 4:4

  • S²⁻ forms an HCP array.
  • Zn²⁺ occupies half of the tetrahedral voids.
  • Conventional HCP description: 6 anions + 6 cations → 6 ZnS formula units.
  • CN = 4:4; TV occupancy = 50%; OV occupancy = 0%.

5. Fluorite CaF₂ — 8:4

  • Ca²⁺ forms FCC.
  • F⁻ occupies all 8 tetrahedral sites.
  • Ca per cell = 4; F per cell = 8; Z = 4 CaF₂.
  • CN(Ca²⁺)=8; CN(F⁻)=4.
  • All tetrahedral sites occupied; octahedral sites empty.

◆Concept

F⁻ coordinates are the eight combinations (x,y,z) with x,y,z∈{1/4,3/4} relative to the FCC Ca framework.

∑Formula

Nearest Ca–F distance = √3a/4.

◆Structural insight

The F⁻ sublattice can also be viewed as a simple-cubic arrangement with alternate cubic centres occupied by Ca²⁺. The anions are not required to touch each other in the real fluorite structure.

6. Antifluorite — Li₂O / Na₂O — 4:8

  • O²⁻ forms FCC.
  • Li⁺/Na⁺ occupies all tetrahedral sites.
  • Cations = 8, anions = 4, Z = 4 formula units.
  • CN(cation)=4; CN(anion)=8.
  • This is topologically the fluorite arrangement with cation/anion roles reversed.

7. Corundum Type

  • Examples: Al₂O₃, Cr₂O₃, Fe₂O₃.
  • O²⁻ forms approximately HCP.
  • Cations occupy 2/3 of octahedral sites.
  • Representative conventional count in the source: 4 cations + 6 anions = 2 A₂O₃ formula units.
  • Tetrahedral occupancy = 0%; octahedral occupancy = 66.67%.

8. Diamond Cubic

  • Carbon forms an FCC framework with half the tetrahedral sites occupied by identical carbon.
  • Equivalent description: two interpenetrating FCC sublattices displaced by (1/4,1/4,1/4).
  • Z = 8 atoms per conventional cubic cell; CN = 4 tetrahedral.

9. Spinel / Inverse Spinel — Source Index Enrichment

◆Concept

The source’s structure-type table includes spinel examples such as MgAl₂O₄, ZnFe₂O₄ and related compounds. For JEE-level structural bookkeeping, O²⁻ forms a close-packed framework with cations distributed over tetrahedral and octahedral sites.
  • Normal spinel AB₂O₄: A cations occupy tetrahedral sites; B cations occupy octahedral sites.
  • Inverse spinel: half of B occupies tetrahedral sites, while A and the remaining B share octahedral sites.
  • Site preference depends on size, charge, crystal-field stabilization and covalency; radius ratio alone is insufficient.

10. Additional Structure-Type Index

◆Concept

The source also names nickel-arsenide, perovskite and rutile structure families alongside rock salt, CsCl, fluorite, antifluorite, sphalerite, spinel and wurtzite. These names are useful as a structure-type index even when the detailed geometry is not developed in the handwritten sequence.

11. Truncated-Octahedron JEE Geometry Sidebar

◆Concept

The source includes a JEE problem on a truncated octahedron. A truncated octahedron has 14 faces: 8 regular hexagons and 6 squares.

★JEE result

Number of hexagonal faces in a truncated octahedron = 8.

⚠JEE Advanced traps & edge cases

  • NaCl: anions FCC + all OV occupied; CsCl: simple-cubic sublattice + body-centre basis — do not mix them.
  • Zinc blende and wurtzite both have 4:4 coordination but differ in CCP/FCC versus HCP anion stacking.
  • Fluorite has cation CN 8 and anion CN 4; antifluorite reverses the species roles.
  • Diamond is not close-packed; its packing efficiency is only about 34%.

▶Solved examples

Worked example 1

Example 1 — Void occupancy to formula

An FCC array of X has all tetrahedral sites occupied by M. Find formula.

  1. FCC host X count per cell = 4.
  2. Tetrahedral sites = 8 and all are occupied.
  3. M:X = 8:4 = 2:1.

Formula = M₂X (antifluorite topology).

Worked example 2

Example 2 — Matrix match

Match NaCl, CsCl, ZnS(blende), CaF₂ with 6:6, 8:8, 4:4, 8:4 coordination.

  1. NaCl → 6:6.
  2. CsCl → 8:8.
  3. ZnS blende → 4:4.
  4. CaF₂ → cation:anion = 8:4.

NaCl–6:6; CsCl–8:8; ZnS–4:4; CaF₂–8:4.
