23. Reformatsky reaction

Reagents: an α-bromo ester (BrCH2CO2Et) + zinc dust (activated), then an aldehyde or ketone, then H3O+. Product: β-hydroxy ester, which can be dehydrated (H+, Δ) to the α,β-unsaturated ester. Mechanism: Zn inserts into the C–Br bond to give the organozinc enolate BrZn–CH2CO2Et. It is less basic and less reactive than a Grignard, so it adds to an aldehyde or ketone C=O but does not attack the ester carbonyl of another molecule, which a Grignard reagent or a lithium enolate would. This chemoselectivity is the reason the reaction is used.

Trap A Grignard cannot be made from BrCH2CO2Et (it would attack the ester of a neighbouring molecule); the zinc enolate can. Compare: Reformatsky = Zn + α-bromo ester + carbonyl → β-hydroxy ester; Darzens = base + α-chloro ester + carbonyl → epoxy ester.
