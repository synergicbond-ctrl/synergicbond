Part C. Named reactions on aromatic rings

Two themes run through this Part. (1) Introducing a one-carbon unit (–CHO, –COOH, –CH2OH) onto a ring: Reimer–Tiemann, Kolbe–Schmitt, Gattermann–Koch, Gattermann, Houben–Hoesch, Vilsmeier–Haack, Etard, Lederer–Manasse. In each, decide the electrophile and the position it attacks (ortho/para to an activator). (2) Converting –NH2 through the diazonium salt: Sandmeyer, Gattermann, Balz–Schiemann, azo coupling.
