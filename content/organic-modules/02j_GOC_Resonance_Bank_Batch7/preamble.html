RM-107 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q107 · Status: Verified (standard rules)

Statement I: resonance contributors must have the same number of unpaired electrons. Statement II: atoms may be moved between contributors if the energy is lower. (a) I true, II false (b) I false, II true (c) both true (d) both false

Full solution
  1. Statement I is the standard rule (same number of paired/unpaired electrons).
  2. Statement II is false: resonance moves only electrons; atoms never move (that would be tautomerism).

Answer: (a)

RM-108 [Application] · Master sheet Q108 · Status: Verified

Which statement about the allyl cation is FALSE? (a) both C–C bonds are equal (b) positive charge is shared by C1 and C3 (c) the central carbon bears the charge in one contributor (d) it is more stable than the isopropyl cation in solution

Full solution
  1. (a) and (b) are true (resonance over C1 and C3).
  2. (c) is false: the central carbon never carries the positive charge in any contributor.
  3. (d) is true in solution (allyl ≈ 2° order stated in passage data).

Answer: (c)

RM-109 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q109 · Status: Verified

Hyperconjugation involves overlap of σ(C–H) with: (a) an adjacent empty p, π or half-filled p orbital (b) an adjacent σ* only (c) a lone pair only (d) a d orbital

Full solution
  1. Hyperconjugation is σ–p or σ–π overlap, which covers empty p, π and half-filled p orbitals.

Answer: (a)

RM-110 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q110 · Status: Verified

Stability order of alkyl free radicals is explained mainly by: (a) hyperconjugation (b) –M effect (c) steric inhibition of resonance (d) –I effect only

Full solution
  1. Radical stability tracks the number of α-H (hyperconjugation), not –M or –I.

Answer: (a)

RM-115 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q115 · Status: Verified

Hybridisation of the cationic carbon in a carbocation, and its shape: (a) sp², trigonal planar (b) sp³, pyramidal (c) sp, linear (d) sp³d

Full solution
  1. Three σ-bonds and an empty p orbital: sp², trigonal planar.

Answer: (a)

RM-116 [Application] · Master sheet Q116 · Status: Verified (standard mechanisms)

Which does not involve a free-radical intermediate? (a) Cl₂ + CH₄ under UV (b) Kolbe electrolysis (c) Wurtz reaction (via R–Na) (d) Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky bromination (mainly enol)

Full solution
  1. (a) radical chain; (b) radicals from carboxylate oxidation; (c) radical pathway (conventional).
  2. (d) HVZ proceeds via the enol of the acid; no radical intermediate.

Answer: (d)

RM-128 [Application] · Master sheet Q128 · Status: Verified (standard rearrangement)

[JEE Main pattern] Which rearranges with HBr to form a 3° cation from a 2° cation? (a) 3-methylbut-1-ene (b) but-1-ene (c) propene (d) cyclohexene

Full solution
  1. 3-methylbut-1-ene + H⁺ gives the 2° cation (CH₃)₂CH–CH⁺–CH₃.
  2. A 1,2-hydride shift gives the 3° cation (CH₃)₂C⁺–CH₂CH₃ (the tert-amyl type).
  3. The other options give no 3° cation by a single shift.

Answer: (a)
