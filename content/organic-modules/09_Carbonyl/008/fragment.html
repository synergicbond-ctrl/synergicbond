5. Overview of preparative methods

Every method for making a carbonyl compound falls into one of five families. Keeping the family in mind makes a hundred reactions look like five ideas.

FamilyIdeaExamples
1. OxidationRaise the oxidation level of carbon: alcohol → C=O; diol → two C=O; alkene → two C=OPCC, Swern, Cu/573 K, ozonolysis, Wacker, periodate
2. HydrationAdd H2O across C≡C; enol tautomerisesKucherov (Hg2+), hydroboration–oxidation
3. Partial reduction of acid derivativesStop at the aldehyde stageRosenmund, Stephen, DIBAL-H, LiAlH(OtBu)3
4. C–C bond formation to an acid derivativeOrganometallic + acyl compound, stopping at the ketoneR2CuLi + RCOCl, R2Cd + RCOCl, RMgX + RCN
5. Aromatic acylation / formylationElectrophilic substitution brings in C=OFriedel–Crafts acylation, Gattermann–Koch, Gattermann, Reimer–Tiemann, Vilsmeier–Haack, Etard
