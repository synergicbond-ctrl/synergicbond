Part H. Road-map syntheses and structure-deduction chains

A road-map problem gives a start and a target and asks for the reagents in between. Work backwards (retrosynthesis): ask what single reaction makes the target's functional group or C–C bond, then what makes *that* precursor. Four checks at every step: (1) carbon count, (2) position of substituents on a ring (order of the directing groups), (3) compatible functional groups, (4) protecting group needed or not. Every route below was balance-checked step by step.
