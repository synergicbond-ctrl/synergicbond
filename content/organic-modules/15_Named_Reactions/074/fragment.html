67. Saytzeff vs Hofmann elimination (regiochemistry of E2)

Saytzeff (Zaitsev) rule: with a neutral good leaving group (Cl, Br, I, OTs) and a small base (EtO−, OH−), the more substituted (more stable) alkene is the major product. Hofmann rule: with a bulky base (t-BuOK) or a bulky/charged leaving group (–N+R3, –S+R2, –F), the less substituted alkene is the major product, because the base removes the most accessible, most acidic β-hydrogen (E2 with a "carbanion-like" transition state) or because the leaving group crowds the more substituted side.

Key idea Stereochemistry of E2: anti-periplanar (H and the leaving group on opposite sides, 180° dihedral). In cyclohexane rings both must be axial (trans-diaxial). Therefore: *neomenthyl chloride* (Cl axial, two anti axial H available) eliminates fast and mainly gives the more substituted alkene; *menthyl chloride* (Cl equatorial) must flip to the conformer with the isopropyl group axial before it can eliminate: it reacts slowly and gives the less substituted alkene.

Example 59 Main

Hofmann or Saytzeff?

Predict the major alkene: (a) 2-bromo-3-methylbutane with NaOEt; (b) the same with t-BuOK; (c) the quaternary hydroxide (CH3)3N+–CH(CH3)CH2CH3 OH−, heated.

Show solution
  1. (a) Saytzeff: removal of the tertiary β-H gives 2-methylbut-2-ene; (b) bulky base removes the more accessible methyl H: 3-methylbut-1-ene as the major product; (c) Hofmann elimination: but-1-ene (+ N(CH3)3 + H2O).
