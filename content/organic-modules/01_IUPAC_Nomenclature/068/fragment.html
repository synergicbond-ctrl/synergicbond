Meso compounds. A molecule that has stereocentres and an internal mirror plane is achiral and optically inactive despite its R and S labels: (2R,3S)-tartaric acid and (1R,2S)-1,2-dimethylcyclohexane (cis). Mirror-related centres in a meso compound carry opposite labels.

Exam Trap The mirror image of a meso compound is the same compound, so it is not counted again. Tartaric acid has three stereoisomers (one enantiomeric pair and one meso form), not four.
Validity Check The counting formulas in the table assume that every stereo-element is independent. They fail for ring systems that constrain configurations (for example bridged bicyclic compounds, §8.7), where the geometrically possible isomers are fewer than 2ⁿ.
