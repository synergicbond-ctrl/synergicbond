Rules that cost marks

  • The carbon of –COOH, –COCl, –CONH2, –CN (as –nitrile), –CHO is counted in the parent chain; with the suffixes –carboxylic acid, –carbonyl chloride, –carboxamide, –carbonitrile it is not.
  • Esters are named as the alkyl group of the alcohol first, then the acid stem: CH3CH2COOCH3 is methyl propanoate, not propyl ethanoate.
  • In an ester that also contains a ketone, the ester wins: CH3COCH2COOC2H5 is ethyl 3-oxobutanoate (ethyl acetoacetate).
  • N-substituents on amides are cited with the locant N: HCON(CH3)2 is N,N-dimethylmethanamide (DMF).
  • Dicarboxylic acids: –dioic acid (oxalic = ethanedioic, malonic = propanedioic, succinic = butanedioic, glutaric = pentanedioic, adipic = hexanedioic, pimelic = heptanedioic, phthalic = benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid).
  • Unsaturated acids keep the double bond inside the numbering: CH3CH=CHCOOH is but-2-enoic acid (crotonic = E, isocrotonic = Z).

Naming practice: each name is worth working out yourself before looking
Example 1 Main

Naming

Name (a) CH3CH(OH)CH2COOH, (b) CH3COOCH(CH3)2, (c) ClCH2CH2COCl, (d) NCCH2CH2COOH, (e) the cyclic ester O=C1CCCO1.

Show solution
  1. (a) COOH is the principal group, OH becomes a prefix: 3-hydroxybutanoic acid.
  2. (b) Alkyl part from the alcohol = isopropyl (propan-2-yl): propan-2-yl ethanoate (isopropyl acetate).
  3. (c) Acyl chloride suffix beats the halogen prefix: 3-chloropropanoyl chloride.
  4. (d) Acid outranks nitrile; the –CN carbon is not in the chain, so it becomes the prefix cyano: 3-cyanopropanoic acid. HOOC–CH2–CH2–CN: the acid carbon is C-1, and the –CN carbon is not counted in the chain, so the cyano prefix sits on C-3.
  5. (e) Five-membered ring with one oxygen and a C=O: oxolan-2-one (γ-butyrolactone), the lactone of 4-hydroxybutanoic acid.
Example 2 Main

Isomer count with nomenclature

How many isomeric carboxylic acids and esters are there for C5H10O2? How many of the acids are chiral?

Show solution
  1. Acids C4H9COOH: C4H9 has four isomers (n-butyl, sec-butyl, isobutyl, tert-butyl), so 4 acids: pentanoic, 2-methylbutanoic, 3-methylbutanoic, 2,2-dimethylpropanoic.
  2. Esters HCOOR (R = C4H9: 4), CH3COOR (R = C3H7: 2), C2H5COOR (R = C2H5: 1), C3H7COOR (R = CH3: 2 since C3H7 is n- or iso-): 4 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 9 esters.
  3. Only 2-methylbutanoic acid CH3CH2CH(CH3)COOH has a stereocentre: 1 chiral acid. Among the esters only sec-butyl methanoate HCOOCH(CH3)CH2CH3 is chiral, so 2 chiral isomers in all.
