📚 The five secrets (in simple words)

  1. Fischer picture = a curled caterpillar. The up-and-down line is the spine and it curls away from you. The side arms reach toward you, like a hug.
  2. Half-turn on the page (180°) is safe. The molecule is the same. Quarter-turn (90°) is NOT safe – you get its mirror twin.
  3. Swapping two arms makes the twin. Swap again and you are back home.
  4. Twisting around a bond is just wiggling. The R/S badges do not change, so it is still the same molecule. That is how a Fischer picture (all eclipsed) becomes a zig-zag (all staggered).
  5. Looking from a different place is just looking. Sawhorse = look at the bond from a slant. Newman = put your eye exactly in line with the bond: the back carbon hides behind the front one.
