K. Intermediate stability

Problem 140 [Application]

Which carbocation is more stable: Ph₃C⁺ or CH₃⁺? Give the reason.

Worked solution
  1. Resonance or hyperconjugation/inductive stabilisation: the more delocalised or better-alkylated cation is more stable.

Answer: Ph₃C⁺ is more stable.
