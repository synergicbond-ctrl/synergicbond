Example 6 Main

Isomer counting with symmetry

(a) How many dichlorotoluenes (C7H6Cl2 with Cl on the ring) exist? (b) How many monochlorination products can toluene give if both ring and side chain are counted? (c) How many trimethylbenzenes? How many tribromotoluenes?

Show solution
  1. (a) Toluene has a unique C1 (CH3); the ring positions 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 give pairs 2≡6 and 3≡5. Choose two Cl positions out of {2,3,4,5,6} for two Cl: pairs (2,3) (2,4) (2,5) (2,6) (3,4) (3,5) = 6 dichlorotoluenes: 2,3-; 2,4-; 2,5-; 2,6-; 3,4-; 3,5-.
  2. (b) Ring: o, m, p = 3; side chain: benzyl chloride = 1; total 4.
  3. (c) Trimethylbenzenes: hemimellitene (1,2,3), pseudocumene (1,2,4), mesitylene (1,3,5) = 3. Tribromotoluenes: choose 3 of the five positions {2,3,4,5,6}: unique sets up to the symmetry 2↔6, 3↔5: 2,3,4; 2,3,5; 2,3,6; 2,4,5; 2,4,6; 3,4,5 = 6.
