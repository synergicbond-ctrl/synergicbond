51. Semipinacol rearrangements and the Tiffeneau–Demjanov ring expansion

A semipinacol rearrangement has the same 1,2-shift to a carbocation, but the cation is generated from something other than a diol: a halohydrin (Ag+), an epoxide (Lewis acid), or a β-amino alcohol with HNO2 (diazonium as the leaving group). Tiffeneau–Demjanov: a 1-(aminomethyl)cycloalkanol + HNO2 (NaNO2/HCl) → the diazonium CH2N2+ loses N2, a ring C–C bond migrates to the CH2, and the ring expands by one carbon to a ketone. Simple Demjanov: aminomethylcycloalkane + HNO2 → mixture of the ring-expanded alcohol and the unrearranged carbinol.
