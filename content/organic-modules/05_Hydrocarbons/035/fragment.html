34. Acetylide chemistry and C–C bond formation

Acetylide ions are good nucleophiles (and strong bases):

  • SN2 with 1° alkyl halides → internal alkynes (chain extension).
  • Addition to C=O: RC≡C− + R′CHO → propargylic alcohol RC≡C–CH(OH)R′ (and with ketones, tertiary propargyl alcohols). With HCHO: RC≡CCH2OH. With ethylene oxide: RC≡C–CH2CH2OH.
  • Grignard of alkynes: RC≡CH + CH3MgBr → RC≡CMgBr + CH4.
