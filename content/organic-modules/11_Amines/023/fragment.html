23. Summary sheet — aniline

Key idea Aniline is activated at o/p but must be tamed: acetylation → mono-substitution, no oxidation, no anilinium. Failures to remember: Friedel–Crafts (fails), direct nitration (47 % meta), bromination (tri, unless acetylated), sulphonation (sulphanilic acid, a zwitterion).
| Reaction | Conditions | Product |
| Bromination | Br2/H2O | 2,4,6-tribromoaniline |
| Nitration | HNO3/H2SO4 | p (51 %), m (47 %), o (2 %) |
| Sulphonation | conc. H2SO4, 453–473 K | sulphanilic acid |
| Acylation | Ac2O or AcCl | acetanilide |
| Carbylamine | CHCl3, KOH | PhNC |
| Oxidation | K2Cr2O7/H+ | p-benzoquinone |
| Diazotisation | NaNO2, HCl, 273–278 K | PhN2+Cl− |
