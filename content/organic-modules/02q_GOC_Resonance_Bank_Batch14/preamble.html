AC-078 [Foundation] · Akshay topic Q78 · Status: Verified (standard definition; Ref. [2], p. 222)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, master-sheet page 23] Hyperconjugation is best described as: (a) delocalisation of p electrons into a nearby empty orbital (b) delocalisation of σ electrons into a nearby empty orbital (c) inductive donation of alkyl groups (d) migration of a carbon or hydrogen between carbocations

Full solution
  1. Hyperconjugation is σ(C–H) electron delocalisation into an adjacent empty or partly filled p (or π*) orbital.
  2. Option (b).

Answer: (b)

AC-081 [Foundation] · Akshay topic Q81 · Status: Verified (standard bonding facts)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 23] Which statement is NOT true for ethylene? (a) both carbons are sp² (b) C=C is shorter than C–C in ethane (c) the two C=C bonds are equally strong (one π, one σ) (d) the molecule is planar

Full solution
  1. (a), (b), (d) are true for ethylene.
  2. (c) is false: the σ bond is much stronger than the π bond; they are not equal.

Answer: (c)

AC-011 [Application] · Akshay topic Q11 · Status: Verified (arrow-pushing check)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 24] Which is an acceptable resonance structure for the drawn oxocarbenium-type species (H₂C⁺–O–CH₃)? (a) CH₃CH⁺–OH (b) CH₂=O⁺–CH₃ (c) CH₂CH₂O⁺ (d) CH₃CH=O⁺H

Full solution
  1. Resonance moves electrons only: the O lone pair forms a C=O⁺ π bond.
  2. (b) keeps every second-row atom with an octet and the same atom connectivity.
  3. The other options change the connectivity or give invalid octets.

Answer: (b)

AC-091 [Application] · Akshay topic Q91 · Status: Partially verified (structure read from the image; trend standard)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 23] Most stable allyl carbocation among (a) CH₃–CH=CH–CH₂⁺ (b) CH₃–CH=CH–CH⁺–CH₃ (c) CH₃–CH=CH–C⁺(CH₃)₂-type (tertiary allyl) (d) all same

Full solution
  1. Allyl cation stability rises with alkyl substitution at the charged carbon (hyperconjugation).
  2. Option (c) has the tertiary charged carbon: the most stable.

Answer: (c)
