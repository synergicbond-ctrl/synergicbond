19.4 Eudiometry of gaseous organic compounds

Gaseous hydrocarbons and other gaseous or very volatile organic compounds are analysed by exploding with excess oxygen in a eudiometer tube over mercury, measuring volumes at the same T and P (Gay-Lussac's law of combining volumes: volume ∝ moles).

Formula CxHy + (x + y/4) O2 → xCO2 + (y/2) H2O(l) Contraction on cooling (water condensed) = V(1 + y/4) Volume of CO2 (absorbed by KOH) = xV
Formula CxHyOz: O2 consumed = x + y/4 − z/2 per mole; contraction = 1 + y/4 − z/2 per mole of compound (when H2O is liquid); CO2 formed = x
  • Vcontraction = (Vinitial total) − (V after explosion and cooling to room temperature).
  • VKOH = (V after explosion) − (V after KOH absorption) = V of CO2.
  • VO2 left = V after KOH absorption (if only O2 remains; N2, if present, is also left).
Example 42 Adv

Eudiometry of a hydrocarbon

10 mL of a gaseous hydrocarbon was mixed with 50 mL of O2 and exploded. After cooling to room temperature the volume was 40 mL; on passing through KOH it fell to 20 mL. Find the formula of the hydrocarbon.

Show solution
  1. Initial volume = 10 + 50 = 60 mL; after explosion and cooling = 40 mL; contraction = 20 mL.
  2. Volume of CO2 (absorbed by KOH) = 40 − 20 = 20 mL ⇒ x = 20/10 = 2.
  3. Contraction = V(1 + y/4) ⇒ 20 = 10(1 + y/4) ⇒ y = 4. The hydrocarbon is C2H4.
  4. Check: O2 consumed = 10 × (2 + 1) = 30 mL, left 20 mL = the volume remaining after KOH. ✔
Example 43 Adv

Eudiometry with an oxygen-containing compound

10 mL of the vapour of a compound CxHyOz (vapour density 23) was exploded with 40 mL of O2. The volume after cooling to room temperature was 30 mL, and it fell to 10 mL when passed through KOH. Find the molecular formula.

Show solution
  1. M = 2 × 23 = 46. Volume of CO2 = 30 − 10 = 20 mL ⇒ x = 2.
  2. Contraction = (10 + 40) − 30 = 20 mL = 10(1 + y/4 − z/2) ⇒ y/4 − z/2 = 1.
  3. Mass: 12 × 2 + y + 16z = 46 ⇒ y + 16z = 22. With y = 4 + 2z from the contraction equation: 4 + 2z + 16z = 22 ⇒ z = 1, y = 6. Formula C2H6O.
  4. Check: O2 consumed = 10 × (2 + 1.5 − 0.5) = 30 mL, left 10 mL = the residue after KOH. ✔ (ethanol or dimethyl ether; the formula does not decide between them.)
