40. Williamson ether synthesis

Reagents: an alkoxide or phenoxide RO−Na+ + R′–X → R–O–R′ + NaX (SN2). Alkoxide from ROH + Na or NaH; phenoxide from ArOH + NaOH. Rule of design: the alkyl halide must be methyl or primary (secondary gives mostly elimination; tertiary gives only alkene). The more hindered group, if any, should be on the alkoxide, not on the halide.

Choose the pair

Give the correct reactants for (a) ethyl isopropyl ether, (b) tert-butyl ethyl ether, (c) benzyl methyl ether.

Show solution
  1. (a) CH3CH2Br + (CH3)2CHONa (primary halide, secondary alkoxide) — not CH3CH2ONa + isopropyl bromide (elimination). (b) CH3CH2Br + (CH3)3CONa. (c) C6H5CH2Br + CH3ONa (or PhCH2ONa + CH3I): benzyl is primary and reactive, both are fine.
