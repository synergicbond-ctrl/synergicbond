60. Wolff rearrangement and the Arndt–Eistert homologation

Arndt–Eistert (acid → next higher homologue): RCOOH → RCOCl (SOCl2) → with 2 equivalents of diazomethane CH2N2 → α-diazoketone RCO–CHN2 (the second CH2N2 traps the HCl as CH3Cl) → Wolff rearrangement with Ag2O (or Ag benzoate), H2O, warm (or photolysis/heat): loss of N2 gives a carbene RCO–CH:, R migrates from C=O carbon to the carbene carbon giving a ketene R–CH=C=O, which adds water to give RCH2COOH. With ROH instead of water: ester; with NH3: amide.

Key idea Chain +1 carbon with retention of the configuration at R. Compare: Arndt–Eistert +1 C (acid → homologous acid); Kiliani–Fischer +1 C (sugar); nitrile route RX → RCN → RCOOH +1 C; Hunsdiecker, Hofmann −1 C.
