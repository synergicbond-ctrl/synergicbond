10.5 Ethers

Ethers are neutral and unreactive: no reaction with Na, NaOH, HCl (cold), 2,4-DNP. They dissolve in cold conc. H2SO4 (oxonium salt, soluble). They are identified by exclusion and cleaved by hot HI (Ziesel-type): R–O–R′ + 2HI → RI + R′I + H2O. Peroxides in old ether are detected by acidified KI (liberated I2, brown or blue with starch).

Exam Trap Ethanol vs diethyl ether: only the alcohol gives H2 with sodium and a fruity ester with acids. Methanol vs ethanol: only ethanol gives the iodoform test.
