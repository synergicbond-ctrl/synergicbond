C. Hammett estimates

Problem 69 [Application]

Using Hammett σ from the standard table, estimate the pKₐ of the meta-NO2 benzoic acid (ρ = 1 for ionisation of benzoic acids; benzoic acid pKₐ 4.19).

Worked solution
  1. σ(NO2, meta) = 0.71.
  2. pKₐ ≈ 4.19 − σ = 4.19 − (0.71) = 3.48.

Answer: Sign check: σ > 0 (electron-withdrawing) lowers pKₐ; σ < 0 (donating) raises it.
