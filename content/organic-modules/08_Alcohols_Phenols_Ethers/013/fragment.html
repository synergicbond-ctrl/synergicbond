29.3 Ethers

Practice 1 L1 ETH

Write the IUPAC name of CH3OCH2CH2CH3.

Show answer
  1. The smaller group with oxygen is the alkoxy prefix on the longer chain: 1-methoxypropane (methyl propyl ether).
Practice 2 L1 ETH

Write the IUPAC name of C6H5OC2H5.

Show answer
  1. Ethoxybenzene (phenetole).
Practice 3 L1 ETH

Write the structure and name of the ether formed from (CH3)3C–ONa and CH3I.

Show answer
  1. (CH3)3C–O–CH3: 2-methoxy-2-methylpropane (methyl tert-butyl ether, MTBE).
Practice 4 L1 ETH

How many ether isomers does C4H10O have? Name them.

Show answer
  1. Three: methoxypropane, 2-methoxypropane, ethoxyethane.
Practice 5 L1 ETH

Name the functional isomer of ethanol and state the type of isomerism.

Show answer
  1. Methoxymethane (dimethyl ether); functional isomerism (alcohol vs ether).
Practice 6 L1 ETH

What type of isomerism is shown by methoxypropane and ethoxyethane?

Show answer
  1. Metamerism (different alkyl groups on the two sides of O).
Practice 7 L1 ETH

Why are ethers much less soluble in water than alcohols of similar molar mass?

Show answer
  1. Ethers can only accept hydrogen bonds from water, not donate them, and cannot associate with each other as alcohols do. (Diethyl ether dissolves about 7 g per 100 mL; the small cyclic ethers THF and dioxane are fully miscible.)
Practice 8 L1 ETH

What is Williamson ether synthesis? Give its general equation.

Show answer
  1. Reaction of an alkoxide with an alkyl halide by SN2: RO−Na+ + R′X → ROR′ + NaX.
Practice 9 L1 ETH

Which halide is best for Williamson synthesis: methyl, primary, secondary or tertiary?

Show answer
  1. Methyl or primary. Tertiary halides eliminate; aryl and vinyl halides do not react.
Practice 10 L1 ETH

Give the products of the reaction of sodium ethoxide with 2-bromo-2-methylpropane.

Show answer
  1. Isobutene + ethanol (E2). The intended ether is not formed.
Practice 11 L1 ETH

Which pair makes ethyl tert-butyl ether: NaOEt + (CH3)3CBr or (CH3)3CONa + CH3CH2Br?

Show answer
  1. (CH3)3CONa + CH3CH2Br; the halide must be primary.
Practice 12 L1 ETH

Write the product of sodium phenoxide with bromoethane.

Show answer
  1. Ethoxybenzene (phenetole).
Practice 13 L1 ETH

Which reagent is used to dehydrate ethanol to diethyl ether, and at what temperature?

Show answer
  1. Concentrated H2SO4 at 413 K (140 °C) with excess ethanol.
Practice 14 L1 ETH

Why can symmetrical ethers but not mixed ethers be prepared well by dehydration of alcohols?

Show answer
  1. A mixture of two alcohols gives three ethers (R2O, R′2O, ROR′), which are hard to separate.
Practice 15 L1 ETH

Give the products when diethyl ether reacts with excess hot HI.

Show answer
  1. Two molecules of ethyl iodide: C2H5OC2H5 + 2HI → 2C2H5I + H2O.
Practice 16 L1 ETH

Give the products of anisole with HI.

Show answer
  1. Phenol + methyl iodide. Iodobenzene is not formed.
Practice 17 L1 ETH

Give the products of methyl tert-butyl ether with HI (cold).

Show answer
  1. tert-Butyl iodide + methanol (SN1 at the tertiary carbon).
Practice 18 L1 ETH

Does diphenyl ether react with HI?

Show answer
  1. No. The C(sp2)–O bonds cannot be cleaved by SN1 or SN2.
Practice 19 L1 ETH

What does the Zeisel method estimate?

Show answer
  1. The number of methoxy (or ethoxy) groups in a compound: heat with HI, collect CH3I in alcoholic AgNO3, weigh the AgI.
Practice 20 L1 ETH

What is formed when diethyl ether is treated with PCl5?

Show answer
  1. Ethyl chloride + POCl3: C2H5OC2H5 + PCl5 → 2C2H5Cl + POCl3.
Practice 21 L1 ETH

Why must old diethyl ether be tested before distillation?

Show answer
  1. It may contain explosive peroxides formed by autoxidation; they concentrate in the distillation residue.
Practice 22 L1 ETH

How are peroxides in ether detected and removed?

Show answer
  1. Detected with acidified KI (iodine colour) or FeSO4/KSCN (red). Removed by shaking with FeSO4 (or Na2SO3) solution.
Practice 23 L1 ETH

Why are ethers good solvents for Grignard reagents?

Show answer
  1. The oxygen lone pairs coordinate to magnesium, stabilising RMgX; ethers have no acidic H and are inert to the reagent.
Practice 24 L1 ETH

What is the directing effect of OCH3 in anisole? Give the major mononitration products.

Show answer
  1. OCH3 is activating and ortho, para-directing (+M). Products: 4-nitroanisole (major) and 2-nitroanisole.
Practice 25 L1 ETH

What is the product of anisole with CH3COCl/AlCl3?

Show answer
  1. 4-Methoxyacetophenone (Friedel–Crafts acylation at the para position).
Practice 26 L1 ETH

What is MTBE used for? How is it made industrially?

Show answer
  1. An octane-raising fuel additive. Made from isobutene + methanol over an acid resin.
Practice 27 L1 ETH

What is the structure of THF? Give its IUPAC name.

Show answer
  1. A five-membered ring with four CH2 and one O: oxolane.
Practice 28 L1 ETH

Write the structure of 1,4-dioxane.

Show answer
  1. A six-membered ring with oxygens at positions 1 and 4: O(CH2CH2)2O, C4H8O2.
Practice 29 L1 ETH

Which is the product of dehydration of ethylene glycol with conc. H2SO4 giving a cyclic ether?

Show answer
  1. 1,4-Dioxane (intermolecular dehydration of two molecules; the intramolecular route gives ethanal).
Practice 30 L1 ETH

What happens when diethyl ether is shaken with conc. H2SO4?

Show answer
  1. It dissolves as the diethyloxonium hydrogensulphate, (C2H5)2OH+HSO4− (ethers are Lewis bases); alkanes would not dissolve.
Practice 31 L1 ETH

What is the product of free-radical chlorination of diethyl ether with one equivalent of Cl2/light?

Show answer
  1. α-Chlorodiethyl ether, CH3CHClOCH2CH3 (chlorine on the carbon next to O).
Practice 32 L1 ETH

Give a use of diethyl ether in the laboratory.

Show answer
  1. As a solvent for extraction and for Grignard and LiAlH4 reactions (it was formerly an anaesthetic).
