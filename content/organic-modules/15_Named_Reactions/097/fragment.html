Block D. Rearrangements and mechanisms

Practice 76 Rearr

Which involve migration to an electron-deficient nitrogen: (A) Hofmann (B) pinacol (C) Beckmann (D) Curtius?

Show answer
  1. A, C, D. Pinacol migrates to electron-deficient carbon.
Practice 77 Rearr

In the Baeyer–Villiger oxidation of methyl isopropyl ketone, which group migrates?

Show answer
  1. Isopropyl (secondary alkyl > methyl): product isopropyl ethanoate.
Practice 78 Rearr

The Beckmann rearrangement of an unsymmetrical ketoxime: which group migrates?

Show answer
  1. The group anti (trans) to the OH on the C=N.
Practice 79 Rearr

2-Chlorocyclohexanone + NaOEt gives which product class and ring size?

Show answer
  1. Ethyl cyclopentanecarboxylate (Favorskii; ring contraction 6 → 5).
Practice 80 Rearr

In the Claisen rearrangement of allyl phenyl ether, which ring position is attacked and why does the product tautomerise?

Show answer
  1. The ortho carbon; the initial 6-allylcyclohexa-2,4-dienone is non-aromatic and enolises to restore aromaticity.
Practice 81 Rearr

A 1,5-diene on heating rearranges to an isomeric 1,5-diene. Name the reaction and the transition-state shape.

Show answer
  1. Cope rearrangement, [3,3] through a chair transition state.
Practice 82 Rearr

Phenyl ethanoate + AlCl3: how does temperature change the o/p ratio?

Show answer
  1. Low T (≈ 330 K): para (kinetic). High T (≈ 430 K): ortho (thermodynamic, chelate). This is the Fries rearrangement.
Practice 83 Rearr

What is the second, minor product of the benzidine rearrangement?

Show answer
  1. Diphenyline (2,4′-diaminobiphenyl), about 30 %.
Practice 84 Rearr

In the Arndt–Eistert synthesis, what is the intermediate formed from the diazoketone on loss of N2?

Show answer
  1. A ketene (R–CH=C=O) through the Wolff rearrangement; water adds to give the acid.
Practice 85 Rearr

Cumene hydroperoxide + dilute H2SO4: products and the group that migrates?

Show answer
  1. Phenol + acetone; phenyl migrates from carbon to oxygen.
Practice 86 Rearr

A 4,4-disubstituted cyclohexa-2,5-dienone in acid gives what class of product?

Show answer
  1. A 3,4-disubstituted phenol (dienone–phenol rearrangement; one group shifts C-4 → C-3).
Practice 87 Rearr

The sequence 1° → 3° carbocation by a methyl shift is called what?

Show answer
  1. A Wagner–Meerwein (1,2-alkyl) shift.
Practice 88 Rearr

Name the common intermediate of the Hofmann, Curtius, Schmidt and Lossen rearrangements.

Show answer
  1. An isocyanate, R–N=C=O.
Practice 89 Rearr

Which carbonyl compound classes are formed by the Dakin oxidation of o- and p-hydroxybenzaldehydes?

Show answer
  1. Catechol from the ortho and hydroquinone from the para isomer (via the formate ester).
Practice 90 Rearr

The Tiffeneau–Demjanov rearrangement changes cyclohexanone into which compound after the necessary steps?

Show answer
  1. Cycloheptanone (ring expansion by one carbon: cyanohydrin, reduction to the aminomethyl alcohol, then HNO2).
