RM-036 [Foundation] · Master sheet Q36 · Key: (see key) · Status: Verified (textbook classification)

Which groups show both −I and +M effects? (a) –OH (b) –NH₂ (c) –NO₂ (d) –Br

Full solution
  1. –OH, –NH₂ and –Br are σ-withdrawing (−I) and have lone pairs that donate by resonance (+M).
  2. –NO₂ is −I and −M (no +M).

Answer: a, b, d

RM-037 [Application] · Master sheet Q37 · Key: (see key) · Status: Verified

In which species is the negative charge located mainly on oxygen (major contributor)? (a) enolate (b) acetate (c) CH₃CH₂⁻ (d) phenoxide

Full solution
  1. Enolate: O⁻ form is the major contributor (Ref. [2], Table 22-1 discussion: charge mostly on O).
  2. Acetate: two equivalent O⁻ forms.
  3. Phenoxide: O⁻ contributor is the most important; ring-carbon forms are minor.
  4. CH₃CH₂⁻: charge on carbon, no oxygen.

Answer: a, b, d

RM-052 [Application] · Master sheet Q52 · Key: (see key) · Status: Verified against passage data

Passage 1: which statement is consistent with the data? (a) successive methyl substitution gives diminishing stabilisation (42, 21, 18) (b) each methyl contributes equal stabilisation (c) hyperconjugation is absent in (CH₃)₃C⁺ (d) allyl is more stable than isopropyl in the gas phase

Full solution
  1. CH₃⁺→CH₃CH₂⁺: 312−270 = 42; →(CH₃)₂CH⁺: 270−249 = 21; →(CH₃)₃C⁺: 249−231 = 18.
  2. Steps shrink: (a) true. (c) is false (t-Bu has nine α-H). (d) false: allyl 256 > isopropyl 249 in HIA, so isopropyl is more stable.

Answer: (a)
