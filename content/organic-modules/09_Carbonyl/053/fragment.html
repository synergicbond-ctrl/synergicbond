45. Structure elucidation: a method

Almost every "identify A, B, C" problem in this chapter yields to a fixed sequence.

  • Step 1: Compute the degree of unsaturation DBE = (2C + 2 + N − H − X)/2 from the formula. A DBE of 1 with one oxygen suggests a carbonyl compound (or an alcohol with a ring, or an epoxide).
  • Step 2: Use qualitative tests to classify: 2,4-DNP positive (C=O); Tollens' positive (aldehyde); Fehling's positive (aliphatic aldehyde); iodoform positive (CH3CO– or CH3CH(OH)–).
  • Step 3: Use degradations: ozonolysis (fragments), Clemmensen/Wolff–Kishner (identify the carbon skeleton), Baeyer–Villiger and hydrolysis (identify groups on each side), haloform (loses CH3).
  • Step 4: Use synthetic reactions (aldol, Cannizzaro, Grignard) to restrict structures; use the carbon count.
  • Step 5: Check the answer against every given fact.
Example 44 Main

Identify

A compound A, C3H6O, gives a yellow-orange precipitate with 2,4-DNP, no silver mirror, and a yellow precipitate with I2/NaOH. Identify A and B formed with Wolff–Kishner.

Show solution
  1. DBE = (2×3 + 2 − 6)/2 = 1. C=O present (DNP). No Tollens': ketone. Iodoform positive: CH3CO–. Only one ketone has three carbons: propanone. Wolff–Kishner gives propane.
Example 45 Main

Identify

A (C8H8O) gives a 2,4-DNP precipitate, does not reduce Tollens', and gives benzoic acid and CHI3 with I2/NaOH. Identify A and the Clemmensen product.

Show solution
  1. DBE = (2×8 + 2 − 8)/2 = 5: a benzene ring (4) + one C=O. Haloform gives PhCOOH: A = acetophenone C6H5COCH3. Clemmensen: ethylbenzene.
acetophenone ethylbenzene
Example 46 Adv

Same formula, different ketones

A and B are isomeric ketones C5H10O. Both give iodoform; on treatment with peracid followed by hydrolysis, A gives ethanoic acid and propan-1-ol, B gives ethanoic acid and propan-2-ol. Identify A and B.

Show solution
  1. Iodoform positive: both are methyl ketones. C5H10O methyl ketones: pentan-2-one (CH3COCH2CH2CH3) and 3-methylbutan-2-one (CH3COCH(CH3)2).
  2. Baeyer–Villiger: the larger alkyl group migrates (n-propyl or isopropyl > methyl): pentan-2-one → propyl ethanoate → propan-1-ol + CH3COOH. 3-Methylbutan-2-one → isopropyl ethanoate → propan-2-ol + CH3COOH.
  3. A = pentan-2-one; B = 3-methylbutan-2-one.
A: pentan-2-one B: 3-methylbutan-2-one
Example 47 Adv

Ozonolysis

Compound C (C6H10) on ozonolysis (Zn/H2O) gives a single compound D (C6H10O2) that gives a silver mirror with Tollens' and a red precipitate with Fehling's. D also gives a positive 2,4-DNP test and on intramolecular aldol condensation gives E. Identify C, D, E.

Show solution
  1. C: DBE = 2. Single product with the same carbon number and DBE 2 (two C=O): C is a cycloalkene (cyclohexene), D is hexanedial OHC(CH2)4CHO (Tollens', Fehling's positive).
  2. Intramolecular aldol: C-2 enolate attacks C-6 CHO: five-membered ring; after dehydration E = cyclopent-1-ene-1-carbaldehyde.

Example 48 Adv

Stereochemistry of a reduction

A compound F (C4H8O) is reduced by NaBH4 to G (C4H10O), which has a stereocentre. Identify F and state whether G is optically active.

Show solution
  1. Butanal and 2-methylpropanal give butan-1-ol and 2-methylpropan-1-ol, neither of which has a stereocentre. Butanone gives butan-2-ol, which does. So F = butanone.
  2. NaBH4 is achiral, so hydride attacks the two faces of C=O equally: G is formed as a racemate and is optically inactive, although each molecule is chiral.
Example 49 Adv

Synthetic sequence

Synthesise 2-methylbutan-2-ol from propanone, using ethyl bromide and inorganic reagents.

Show solution
  1. C2H5Br + Mg in dry ether gives C2H5MgBr. Propanone + C2H5MgBr gives the tertiary alkoxide; H3O+ gives (CH3)2C(OH)CH2CH3, 2-methylbutan-2-ol.
