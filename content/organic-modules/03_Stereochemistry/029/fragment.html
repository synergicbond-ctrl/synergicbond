26. Summary sheet: stereochemistry of reactions

  • SN2: inversion. SN1: racemisation. SNi: retention. NGP / double inversion: retention.
  • E2: anti-periplanar (trans-diaxial in cyclohexane); stereospecific. E1: loses stereochemistry; Zaitsev.
  • Alkenes: Br2 anti; OsO4/KMnO4 syn; epoxide opening anti; H2 syn; hydroboration syn; carbene syn; HX not stereospecific. cis + anti → racemic; trans + anti → meso; cis + syn → meso; trans + syn → racemic.
  • Alkynes: Lindlar → cis; Na/NH3 → trans.
  • Diels–Alder retains dienophile geometry. Carbonyl + achiral nucleophile: racemate.
