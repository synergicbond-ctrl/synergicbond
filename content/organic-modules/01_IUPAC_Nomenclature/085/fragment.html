10.3 Counting questions: how to extract the number you are asked for

JEE Advanced regularly asks "the sum of all locants", "the number of carbon atoms in the parent chain", "the number of substituents" or "the number of different prefixes". Do each in the order: (1) write the correct name; (2) read the quantity off the name; (3) never count from the drawing alone.

Counting conventions
QuantityHow to read it from the namePitfall
Carbons in parent chainthe root: hex = 6, hept = 7, …; for ring suffixes (-carboxylic acid) the ring onlythe COOH carbon is not in a cyclohexanecarboxylic acid parent
Total carbons in the moleculeparent + sum of carbons in all prefixes (methyl 1, ethyl 2, propyl 3; a complex group counts all its carbons)cyano adds 1 carbon outside the chain; carboxy adds 1
Number of substituentscount the individual groups (dimethyl = 2, trichloro = 3)the principal group (suffix) is not a substituent
Number of different substituent typesdistinct prefix names (dimethyl + ethyl = 2 types)di/tri do not make new types
Sum of locantsadd every number in the name, including the double-bond locant and the suffix locant; exclude numbers inside brackets of bicyclo[x.y.z] unless toldan implied locant 1 (cyclohexanol) is not printed, so is not added unless the question says so
Degree of unsaturation of the moleculefrom the formula, not from the namerings count; C=O counts
Example 54 Adv

For the compound shown, find (a) the number of carbons in the parent chain, (b) the total carbons, (c) the sum of all printed locants.

Show solution
  1. The structure is HOOC–CH₂–CH(CH₃)–CH(CH₂CH₃)–CH₂–CH(CH₃)–CH₃. C1 is the acid carbon; follow the chain: C2 CH₂, C3 CH(CH₃), C4 CH(ethyl), C5 CH₂, C6 CH, C7 CH₃ (one of the two methyls on C6 is the chain end).
  2. Parent: heptanoic acid (7 carbons). Substituents: methyl on C3, ethyl on C4, methyl on C6.
  3. Name with prefixes in alphabetical order: 4-ethyl-3,6-dimethylheptanoic acid.
  4. (a) 7. (b) 7 + 2 (ethyl) + 1 + 1 (two methyls) = 11. (c) locants 4 + 3 + 6 = 13.

(a) 7, (b) 11, (c) 13.
