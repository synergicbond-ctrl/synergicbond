Nitrogen groups: structures
Exam Trap Cyanide and isocyanide are different. CH₃–C≡N is methyl cyanide (ethanenitrile): the carbon of the group is bonded to the alkyl carbon. CH₃–N⁺≡C⁻ is methyl isocyanide (isocyanomethane): the nitrogen is bonded to the alkyl carbon. They also differ in chain carbon count: ethanenitrile has two carbons, isocyanomethane has one.
