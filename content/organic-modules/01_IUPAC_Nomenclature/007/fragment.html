1.2 Rule-sets and which one a problem uses

The IUPAC rules have been revised several times: the 1979 rules (Rules A, B and C), the 1993 guide, and the 2013 recommendations, which introduced preferred IUPAC names (PINs). School and JEE problems follow the 1979/1993 conventions and still use names such as isopropyl, 1-methylethyl, vinyl and allyl; the 2013 recommendations prefer propan-2-yl, ethenyl and prop-2-en-1-yl.

Compare & Distinguish The two rule-sets agree on almost every compound met in practice. They disagree chiefly on (i) whether the parent chain must contain a double bond even if a longer chain exists, (ii) whether a ring always outranks a chain, and (iii) the preferred forms of a few substituent names. Each disagreement is marked where it arises. When a problem is silent, answer in the 1979/1993 convention and be able to convert to the 2013 form.
