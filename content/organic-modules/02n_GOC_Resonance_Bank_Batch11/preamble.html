AC-048 [Application] · Akshay topic Q48 · Status: Verified (Ref. [2] σₚ values, read at 220 dpi)

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, master-sheet page 20] Most stable benzyl-type carbocation among para-substituted (a) CH₃ (b) OH (c) Cl (d) NO₂

Full solution
  1. Para-OH is a strong +M donor (σₚ −0.38, Ref. [2], Table 4.5), which stabilises the benzyl cation most.
  2. CH₃ is a weaker donor; Cl is net withdrawing; NO₂ is strongly withdrawing.
  3. Option (b).

Answer: (b)

AC-049 [Application] · Akshay topic Q49 · Status: Verified against Ref. [2] σₚ values

[Akshay Chaudhary topic, page 20] Decreasing stability of para-substituted benzyl cations: (i) p-OCH₃ (ii) p-OH (iii) p-NH₂ (iv) p-Cl. Options: (a) iii>ii>i>iv (b) i>ii>iii>iv (c) iii>i>ii>iv (d) ii>iii>i>iv

Full solution
  1. Donor strength from σₚ (Ref. [2], Table 4.5): OH −0.38; NH₂ −0.30; OCH₃ −0.12; Cl +0.24.
  2. Most stabilising first: OH > NH₂ > OCH₃ > Cl.
  3. Option (d) ii > iii > i > iv.

Answer: (d)
