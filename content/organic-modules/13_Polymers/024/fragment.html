20. Natural polymers: a polymer-chemist’s view

Cellulose (β-1,4-D-glucose), starch (α-glucose: amylose linear, amylopectin branched), glycogen, proteins (α-amino acids with peptide bonds), nucleic acids (nucleotides with phosphodiester bonds), natural rubber (cis-1,4-isoprene). Proteins and nucleic acids are monodisperse (all chains identical, PDI = 1) because their sequences are templated by the genetic code, a rare exception to polydispersity. These are all condensation-type polymers (the monomer loses H2O on linking), except rubber, whose isoprene units are joined by enzymatic polyprenyl chain elongation.
