47. Problem bank, Level 1: alkynes and dienes

Practice 108 L1 Alkynes

Acidity order

Arrange in decreasing acidity: ethane, ethene, ethyne, water, ethanol, ammonia.

Show answer

Water (15.7) > ethanol (16–18) > ethyne (25) > ammonia (38) > ethene (44) > ethane (50).

Practice 109 L1 Alkynes

Why acidic

Explain why ethyne is more acidic than ethene and ethane.

Show answer

The ≡C–H bond has 50 % s-character (sp), so the carbanion lone pair is held closer to the nucleus and is stabilised; sp2 (33 %) and sp3 (25 %) are less so.

Practice 110 L1 Alkynes

Reaction with bases

Which bases deprotonate propyne appreciably: NaOH, NaOEt, NaNH2, NaH?

Show answer

NaNH2 and NaH only. Hydroxide and ethoxide have conjugate acids (water 15.7, ethanol 16) stronger than propyne (pKa 25).

Practice 111 L1 Alkynes

Distinguishing tests

Which reagent distinguishes (a) propyne from but-2-yne (b) ethene from ethyne (c) but-1-yne from but-2-yne?

Show answer

Ammoniacal AgNO3 (white ppt with the terminal alkyne) or ammoniacal Cu2Cl2 (red ppt) in all three.

Practice 112 L1 Alkynes

Preparation

Write the preparation of ethyne from (a) calcium carbide (b) 1,2-dibromoethane (c) CHBr2CHBr2.

Show answer

(a) CaC2 + 2H2O → C2H2 + Ca(OH)2. (b) BrCH2CH2Br + 2NaNH2 → HC≡CH. (c) CHBr2CHBr2 + 2Zn → HC≡CH + 2ZnBr2.

Practice 113 L1 Alkynes

Double dehydrohalogenation

What does 2,2-dibromopropane give with excess NaNH2, then H2O?

Show answer

Propyne (the terminal acetylide forms and is protonated by water).

Practice 114 L1 Alkynes

Acetylide alkylation

Prepare pent-2-yne from propyne.

Show answer

CH3C≡CH + NaNH2 → CH3C≡C−Na+; + CH3CH2Br → CH3C≡CCH2CH3.

Practice 115 L1 Alkynes

Alkylation limits

Why can't tert-butyl bromide be used to alkylate sodium acetylide?

Show answer

The acetylide is a strong base; with a tertiary halide E2 elimination (isobutene) dominates over SN2.

Practice 116 L1 Alkynes

Lindlar vs Na/NH3

Products of pent-2-yne with (a) H2, Lindlar (b) Na/NH3(l).

Show answer

(a) cis (Z)-pent-2-ene (b) trans (E)-pent-2-ene.

Practice 117 L1 Alkynes

Terminal alkyne with Na/NH3

Does 1-butyne give 1-butene with Na/liq. NH3?

Show answer

No: the terminal alkyne is deprotonated to the acetylide, which is not reduced further.

Practice 118 L1 Alkynes

Hydration

Products of hydration (HgSO4/H2SO4) of (a) ethyne (b) propyne (c) but-2-yne (d) pent-2-yne.

Show answer

(a) ethanal (b) propanone (c) butanone (d) mixture of pentan-2-one and pentan-3-one.

Practice 119 L1 Alkynes

Hydroboration

Convert but-1-yne into (a) butanone (b) butanal.

Show answer

(a) H2O, HgSO4, H2SO4. (b) 1. Sia2BH (or 9-BBN) 2. H2O2, NaOH.

Practice 120 L1 Alkynes

HX addition

Product of propyne + 2 HBr. Of propyne + 1 HBr with peroxide?

Show answer

2,2-Dibromopropane. With peroxide: (Z)-1-bromopropene (anti-Markovnikov).

Practice 121 L1 Alkynes

Br2 addition

Product of but-2-yne + 1 equivalent of Br2; and with excess.

Show answer

(E)-2,3-dibromobut-2-ene (anti); with excess, 2,2,3,3-tetrabromobutane.

Practice 122 L1 Alkynes

Ozonolysis

Products of reductive/oxidative ozonolysis of but-2-yne and of propyne.

Show answer

But-2-yne: 2 CH3COOH. Propyne: CH3COOH + CO2 (+ H2O).

Practice 123 L1 Alkynes

Oligomerisation

What forms when ethyne is passed through a red-hot iron tube? Through Ni(CN)2? With Cu2Cl2/NH4Cl?

Show answer

Benzene (3 C2H2); cyclooctatetraene (4 C2H2); vinylacetylene CH2=CH–C≡CH (2 C2H2).

Practice 124 L1 Alkynes

Mesitylene

Which reactions yield 1,3,5-trimethylbenzene: (a) 3 propyne, red-hot Fe tube (b) acetone, conc. H2SO4 (c) toluene + 2 CH3Cl/AlCl3?

Show answer

(a) and (b).

Practice 125 L1 Alkynes

Isomerisation

How is but-1-yne converted into but-2-yne and vice versa?

Show answer

But-1-yne → but-2-yne: alcoholic KOH, 175 °C. But-2-yne → but-1-yne: NaNH2 (via the terminal acetylide), then H2O.

Practice 126 L1 Alkynes

Bond data

State the hybridisation, C≡C length and bond angle in ethyne.

Show answer

sp; 120 pm; H–C≡C 180° (linear).

Practice 127 L1 Alkynes

Ethyne uses

Name the products when ethyne reacts with (a) HCl/HgCl2 (b) CH3COOH/Zn(OAc)2 (c) HCN/CuCl.

Show answer

(a) vinyl chloride (b) vinyl acetate (c) acrylonitrile.

Practice 128 L1 Alkynes

Alkyne from alkene

How can propene be converted to propyne?

Show answer

Br2/CCl4 gives 1,2-dibromopropane; 3 NaNH2 (liq. NH3) then H2O gives propyne.

Practice 129 L1 Dienes

Classification

Classify: (a) CH2=C=CH2 (b) CH2=CH–CH=CH2 (c) CH2=CH–CH2–CH=CH2 (d) CH3CH=C=CHCH3.

Show answer

(a) cumulated (b) conjugated (c) isolated (d) cumulated.

Practice 130 L1 Dienes

Heats of hydrogenation

The heat of hydrogenation of buta-1,3-diene is 239 kJ and of a molecule with two isolated C=C is about 254 kJ. Interpret.

Show answer

Conjugation stabilises the diene by about 15 kJ mol-1 (delocalisation energy).

Practice 131 L1 Dienes

Allene chirality

Which is chiral: (a) buta-1,2-diene (b) penta-2,3-diene (c) 3-methylbuta-1,2-diene?

Show answer

(b) only: each terminal carbon carries two different groups (H and CH3); (a) and (c) have two identical groups on one end.

Practice 132 L1 Dienes

HBr addition

Products of HBr + buta-1,3-diene at −80 °C and at 40 °C.

Show answer

−80 °C: 3-bromobut-1-ene (1,2-adduct, kinetic). 40 °C: 1-bromobut-2-ene (1,4-adduct, thermodynamic).

Practice 133 L1 Dienes

Why 1,4

Why does conjugated diene addition give a 1,4-product?

Show answer

The allylic cation intermediate has positive charge at C-2 and C-4; nucleophile attack at either end gives 1,2 or 1,4 products.

Practice 134 L1 Dienes

Diels–Alder basic

Give the product of buta-1,3-diene + ethene (hint: poor yield) and + maleic anhydride.

Show answer

Cyclohexene; cis-1,2,3,6-tetrahydrophthalic anhydride (cis-cyclohex-4-ene-1,2-dicarboxylic anhydride).

Practice 135 L1 Dienes

Diene requirements

State two conditions for a diene to undergo Diels–Alder.

Show answer

It must be conjugated and able to adopt the s-cis conformation; the dienophile should carry electron-withdrawing groups.

Practice 136 L1 Dienes

Which dienophile is best?

Rank by reactivity towards butadiene: ethene, acrolein, maleic anhydride.

Show answer

Maleic anhydride > acrolein > ethene.

Practice 137 L1 Dienes

Cyclopentadiene

Why does cyclopentadiene dimerise at room temperature but the dimer reverts on heating?

Show answer

It is a locked s-cis diene (very reactive) and also a good dienophile; the dimer is the Diels–Alder adduct, and at 170 °C the retro-Diels–Alder regenerates the monomer.

Practice 138 L1 Dienes

Polymers

Name the monomer(s) of (a) natural rubber (b) neoprene (c) Buna-S.

Show answer

(a) isoprene (cis-1,4) (b) chloroprene (c) buta-1,3-diene + styrene.

Practice 139 L1 Dienes

Vulcanisation

What happens when natural rubber is heated with sulphur?

Show answer

Sulphur forms cross-links between chains at the allylic positions/C=C, increasing strength and elasticity (vulcanisation).

Practice 140 L1 Dienes

Geometric isomers

How many stereoisomers does penta-1,3-diene have?

Show answer

2 (E and Z about C3=C4).

Practice 141 L1 Dienes

Preparation

How is buta-1,3-diene prepared from ethanol?

Show answer

Lebedev process: 2 C2H5OH → CH2=CH–CH=CH2 + 2 H2O + H2, ZnO/Al2O3, ≈ 700 K.

Practice 142 L1 Dienes

Isoprene HBr

Which carbon is protonated when isoprene reacts with HBr, and why?

Show answer

C-1 (the carbon of CH2= attached to C bearing CH3), giving the tertiary allylic cation.

Practice 143 L1 Dienes

Electrocyclic

State the stereochemical course of thermal ring-closure of (2E,4E)- and of (2E,4Z)-hexa-2,4-diene.

Show answer

Conrotatory (4π electrons): (2E,4E) gives trans-3,4-dimethylcyclobutene; (2E,4Z) gives the cis isomer.

Practice 144 L1 Mixed

Reagent matching

Match: (A) Lindlar (B) NBS (C) mCPBA (D) 9-BBN with (1) epoxide (2) allylic bromide (3) cis-alkene (4) aldehyde from terminal alkyne.

Show answer

A–3, B–2, C–1, D–4.

Practice 145 L1 Mixed

Anti-Markovnikov routes

List three ways to obtain 1-bromopropane from propene or related compounds.

Show answer

HBr/peroxide on propene; propene → hydroboration → propan-1-ol → PBr3 (or HBr); propan-1-ol + PBr3.

Practice 146 L1 Mixed

Compare hydration methods

For 3,3-dimethylbut-1-ene give the major alcohol with (a) dil. H2SO4 (b) Hg(OAc)2/H2O, NaBH4 (c) BH3, H2O2/OH−.

Show answer

(a) 2,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol (rearranged). (b) 3,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol. (c) 3,3-dimethylbutan-1-ol.
