13.3 Source notes

The module was written for this course. Names and structures were checked by a program (name-to-structure parsing, numbering and alphabetical checks, CIP labelling and isomer enumeration) as described in the editorial coverage report, which is kept separate from this chapter. Sources cross-checked. The following references were opened, and the sections listed were used to check the rules stated in the module.

  1. IUPAC Recommendations 2013, Nomenclature of Organic Chemistry (Favre and Powell, Royal Society of Chemistry, ISBN 978-0-85404-182-4): Chapter P-4, sections P-41 (seniority of classes), P-44.1 (seniority of parent structures) and P-44.1.2 (senior atom). Used for the 2013 comparisons in §1.2, §2.6, §5.1 and Part 10.
  2. Wade, Organic Chemistry, 9th edition: Chapter 3, section 3-3 (nomenclature of alkanes: the rules, the alkyl group table and the complex substituent method) and Appendix III to V (the priority table, substituent and functional group naming, aromatic nomenclature, the polycyclic and spiro rules). Used for §2.1–2.4, §4.1, §7.1–7.2 and §6.3.

Not used. No other book or paper is cited. Where a value or rule could not be checked against a source opened for this chapter, it is marked as a convention in the text (for example the 1979/1993 and 2013 difference) rather than presented as a fact from a source.
