11. Preparation of HCHO, CH3CHO and acetone: industrial routes

  • Methanal: oxidation of methanol over Ag or Cu at 900 K with limited air: 2CH3OH + O2 → 2HCHO + 2H2O (or dehydrogenation over Cu).
  • Ethanal: Wacker oxidation of ethene (above), hydration of ethyne with HgSO4, catalytic dehydrogenation of ethanol over Cu at 573 K.
  • Propanone: the cumene process gives phenol and propanone together: cumene (isopropylbenzene) + O2 → cumene hydroperoxide → (H+) phenol + propanone. Also the oxidation of propan-2-ol and the Wacker oxidation of propene.

Cumene process: the phenyl group migrates from carbon to oxygen
Key idea In the cumene process the key step is a Hock rearrangement: the hydroperoxide is protonated, water leaves with concerted migration of the phenyl group to oxygen, giving a carbocation that hydrolyses to phenol and acetone. Phenyl migrates in preference to methyl.
