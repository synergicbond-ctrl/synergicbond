77. Level 3, set C — matrix match

Practice 268 L3 Matrix 1

Match the reagent (Column I) with the product from benzenediazonium chloride (Column II). Column I: (P) KI; (Q) HBF4, then heat; (R) CuCN/KCN; (S) H3PO2. Column II: (1) benzene; (2) fluorobenzene; (3) iodobenzene; (4) benzonitrile; (5) phenol.

Show answer
  1. Iodide displaces N2 directly; Balz–Schiemann gives ArF; Sandmeyer cyanation gives ArCN; hypophosphorous acid reduces to Ar–H. Phenol (5) would arise from warm water, not listed.

P → 3; Q → 2; R → 4; S → 1.

Practice 269 L3 Matrix 2

Match the amine (Column I) with the result (Column II) of the test indicated. Column I: (P) (CH3)3N; (Q) C6H5NHCH3; (R) C6H5CH2NH2; (S) C6H5NH2. Column II: (1) N2 evolved with NaNO2/HCl at 273 K; (2) clear solution with PhSO2Cl/KOH, ppt. on acidification; (3) yellow oil with HNO2; (4) no reaction with PhSO2Cl/KOH; (5) stable diazonium salt.

Show answer
  1. (CH3)3N is 3° (no reaction with Hinsberg's reagent). N-Methylaniline is 2° (yellow N-nitrosamine). Benzylamine is a 1° aliphatic amine (N2; sulphonamide soluble in KOH). Aniline is a 1° aromatic amine (soluble sulphonamide; stable diazonium).

P → 4; Q → 3; R → 1, 2; S → 2, 5.

Practice 272 L3 Matrix 5

Match the coupling partner (Column I) with the pH range for azo coupling (Column II). Column I: (P) phenol; (Q) aniline; (R) β-naphthol; (S) N,N-dimethylaniline. Column II: (1) pH 4–5; (2) pH 9–10; (3) strongly acidic (< 2); (4) strongly alkaline (> 12).

Show answer
  1. Phenols and naphthols couple as phenoxide ions in weakly alkaline solution; amines couple as the free base in weakly acidic solution.

P → 2; Q → 1; R → 2; S → 1.

Practice 273 L3 Matrix 6

Match the nitro compound (Column I) with the observation (Column II). Column I: (P) CH3NO2; (Q) CH3CH2NO2; (R) (CH3)2CHNO2; (S) (CH3)3CNO2. Column II: (1) soluble in NaOH; (2) red colour with HNO2/NaOH; (3) blue colour with HNO2/NaOH; (4) no reaction with HNO2; (5) does not dissolve in NaOH.

Show answer
  1. Nitro compounds with an α-H form the nitronate and dissolve in alkali. 1° → red; 2° → blue. The 3° nitro compound lacks α-H.

P → 1, 2; Q → 1, 2; R → 1, 3; S → 4, 5.
