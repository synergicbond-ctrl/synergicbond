18.1 Oxygen

Oxygen is usually obtained by difference: %O = 100 − (%C + %H + %N + %S + %halogens + %P). Direct estimation (Aluise): the compound is pyrolysed in a stream of N2 over platinised carbon at ~1373 K, converting all oxygen to CO; the CO is passed over hot I2O5 giving CO2 (5CO + I2O5 → 5CO2 + I2), which is absorbed and weighed:

Formula %O = (16/44)(wCO2/w) × 100 (each CO2 measured corresponds to one O atom of the compound, from CO)
Exam Trap The oxygen by difference is unreliable if any other element has been missed or the percentages are inaccurate. In JEE problems "the compound contains C, H and O" can be used to infer O as the remainder.
