3.3 Grignard and organolithium reagents

A Grignard reagent RMgX behaves as a carbanion (R−); it adds to the C=O group, and the magnesium alkoxide is hydrolysed by dilute acid (aqueous NH4Cl is used for acid-sensitive tertiary alcohols). Prepared in dry ether or THF from RX and Mg turnings.

Key idea Grignard reagents are also very strong bases. They are destroyed by water, alcohols, carboxylic acids, terminal alkynes, amines and N–H/S–H compounds, releasing the alkane. A substrate that contains any acidic H consumes one extra equivalent of RMgX per acidic H. Counting equivalents is a repeated JEE Advanced question.
Example 7 L2 Main

Equivalents of Grignard reagent

How many moles of CH3MgBr are consumed per mole of HOCH2CH2COOC2H5, and what is the final product after work-up?

Show solution
  1. The free O–H consumes 1 equivalent (CH4 evolved): the alkoxide BrMgO–CH2CH2COOC2H5 forms.
  2. The ester then takes 2 equivalents (first gives a ketone, with loss of ethoxide; the ketone reacts with another equivalent).
  3. Total = 3 equivalents. After H3O+: HOCH2CH2C(CH3)2OH, 3-methylbutane-1,3-diol.
Example 8 L2 Main

Retrosynthesis of an alcohol

Suggest three different Grignard disconnections for 3-methylpentan-3-ol, (C2H5)2C(OH)CH3.

Show solution
  1. The carbinol carbon bears CH3, C2H5, C2H5. Each of the three C–C bonds to the carbinol carbon may be the bond made by the Grignard.
  2. (i) C2H5MgBr + CH3COC2H5 (butan-2-one): ethyl adds to the ketone.
  3. (ii) CH3MgBr + C2H5COC2H5 (pentan-3-one): methyl adds.
  4. (iii) 2 C2H5MgBr + CH3COOC2H5 (or CH3COCl): two ethyl groups from an ester of ethanoic acid.
