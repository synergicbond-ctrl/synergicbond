7.7 Oxygen

There is no simple direct qualitative test for oxygen. Its presence is concluded from functional-group tests, or it is calculated in elemental analysis as 100 − (sum of percentages of all other elements).

Concept check

A compound containing N and S gives blood red but no Prussian blue. What should be changed in the experiment to get separate N and S tests?

Show answer

Use excess sodium: NaSCN + 2Na → NaCN + Na2S.

Concept check

Why must the SFE be boiled with HNO3 before AgNO3 when N or S is present?

Show answer

To expel CN− and S2− as HCN and H2S; otherwise AgCN/Ag2S precipitate.
