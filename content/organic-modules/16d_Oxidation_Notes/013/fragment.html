Practice 1 L1 Foundation

What is the oxidation number of the carbonyl carbon in ethanal, CH3CHO?

(A) −1

(B) 0

(C) +1

(D) +2

Show answer
  1. Count bonds to oxygen: the C=O double bond counts as two, giving +2.
  2. Subtract bonds to hydrogen (one, −1); the bond to carbon counts zero. Total = +2 − 1 = +1.

Answer: C

Practice 2 L1 Foundation

Butan-1-ol is oxidised to butanal in good yield by pyridinium chlorochromate in anhydrous CH2Cl2, but to butanoic acid by aqueous acidic chromium(VI). The best explanation is that:

(A) PCC is thermodynamically unable to oxidise an aldehyde.

(B) without water no aldehyde hydrate forms, so no second chromate ester can be made from the aldehyde.

(C) the aldehyde is distilled out of the mixture as it forms.

(D) pyridine reduces the aldehyde back to the alcohol.

Show answer
  1. Further oxidation of an aldehyde by Cr(VI) goes through its hydrate RCH(OH)2, which has a carbinol hydrogen and forms a chromate ester.
  2. In anhydrous dichloromethane no hydrate forms, so the sequence stops at the aldehyde. The oxidising power of Cr(VI) itself is not the issue, and pyridine does not act as a reductant here.

Answer: B

(A) butan-1-ol

(B) butan-2-ol

(C) 2-methylpropan-2-ol

(D) 2-methylpropan-1-ol

Show answer

  2. 2-Methylpropan-2-ol is tertiary and has no such hydrogen; the other three are primary or secondary and are oxidised (to acid, ketone and acid respectively).

Answer: C

Which of the following reactions of but-2-ene give meso-butane-2,3-diol as the product?

(A) cis-but-2-ene, OsO4 (cat.), NMO

(B) trans-but-2-ene, OsO4 (cat.), NMO

Practice 38 L2 Main

Which statements about the Wacker oxidation of a terminal alkene RCH=CH2 are correct?
