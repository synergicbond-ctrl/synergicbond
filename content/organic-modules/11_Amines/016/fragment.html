16. The nitrous acid reactions (NaNO2 + HCl)

Nitrous acid is unstable; it is prepared in situ from NaNO2 and HCl (or H2SO4). It furnishes the nitrosating species NO+ (nitrosonium): HNO2 + H+ ⇌ H2O+–NO → H2O + NO+. The reaction with amines depends on the class and on whether the amine is aliphatic or aromatic.

Products of the nitrous-acid test

Mechanism of diazotisation (aromatic 1° amine).

  • Step 1: NO+ attacks the lone pair of ArNH2: ArNH2+–N=O.
  • Step 2: proton loss gives ArNH–N=O (N-nitrosamine).
  • Step 3: proton shifts (tautomerism): ArN=N–OH (diazohydroxide).
  • Step 4: protonation and loss of water: ArN≡N+ (aryldiazonium ion).

Diazotisation of aniline: nitrosamine → diazohydroxide → diazonium ion
  • Aliphatic diazonium ions lose N2 instantly (N2 is a superb leaving group) giving a carbocation, which is then captured by water/Cl− or loses H+. The product mixture, and rearrangements (1° R+ → 2°/3° R+), make this reaction synthetically useless but analytically useful (N2 gas, volumetric: Van Slyke amino-N determination).
  • Aryl diazonium ions are stabilised by delocalisation of the π electrons of the ring into the N≡N+ unit, so they survive at 273–278 K; above ~283 K they hydrolyse to phenol.

n-Propylamine gives a mixture of propan-1-ol, propan-2-ol and propene (via the unstable diazonium ion and a propyl cation that undergoes a 1,2-H shift)
Trap A frequent mistake: "all amines react with HNO2 to give N2". Only 1° aliphatic amines evolve N2 quantitatively. 1° aromatic amines give N2 only on warming (via phenol formation). 2° and 3° amines do not evolve N2.
Example 18 Main

Distinguish

How would you distinguish with a single reagent (HNO2) between ethylamine, diethylamine and triethylamine?

Show solution
  1. NaNO2/HCl: ethylamine → brisk N2 (and ethanol); diethylamine → yellow oily N-nitrosodiethylamine; triethylamine → clear solution of the nitrite salt (no gas, no oil).
Example 19 Adv

Integer

How many of the following give N2 gas on treatment with NaNO2/HCl at 273 K: propan-1-amine, propan-2-amine, N-methylethanamine, N,N-dimethylmethanamine, aniline, benzylamine, tert-butylamine?

Show solution
  1. N2 at 273 K is produced only by 1° aliphatic amines (unstable diazonium): propan-1-amine, propan-2-amine, benzylamine (the N is on an sp3 carbon), tert-butylamine. Aniline at 273 K gives a stable diazonium salt (no N2); 2° and 3° give no N2.
  2. Answer 4.
Example 20 Adv

Aromatic vs aliphatic

Compound A (C7H9N) gives a stable solution with NaNO2/HCl at 273 K that couples with β-naphthol to give a red dye; compound B (C7H9N) gives brisk effervescence with NaNO2/HCl at the same temperature. Both give a positive carbylamine test. Identify A and B.

Show solution
  1. Both are 1° (carbylamine). A gives a stable diazonium salt: an arylamine with the NH2 on the ring and a CH3 on the ring: o-, m- or p-toluidine (any one of the three fits the data).
  2. B gives N2 at 273 K: the N is on sp3 carbon: benzylamine, C6H5CH2NH2.
