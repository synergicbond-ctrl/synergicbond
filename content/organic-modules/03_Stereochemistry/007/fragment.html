4. Worked examples: isomer counting in the JEE style

Example 4 Main

C4H10O

How many isomers has C4H10O? How many are alcohols? How many are chiral?

1. 3° alcohol 2. 1° alcohol 3. 2° alcohol 4. 1° alcohol 5. ether
6. ether 7. ether
Show solution
  1. DBE = 0, so no rings or double bonds. Alcohols: butan-1-ol, butan-2-ol, 2-methylpropan-1-ol, 2-methylpropan-2-ol = 4. Ethers: methoxypropane, ethoxyethane, 2-methoxypropane = 3. Total 7.
  2. Chiral: only butan-2-ol (C-2 carries H, OH, CH3, C2H5). Counting stereoisomers gives 8 in all (7 − 1 + 2).
Example 5 Adv

C4H8O

How many isomers does C4H8O have if all constitutional isomers (including enols and rings) are counted? How many are carbonyl compounds?

Show solution
  1. The enumeration finds 26 constitutional isomers of C4H8O.
  2. Carbonyl compounds: butanal, 2-methylpropanal (aldehydes) and butan-2-one (ketone) = 3. Stable open-chain alcohol/ether/carbonyl isomers number fewer; the large total comes from enols, cyclic ethers and cyclic alcohols.
Example 6 Adv

Amines of C4H11N

How many amines (all classes) have the formula C4H11N? Classify them.

1. 1° amine 2. 1° amine 3. 1° amine 4. 1° amine 5. 2° amine
6. 3° amine 7. 2° amine 8. 2° amine
Show solution
  1. DBE = 0. Primary: butylamine, sec-butylamine, isobutylamine, tert-butylamine = 4. Secondary: N-methylpropylamine, N-methylisopropylamine, diethylamine = 3. Tertiary: N,N-dimethylethylamine = 1. Total 8.
Example 7 Integer

Isomeric carboxylic acids and esters

How many esters and how many carboxylic acids have the formula C4H8O2? Give the total of both.

Show solution
  1. Acids: butanoic acid and 2-methylpropanoic acid = 2.
  2. Esters: HCOOC3H7 (propyl, isopropyl: 2), CH3COOC2H5 (1), C2H5COOCH3 (1) = 4.
  3. Total 6.
Example 8 Adv

Chlorination: how many monochloro products?

Free-radical monochlorination of 2-methylbutane: how many structural isomers? how many stereoisomers in all?

1-chloro-2-methylbutane 2-chloro-2-methylbutane 2-chloro-3-methylbutane 1-chloro-3-methylbutane
The four structural monochlorides
Show solution
  1. 2-Methylbutane has four kinds of H (CH3 on C-1 ×2 equivalent methyls, C-2 H, CH2, CH3 on C-4): four structural isomers.
  2. 1-Chloro-2-methylbutane (stereocentre C-2) → 2 enantiomers; 2-chloro-3-methylbutane (stereocentre C-2) → 2 enantiomers; the other two are achiral. Total stereoisomers = 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6.
