8.9 Topic checkpoint

Concept Check

Assign E or Z to CH₃CH=C(Br)CH₃ drawn with the two methyl groups on the same side.

Show answer

The groups are Br > CH₃ on C2 and CH₃ > H on C3. With the two methyl groups on the same side, Br and the C-4 methyl are on opposite sides, so the descriptor is E.

Concept Check

A compound has the label (2R,3S) and an internal mirror plane. What is it called and is it optically active?

Show answer

Meso; it is optically inactive.

Concept Check

Which outranks which: –CH₂OH or –C₆H₅? –SH or –OH?

Show answer

–CH₂OH outranks –C₆H₅ (the set {O,H,H} beats {C,C,C}); –SH outranks –OH (S outranks O).

Think Further

How many stereoisomers does 1,3-dimethylcyclohexane have, and why do the formulas of §8.5 not apply directly?

Show solution

Three: cis (meso, 1R,3S) and trans (a pair of enantiomers, 1R,3R and 1S,3S). The ring is symmetrical about the axis through C-2 and C-5, so the two stereocentres are constitutionally identical, as in the symmetrical case of the table (n = 2), which gives 3.
