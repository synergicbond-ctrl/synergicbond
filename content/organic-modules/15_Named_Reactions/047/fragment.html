42. Rosenmund reduction and Stephen reaction (to aldehydes)

Rosenmund: RCOCl + H2 over Pd/BaSO4 (the catalyst is poisoned with quinoline or sulfur, "Rosenmund catalyst") → RCHO + HCl. Poisoning prevents further hydrogenolysis of RCHO to RCH2OH and then RCH3. Acid chlorides of aromatic and aliphatic acids; not the acid itself. Stephen: R–C≡N + SnCl2 + HCl (anhydrous ether) → imine hydrochloride–tin complex [R–CH=NH2]+[SnCl6]2−, then H3O+ → RCHO + NH4Cl. Best for aromatic nitriles; for aliphatic nitriles DIBAL-H at −78 °C (then H2O) is the modern alternative. The chain length is unchanged: RCN → RCHO.
