20.1 Nomenclature

  • Common names: name the two groups in alphabetical order followed by “ether”: ethyl methyl ether, diethyl ether, tert-butyl methyl ether (MTBE).
  • IUPAC: an alkoxyalkane: the smaller (shorter) alkyl plus oxygen is the alkoxy prefix on the longer chain: CH3OCH2CH3 = methoxyethane; (CH3)3COCH3 = 2-methoxy-2-methylpropane; C6H5OCH3 = methoxybenzene (anisole); C6H5OC2H5 = ethoxybenzene (phenetole).
  • If the groups are of equal size, the one with more substituents or the more complex becomes the parent. Cyclic ethers have heterocyclic names: oxirane (epoxide), oxetane, oxolane (tetrahydrofuran, THF), oxane (tetrahydropyran), 1,4-dioxane.
