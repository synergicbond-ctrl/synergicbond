I. Counting

Problem 131 [Application]

Find the degree of unsaturation of caffeine (C8H10N4O2).

Worked solution

    Answer: DBE = C + 1 + (N − H)/2 = 8 + 1 + (4 − 10)/2 = 6.

    Problem 132 [Application]

    Find the degree of unsaturation of nicotine (C10H14N2).

    Worked solution

      Answer: DBE = C + 1 + (N − H)/2 = 10 + 1 + (2 − 14)/2 = 5.
