6.3 Spiro compounds

Definition. In a spiro compound two rings share exactly one atom, the spiro atom. Name: spiro[x.y]alkane, numbers x ≤ y = the atoms in each ring other than the spiro atom, smaller first; the alkane name counts all atoms = x + y + 1.

  • Numbering: start in the smaller ring at the atom next to the spiro atom, number round that ring, through the spiro atom (which gets x + 1), and on round the second ring.

Compare & Distinguish Spiro[4.5]decane (rings of five and six members sharing one atom; 4 + 5 + 1 = 10 carbons) and bicyclo[4.4.0]decane (decalin, two six-membered rings sharing two adjacent atoms; 4 + 4 + 0 + 2 = 10 carbons) have the same number of carbons and the same formula C₁₀H₁₈ but are different compounds.
