42. Summary sheet: dienes

  • Conjugated diene: stabilised; allylic cation intermediate; 1,2 (kinetic, low T) vs 1,4 (thermodynamic, high T).
  • Diels–Alder: s-cis diene + EWG dienophile → cyclohexene; concerted, stereospecific (cis dienophile → cis); E,E-diene → outside groups cis; endo rule; ortho/para rule; reversible at high temperature.
  • Allenes: sp central carbon, perpendicular π bonds; chiral axis when each end is unsymmetrical.
  • Isolated dienes behave as two separate alkenes; conjugated and cumulated dienes are unusual.
