6.4 Fused polycyclic aromatics

Definition. Fused rings share a bond (two adjacent atoms). The common fused parents have retained names with a fixed numbering; the fusion atoms are labelled with the number of the preceding atom plus a letter (4a, 8a).

naphthalene anthracene quinoline
Fixed numbering of naphthalene, anthracene and quinoline
- **Naphthalene**: positions 1, 4, 5, 8 are **α** and 2, 3, 6, 7 are **β**. Electrophilic substitution occurs preferentially at α. Naphthalene has only two monosubstituted isomers, the 1- and the 2-. - **Anthracene**: the reactive middle ring is 9,10. Anthraquinone = anthracene-9,10-dione. - **Phenanthrene**: C₁₄H₁₀, angular; the 9,10 bond is the one that behaves like an alkene. - Hydrogenated forms: 1,2,3,4-tetrahydronaphthalene (tetralin) and decahydronaphthalene (decalin). @row naphthalene::c1ccc2ccccc2c1 ; 1-nitronaphthalene::O=[N+]([O-])c1cccc2ccccc12 ; naphthalen-2-ol::Oc1ccc2ccccc2c1 ; naphthalen-1-amine::Nc1cccc2ccccc12 ; anthracene::c1ccc2cc3ccccc3cc2c1 || Fused-ring compounds @row 1,2,3,4-tetrahydronaphthalene::C1CCc2ccccc2C1 ; anthracene-9,10-dione::O=C1c2ccccc2C(=O)c2ccccc12 ; phenanthrene::c1ccc2c(c1)ccc1ccccc12 || Hydro-ring and quinone names (RDKit-validated structures) ## 6.5 Heterocyclic compounds A heterocycle contains at least one atom other than carbon in the ring.
