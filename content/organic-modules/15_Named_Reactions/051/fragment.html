46. Kiliani–Fischer, Ruff and Wohl (changing the length of a sugar chain)

Kiliani–Fischer (ascent, +1 C): aldose + HCN → two epimeric cyanohydrins (new stereocentre at the old C=O carbon, so the C-2 epimers); hydrolysis (H2O) of CN to COOH, lactonisation; reduction (Na–Hg or H2/Pd–BaSO4) of the lactone to the aldose. Ruff degradation (descent, −1 C): aldose → (Br2/H2O) aldonic acid → (Fe3+, H2O2) loses the carboxyl carbon as CO2 and gives the aldose with one fewer carbon. Wohl degradation (descent, −1 C): aldose + NH2OH → oxime → acetylation/(CH3CO)2O, then base: loses HCN and gives the shorter aldose.

D-Arabinose → D-glucose (and D-mannose, the C-2 epimer; Kiliani–Fischer, +1 C)
1. Br₂, H₂O 2. Fe³⁺, H₂O₂⟶
D-Glucose → D-arabinose (Ruff, −1 C)
Key idea Epimers and chain length: a Kiliani–Fischer sequence on an aldose with n carbons and k stereocentres gives two aldoses with n + 1 carbons that differ only at C-2. Ruff or Wohl on a hexose gives the single pentose that has the same configuration at C-2 to C-5: D-glucose and D-mannose both give D-arabinose by Ruff degradation, which shows that they are C-2 epimers.
