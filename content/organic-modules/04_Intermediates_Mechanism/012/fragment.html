Ion pairs in SN1 reactions. The cation does not appear in free solution at once: ionisation gives a contact ion pair, then a solvent-separated ion pair, then free ions. Capture by solvent from the side opposite the departing group, within the contact ion pair, gives net inversion, and capture by free ions gives racemic product. When optical activity is lost faster than the leaving group is liberated as a free ion (the polarimetric rate exceeds the titrimetric rate), ion pairs are returning to covalent starting material (internal return). Neighbouring-group participation. A nearby lone pair or π bond (O−, S, N, Br, aryl, C=C) displaces the leaving group intramolecularly with inversion, and the bridged ion that forms is opened by the external nucleophile with a second inversion, so that the net result is retention of configuration and an enhanced rate.
