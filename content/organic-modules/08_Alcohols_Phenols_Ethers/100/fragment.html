29.2 Phenols

Practice 374 L1 PHE

Write the IUPAC name of the compound C6H4(OH)CH3 where groups are para.

Show answer
  1. 4-Methylphenol (p-cresol).
Practice 375 L1 PHE

Which of these is a phenol: benzyl alcohol, p-cresol, anisole, cyclohexanol?

Show answer
  1. p-Cresol: the OH is directly attached to an aromatic ring carbon.
Practice 376 L1 PHE

Why is the boiling point of o-nitrophenol lower than that of p-nitrophenol?

Show answer
  1. o-Nitrophenol has intramolecular hydrogen bonding (OH to NO2); p-nitrophenol has intermolecular H-bonds that associate molecules and raise bp/mp.
Practice 377 L1 PHE

Which isomer of nitrophenol is steam-volatile and why?

Show answer
  1. The ortho isomer: intramolecular H-bonding means no association with other molecules or with water.
Practice 378 L1 PHE

Give the industrial method for making phenol from benzene.

Show answer
  1. The cumene process: benzene + propene → cumene; cumene + O2 → cumene hydroperoxide; H3O+ → phenol + acetone.
Practice 379 L1 PHE

What is the by-product of the cumene process?

Show answer
  1. Acetone (propanone).
Practice 380 L1 PHE

Give the product when benzenediazonium chloride is warmed with water.

Show answer
  1. Phenol (with N2 and HCl).
Practice 381 L1 PHE

Which is a stronger acid, phenol or ethanol? Explain briefly.

Show answer
  1. Phenol: the phenoxide ion is stabilised by resonance (charge delocalised into the ring) and by the electronegative sp2 carbon.
Practice 382 L1 PHE

Why is phenol a weaker acid than acetic acid?

Show answer
  1. In acetate the charge is shared equally between two electronegative O atoms; in phenoxide the charge is shared with ring carbons (less electronegative, and aromaticity is disturbed in the resonance forms).
Practice 383 L1 PHE

Does phenol react with NaHCO3? With NaOH?

Show answer
  1. With NaHCO3: no (carbonic acid is stronger). With NaOH: yes, giving sodium phenoxide.
Practice 384 L1 PHE

Arrange in increasing acidity: phenol, p-nitrophenol, p-methylphenol.

Show answer
  1. p-Methylphenol < phenol < p-nitrophenol (+I decreases; −M, −I of NO2 increases acidity).
Practice 385 L1 PHE

What is picric acid? Give its pKa approximately.

Show answer
  1. 2,4,6-Trinitrophenol; pKa about 0.4 (a strong acid).
Practice 386 L1 PHE

What is the product of phenol with Br2/H2O? Give the colour of the product.

Show answer
  1. 2,4,6-Tribromophenol, a white precipitate.
Practice 387 L1 PHE

What is the product of phenol with Br2 in CS2 at 273 K?

Show answer
  1. Mainly 4-bromophenol with some 2-bromophenol.
Practice 388 L1 PHE

What does dilute HNO3 give with phenol at room temperature?

Show answer
  1. A mixture of 2-nitrophenol and 4-nitrophenol.
Practice 389 L1 PHE

How can the mixture of o- and p-nitrophenol be separated?

Show answer
  1. By steam distillation: o-nitrophenol distils over; p-nitrophenol stays behind.
Practice 390 L1 PHE

Write the product of phenol with concentrated HNO3 (after sulphonation).

Show answer
  1. 2,4,6-Trinitrophenol (picric acid).
Practice 391 L1 PHE

Write the product of Reimer–Tiemann reaction of phenol.

Show answer
  1. Salicylaldehyde (2-hydroxybenzaldehyde), using CHCl3/aq. NaOH, then H+.
Practice 392 L1 PHE

What is the reactive electrophile in the Reimer–Tiemann reaction?

Show answer
  1. Dichlorocarbene, :CCl2.
Practice 393 L1 PHE

What product is formed in Kolbe–Schmitt reaction?

Show answer
  1. Salicylic acid (2-hydroxybenzoic acid) from sodium phenoxide + CO2 (400 K, 4–7 atm), then H+.
Practice 394 L1 PHE

How is aspirin made from salicylic acid?

Show answer
  1. By acetylation of the phenolic OH with acetic anhydride (H+ catalyst) to give 2-acetoxybenzoic acid.
Practice 395 L1 PHE

Give the colour with neutral FeCl3 of (a) phenol; (b) ethanol.

Show answer
  1. (a) Violet (or blue-violet). (b) No colour change.
Practice 396 L1 PHE

What is the name of the reaction of an aryl ester with AlCl3 to give a hydroxy-aryl ketone?

Show answer
  1. Fries rearrangement.
Practice 397 L1 PHE

Write the product of heating allyl phenyl ether.

Show answer
  1. 2-Allylphenol by the Claisen rearrangement ([3,3]-sigmatropic).
Practice 398 L1 PHE

Write the product of phenol with zinc dust on heating.

Show answer
  1. Benzene (ZnO also forms).
Practice 399 L1 PHE

What is formed when phenol is hydrogenated over Ni at 450 K?

Show answer
  1. Cyclohexanol.
Practice 400 L1 PHE

What is the product of phenol with phthalic anhydride and conc. H2SO4? What is it used for?

Show answer
  1. Phenolphthalein, an acid–base indicator (colourless in acid, pink in base).
Practice 401 L1 PHE

Which polymer is made from phenol and formaldehyde?

Show answer
  1. Bakelite (phenol–formaldehyde resin), a thermosetting polymer.
Practice 402 L1 PHE

Give the product of phenol with NaOH and then CH3I.

Show answer
  1. Anisole (methoxybenzene) by Williamson synthesis.
Practice 403 L1 PHE

Why does phenol not give a substitution of OH by Cl with HCl?

Show answer
  1. The C–O bond has partial double-bond character (resonance) and the carbon is sp2; neither SN1 nor SN2 is possible.
Practice 404 L1 PHE

What happens when sodium phenoxide solution is treated with CO2?

Show answer
  1. Phenol is liberated (cloudy solution) because carbonic acid is stronger than phenol: C6H5ONa + CO2 + H2O → C6H5OH + NaHCO3.
Practice 405 L1 PHE

Name the dihydric phenols with OH groups at 1,2; 1,3 and 1,4 positions.

Show answer
  1. Catechol, resorcinol, hydroquinone respectively.
Practice 406 L1 PHE

What is the oxidation product of hydroquinone?

Show answer
  1. p-Benzoquinone.
Practice 407 L1 PHE

Name the compound formed from phenol and benzenediazonium chloride in alkaline medium. Give its colour.

Show answer
  1. p-Hydroxyazobenzene, an orange azo dye (azo coupling at the para position).
Practice 408 L1 PHE

How many monobromophenols are possible? Name them.

Show answer
  1. Three: 2-, 3- and 4-bromophenol.
Practice 409 L1 PHE

What are the isomers of C7H8O containing a benzene ring?

Show answer
  1. Five: o-, m- and p-cresol, benzyl alcohol and anisole.
