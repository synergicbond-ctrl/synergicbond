Practice 26 Product

CH3I and C2H5I are heated with sodium in dry ether. How many different alkanes form?

Show answer
  1. Couplings CH3–CH3, CH3–C2H5, C2H5–C2H5: three alkanes (ethane, propane, butane) in about 1 : 2 : 1.
Practice 27 Product

Sodium propanoate is electrolysed. Give the organic product and the gas at the cathode.

Show answer
  1. Anode: 2 CH3CH2COO− → CH3CH2CH2CH3 (butane) + 2 CO2. Cathode: H2.
Practice 28 Product

(CH3)2CuLi + CH3CH2CH2CH2Br → ?

Show answer
  1. Corey–House coupling: CH3CH2CH2CH2–CH3, pentane.
Practice 29 Product

CH3MgBr + HCHO, then H3O+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Ethanol, CH3CH2OH. Methanal always gives a primary alcohol with one more carbon than R.
Practice 30 Product

CH3MgBr, then CO2, then H3O+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Ethanoic acid, CH3COOH.
Practice 31 Product

CH3CH2MgBr + ethylene oxide, then H3O+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. The Grignard attacks the epoxide CH2: CH3CH2–CH2CH2OH = butan-1-ol (chain +2 carbons).
Practice 32 Product

Ethyl ethanoate + excess CH3MgBr, then H3O+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Two CH3 groups add: (CH3)3C–OH, 2-methylpropan-2-ol (the ethoxy group leaves as ethanol).
Practice 33 Product

CH3C≡N + CH3CH2MgBr, then H3O+. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Imine salt, then hydrolysis: CH3COCH2CH3, butanone.
Practice 34 Product

Benzene + 1-chloropropane/AlCl3. Which product predominates, and why?

Show answer
  1. Isopropylbenzene (cumene): the primary cation CH3CH2CH2+ rearranges by a 1,2-hydride shift to the more stable secondary (CH3)2CH+.
Practice 35 Product

CH3CHO is treated with dilute NaOH at 5 °C, then the product is heated. Give both products.

Show answer
  1. Cold: 3-hydroxybutanal (aldol). Heated: but-2-enal (crotonaldehyde, E), by loss of water.
Practice 36 Product

Benzaldehyde + propanone with dilute NaOH. Give the main product.

Show answer
  1. Claisen–Schmidt: PhCH=CH–CO–CH3, (E)-4-phenylbut-3-en-2-one (benzalacetone).
Practice 37 Product

Benzaldehyde + HCHO with concentrated NaOH, then H+. Give the products.

Show answer
  1. Crossed Cannizzaro: formaldehyde is oxidised: HCOOH (formate) and PhCH2OH (benzyl alcohol).
Practice 38 Product

Diethyl malonate + NaOEt + CH3CH2Br; then NaOH/H2O, H+, heat. Give the final acid.

Show answer
  1. Alkylation gives diethyl ethylmalonate; hydrolysis and decarboxylation: CH3CH2CH2COOH, butanoic acid.
Practice 39 Product

Ethyl acetoacetate + NaOEt + CH3I; then dilute NaOH, H+, heat. Give the final product.

Show answer
  1. Methylation at the active CH2 then ketonic hydrolysis and loss of CO2: CH3COCH2CH3, butanone.
Practice 40 Product

Cyclohexanone + Ph3P=CH2. Give the organic product.

Show answer
  1. Methylenecyclohexane, C6H10=CH2: the C=O oxygen is replaced by =CH2.
Practice 41 Product

Propanone + I2/NaOH. Give the organic products.

Show answer
  1. Haloform: CHI3 (yellow) and CH3COONa.
Practice 42 Product

Two molecules of propanone with Mg–Hg in benzene, then water. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Pinacol coupling: (CH3)2C(OH)–C(OH)(CH3)2, pinacol (2,3-dimethylbutane-2,3-diol).
Practice 43 Product

Toluene + CrO2Cl2 in CS2, then H3O+. Give the product and the name.

Show answer
  1. Benzaldehyde (Etard reaction). No over-oxidation to benzoic acid.
Practice 44 Product

Phenol + CHCl3/NaOH, then H+: give the major and minor products.

Show answer
  1. Major salicylaldehyde (o-hydroxybenzaldehyde); minor p-hydroxybenzaldehyde.
Practice 45 Product

Benzenediazonium chloride + sodium phenoxide (pH 9–10, 273 K). Give the product and its colour class.

Show answer
  1. 4-Hydroxyazobenzene (p-hydroxyazobenzene), an orange azo dye; coupling at the para position.
Practice 46 Product

Aniline → NaNO2/HCl (273 K) → CuCN. Give the final product.

Show answer
  1. Benzonitrile, C6H5CN (Sandmeyer cyanation).
Practice 47 Product

Benzenediazonium chloride + H3PO2. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Benzene (reductive deamination).
Practice 48 Product

Phthalimide, KOH, CH3CH2Br, then N2H4. Give the amine.

Show answer
  1. Ethylamine, CH3CH2NH2 (Gabriel synthesis; pure primary amine).
Practice 49 Product

Chlorobenzene + aqueous NaOH, 623 K, 300 atm, then H+. Give the product and the name of the process.

Show answer
  1. Phenol (Dow process, via benzyne).
Practice 50 Product

Cyclohexanone + mCPBA. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. ε-Caprolactone (oxepan-2-one), Baeyer–Villiger oxidation.
Practice 51 Product

Acetophenone + mCPBA. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Phenyl ethanoate: phenyl migrates in preference to methyl.
Practice 52 Product

Propanoic acid + Br2/red P, then H2O. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. 2-Bromopropanoic acid, CH3CHBrCOOH.
Practice 53 Product

Benzene + CO + HCl, AlCl3/CuCl, high pressure. Give the product and the name.

Show answer
  1. Benzaldehyde (Gattermann–Koch). The electrophile is the formyl cation HC≡O+.
Practice 54 Product

Hex-3-yne + H2/Lindlar's catalyst. Give the stereochemistry of the product.

Show answer
  1. cis (Z)-Hex-3-ene by syn addition of H2 on the poisoned Pd surface.
Practice 55 Product

Hex-3-yne + Na in liquid NH3. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. trans (E)-Hex-3-ene (anti addition by radical-anion/vinyl-radical path).
Practice 56 Product

Anisole + Li, liq. NH3, EtOH. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. 1-Methoxycyclohexa-1,4-diene: the electron-donating OCH3 stays on a double-bonded carbon (Birch).
Practice 57 Product

Benzoyl chloride + NH3 gives A; A + Br2/NaOH gives B. Name A and B.

Show answer
  1. A = benzamide; B = aniline (Hofmann; the carbonyl carbon is lost).
Practice 58 Product

Butanamide + Br2/KOH. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Propan-1-amine: one carbon fewer.
Practice 59 Product

Neopentyl alcohol, (CH3)3CCH2OH, + conc. HBr. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. The primary cation cannot form; a CH3 shift accompanies the loss of water, giving the tertiary cation; bromide captures it: 2-bromo-2-methylbutane.
Practice 60 Product

Pinacol + H2SO4, Δ. Give the product.

Show answer
  1. Pinacolone, (CH3)3C–CO–CH3 (3,3-dimethylbutan-2-one).
