Example 33 L2 Main

Multistep synthesis

Starting from propene, give reagents to prepare (a) propan-1-ol, (b) propan-2-ol, (c) propane-1,2-diol, (d) 1,2,3-trichloropropane, (e) 2-methylpropan-2-ol from propanone.

Show solution
  1. (a) 1. BH3·THF; 2. H2O2/NaOH (anti-Markovnikov hydration).
  2. (b) H3O+ (Markovnikov) or Hg(OAc)2/H2O then NaBH4.
  3. (c) Cold dilute alkaline KMnO4 or OsO4/NMO (syn dihydroxylation) giving a racemic diol; or mCPBA then H3O+.
  4. (d) Cl2, 773 K (allylic chlorination → allyl chloride), then Cl2 (anti addition to C=C) gives 1,2,3-trichloropropane.
  5. (e) CH3MgBr, then H3O+ on propanone.
Example 34 L3 Advanced

Identify the alcohol

An optically active alcohol A (C5H12O) is oxidised by CrO3 to a ketone B that gives a positive iodoform test. Dehydration of A with conc. H2SO4 gives an alkene C whose ozonolysis (Zn/H2O) gives ethanal and propanone. Identify A, B and C, and explain why A is not pentan-2-ol.

Show solution
  1. Ozonolysis gives CH3CHO + (CH3)2C=O, so C = 2-methylbut-2-ene, (CH3)2C=CHCH3.
  2. A is a secondary alcohol (it gives a ketone) that is chiral and has five carbons. Pentan-2-ol is chiral and gives a methyl ketone, but its dehydration gives pent-2-ene (ozonolysis: ethanal + propanal), not C. Dehydration of 3-methylbutan-2-ol, (CH3)2CH–CH(OH)CH3, goes through the 2° cation, which undergoes a hydride shift to the 3° cation (CH3)2C+CH2CH3; loss of H+ gives C as the Zaitsev product.
  3. B = 3-methylbutan-2-one, (CH3)2CH–CO–CH3 (a methyl ketone: iodoform positive ✓). A = 3-methylbutan-2-ol (one stereocentre: C-2 with H, OH, CH3, isopropyl ✓).
