7.1 Benzene as the parent

Definition. When no principal group is attached to the ring, benzene is the parent and the substituents are prefixes.

  • Monosubstituted: substituent + benzene, no locant: chlorobenzene, nitrobenzene, ethylbenzene.
  • Disubstituted: either locants (1,2- / 1,3- / 1,4-) or the older prefixes ortho (o-), meta (m-), para (p-). They mean the same: o = 1,2; m = 1,3; p = 1,4. Both are in use.
  • Three or more substituents: use numbers; give the lowest locant set (first point of difference); if tied, the substituent first in alphabetical order gets the lowest number.
