56. Summary sheet for Part I

  • β-Keto acids lose CO2 through a cyclic six-membered TS; bridgehead carboxyl groups resist (Bredt).
  • Acetoacetic ester synthesis: CH3CO–CH(R)(R′) after alkylation, ketonic hydrolysis and decarboxylation.
  • 18O: ketone O exchanges with H2O*; acetal hydrolysis puts water's O on the carbonyl; BV keeps the carbonyl O as the ester C=O.
  • Oxidation number of carbon: bonds to O/N/X count +1, to H −1, to C 0.
  • Aldol retrosynthesis: α–β bond disconnection → donor (enolisable) + acceptor (carbonyl); prefer a non-enolisable acceptor.
  • Enamine: ketone with α-H + secondary amine; primary amine → imine; tertiary amine → no reaction.
