Example 48 Advanced

Synthesis planning

Prepare (a) pent-2-yne from ethyne, (b) 2,2-dimethylhex-3-yne from ethyne (c) but-2-yne from propyne.

Show solution
  1. (a) HC≡CH + NaNH2 → HC≡C−; + CH3CH2Br → HC≡C–CH2CH3 (but-1-yne); NaNH2; + CH3I → CH3–C≡C–CH2CH3 (pent-2-yne). Two successive alkylations with 1° halides.
  2. (b) Disconnect at the CH2CH3 side: (CH3)3C–C≡C− + CH3CH2Br (a 1° halide, fine). But the alkyne (CH3)3C–C≡CH cannot be made from ethyne by alkylation, because t-BuX would eliminate under the strongly basic conditions; the other disconnection (t-Bu on the electrophile) fails for the same reason. Acetylide alkylation works only with methyl and 1° halides.
  3. (c) CH3C≡CH + NaNH2 → CH3C≡C−; + CH3I → CH3C≡CCH3.
